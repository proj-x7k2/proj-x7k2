# Eight-week postoperative tibial plateau leveling osteotomy radiographs do not change the prescribed management plan for most dogs.

**Journal of the American Veterinary Medical Association (2026)** · 전향적 연구, n=97 (291건 평가) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42762824/) · DOI: 10.2460/javma.26.06.0467
관련 주제: [[topics/musculoskeletal-imaging]]

## 국문 요약
단일 3차 진료기관에서 TPLO 8주차 정기 평가를 받은 개 97마리(291건 평가)를 대상으로, 신체검사(PE)와 방사선검사(RE) 결과가 처방된 관리계획을 얼마나 자주 변경시키는지, 그리고 전공의와 상급 임상의 간 평가가 얼마나 일치하는지를 3명의 평가자(상급 임상의 2명, 전공의 1명)가 독립적으로 평가한 전향적 interobserver agreement 연구다. PE와 RE에 따른 권고사항은 대부분(59.5%) 일치했으나, 평가에 따른 관리계획 변경은 상급 임상의(45.4%)가 전공의(30.5%)보다 더 흔했고, 전체·PE·RE 관리계획에 대한 interrater reliability는 각각 fair(κ=0.2233), fair(κ=0.2252), slight(κ=0.1965)로 낮았다. Patellar desmitis가 가장 흔한 방사선 소견(34.2%)이었지만 관리계획 변경으로 이어진 경우는 18.2%에 불과했다.

## English summary
In this prospective interobserver agreement study, 97 dogs (291 evaluations) presenting for routine 8-week postoperative TPLO evaluation at a single referral hospital were independently assessed by three evaluators (2 senior clinicians, 1 surgical resident) via physical examination (PE) and radiographic evaluation (RE). PE and RE recommendations were consistent in most cases (59.5%), but management plans differed more often for senior clinicians (45.4%) than residents (30.5%), with fair-to-slight interrater reliability overall (κ=0.2233), for PE (κ=0.2252), and for RE (κ=0.1965). Patellar desmitis was the most common radiographic finding (34.2%) but rarely changed the management plan (18.2%).

## 임상 시사점
8주차 TPLO 정기 방사선검사가 실제로 관리계획을 바꾸는 경우는 제한적이고 평가자 간 일치도도 낮으므로, 정기 방사선검사의 방사선 피폭·비용 대비 실질적 이득을 저자들처럼 신중히 저울질할 필요가 있다.

## 연구 설계와 한계
2024년 10월~2025년 7월 단일 3차 진료기관에서 시행된 전향적 interobserver agreement 연구로, PE·RE 각각에 대해 평가자별 독립 판단과 관리계획을 표준 양식에 기록했다. 단일 기관·소수 평가자(3명) 설계로 일반화에 제한이 있고, 낮은 κ값 자체가 방사선 소견의 임상적 가치보다 평가자 간 관리계획 결정 기준의 다양성을 반영할 수 있다는 점에 유의해야 한다.

## 원문 초록
OBJECTIVE: To determine how frequently routine 8-week tibial plateau leveling osteotomy (TPLO) radiographs resulted in a change to the prescribed postoperative management plan when evaluated by surgery residents versus senior clinicians, and how often the resident and senior clinician plans differed.

METHODS: In this prospective interobserver agreement study, dogs that presented for a routine 8-week TPLO evaluation at a single veterinary referral hospital between October 2024 and July 2025 were eligible for enrollment. Three evaluators (2 senior clinicians, 1 surgical resident) independently performed physical examinations (PEs) and later reviewed each dog's radiographic evaluation (RE). After each PE and RE, each evaluator documented their findings and management decision on standard forms. A Cohen κ score was used to evaluate interrater reliability for PE and RE management choice.

RESULTS: 97 dogs (291 evaluations) were enrolled. Corresponding PE and RE recommendations were consistent in most cases (173 [59.5%]). The elected case management plans after each evaluation differed more often for senior clinicians (45.4%) than residents (30.5%). Interrater reliability for overall, PE, and RE prescribed management plans were fair (κ = 0.2233), fair (κ = 0.2252), and slight (κ = 0.1965), respectively. Patellar desmitis (99 [34.2%]) was the most common radiographic finding, but rarely resulted in a different management plan (18 [18.2%]).

CONCLUSIONS: Management plans based on PE and RE evaluations were inconsistent, with REs resulting in prolonged exercise restriction in up to 40.5% of dogs.

CLINICAL RELEVANCE: Clinicians should weigh the costs and risks associated with routine radiographs against their anticipated protocol changes until practical guidance about the clinical impact of common radiographic findings is available.
