# Standardized Minimum Number of Individuals (MNI) Enumeration and Veterinary Forensic Assessment in a Large-Scale Animal Starvation Scene: A Technical Note.

**Animals : an open access journal from MDPI (2026)** · 증례군, n=22 (전체 현장 1256마리 중 상세 감정 대상; 사체 16 + 두개골 6) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42738595/) · DOI: 10.1177/0300985815575049
관련 주제: [[topics/trauma-forensic-imaging]]

## 국문 요약
2023년 3월 한국 경기도 양평군의 한 개인 주택에서 개·고양이 1256마리가 장기간 사료·물 결핍 상태로 폐사한 대규모 동물 방임(starvation) 현장이 발견되었다. 전체 사체를 일일이 세는 것이 불가능해, 온전한(중복되지 않은) 사체 직접 계수와 분리된 두개골 계수를 결합한 표준화된 skull-based minimum number of individuals(MNI) 접근법으로 현장 전체 개체수를 산정했다. 경찰이 제출한 사체 16구와 현장에서 수거한 분리 두개골 6개, 총 22개체에 대해 postmortem radiography를 포함한 상세 법수의학 감정(전신 방사선, 전체 부검, 독성 검사)을 시행했다. 사체 16구 중 body condition score 평가가 가능했던 5구는 모두 9점 척도에서 1~3점으로 중증~극심한 마름을 보였고, 독성 검사는 16구 전례 음성이었다. 분리 두개골 6개의 육안·방사선 검사에서 3개는 외상성 골절과 scavenging에 의한 천공, 2개는 품종 특유의 정상 개방된 fontanelle, 1개는 봉합선 관련 불명확 병변이 확인되었다.

## English summary
In March 2023, the remains of 1256 dogs and cats were recovered from a private residence in Yangpyeong-gun, Gyeonggi-do, Republic of Korea, following prolonged deprivation of food and water. Because whole-carcass enumeration was infeasible, a standardized skull-based minimum number of individuals (MNI) approach combined direct counts of intact, non-duplicated carcasses with counts of isolated skulls to derive a scene-wide count. A representative subset of 22 specimens (16 carcasses submitted by police, 6 isolated skulls) underwent detailed forensic examination including postmortem radiography, full necropsy, and toxicological screening. Body condition scoring was possible in 5 of 16 carcasses, all scoring 1-3 on a 9-point scale (severe to extreme emaciation); toxicological screening was negative in all 16. Gross and radiographic examination of the 6 isolated skulls identified traumatic fractures and scavenging punctures in 3, breed-typical open fontanels without trauma in 2, and an indeterminate suture-associated lesion in 1.

## 임상 시사점
대규모 동물 방임·학대 현장에서 전신 사체 계수가 불가능할 때, postmortem radiography를 포함한 skull-based MNI 접근법이 실무적으로 재현 가능한 개체수 산정·증거 문서화 방법이 될 수 있으며, 분리 두개골의 방사선 소견(외상 골절 vs 품종 특유 정상 fontanelle)을 감별하는 데 방사선검사가 핵심적 역할을 한다.

## 연구 설계와 한계
대규모 동물 방임 현장 1건에 대한 기술적 증례군(technical note)으로, 전체 1256마리 중 22개체(사체 16, 두개골 6)만 상세 법수의학 감정(방사선 포함)을 받아 대표성에 한계가 있다. Body condition score는 16구 중 5구에서만 평가 가능했고, 초록에는 구체적 방사선 소견의 빈도나 판독 기준이 상세히 제시되지 않았다. 단일 현장·단일 기관 사례이므로 다른 대규모 방임 현장에 그대로 일반화하기는 어렵다.

## 원문 초록
Mass companion animal mortality scenes pose major veterinary forensic and animal welfare challenges, particularly when remains are commingled, variably decomposed, or skeletonized. In March 2023, the remains of 1256 dogs and cats were recovered from a private residence in Yangpyeong-gun, Gyeonggi-do, Republic of Korea, after prolonged deprivation of food and water. Because whole-carcass enumeration was not feasible across the entire site, a standardized skull-based minimum number of individuals (MNI) approach was used to derive the scene-wide count by combining direct counts of intact, non-duplicated carcasses with counts of isolated skulls from disarticulated remains. A representative subset of 22 specimens, consisting of 16 carcasses submitted by the investigating police and 6 isolated skulls collected during the scene investigation, underwent detailed forensic examination including postmortem radiography, full necropsy, and toxicological screening. Body condition scoring was possible in 5 of the 16 carcasses, and all five scored 1 to 3 on a 9-point scale, consistent with severe to extreme emaciation. Toxicological screening of all 16 carcasses was negative for all tested substance classes. Gross and Radiographical examination of the 6 isolated skulls identified external traumatic fractures and scavenging punctures in 3 specimens, breed-typical persistent open fontanels without evidence of trauma in 2, and an indeterminate suture-associated lesion in 1. This report proposes a practical and reproducible veterinary forensic workflow for large-scale companion animal mortality scenes in which extensive decomposition prevents reliable direct enumeration. The combined use of skull-based MNI estimation, structured necropsy, whole-body postmortem radiography, toxicological exclusion testing, and standardized skull morphology assessment supports evidentiary documentation and courtroom prosecution in mass animal cruelty investigations.
