# Advanced heartworm disease in dogs is associated with marked right heart and pulmonary artery remodeling.

**American Journal of Veterinary Research (2026)** · 후향적 연구, n=44 · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42987508/) · DOI: 10.2460/ajvr.26.06.0240
관련 주제: [[topics/cardiac-imaging]]

## 국문 요약
2020년 7월-2023년 2월 진단된 heartworm 감염견 44마리의 의무기록을 후향적으로 검토해, 임상 class(1-2 mild vs 3-4 advanced), pulmonary hypertension(PH) 확률, tricuspid regurgitation(TR) pressure gradient별로 echocardiography 지표를 비교했다. Advanced군(n=21)은 mild군(n=23)보다 right atrium·right ventricle 확대가 크고 right pulmonary artery distensibility가 낮았으며, pulmonary artery acceleration time이 짧고 main pulmonary artery-to-aorta ratio가 높았고 right ventricular systolic function이 낮았다. Normalized right atrial diameter가 advanced 질환 식별에서 가장 좋은 성능(AUC 0.95; 95% CI 0.88-1.00)을 보였고, 8마리에서 heartworm extraction 후 right atrial size가 유의하게 감소했다.

## English summary
In 44 client-owned dogs with heartworm infection, advanced disease (class 3-4, n=21) showed greater right atrial and ventricular enlargement, reduced right pulmonary artery distensibility, shorter pulmonary artery acceleration time, higher main PA:Ao ratio and lower RV systolic function than mild disease (n=23). Similar findings applied to dogs with high PH probability. Normalized right atrial diameter best identified advanced disease (AUC 0.95), and right atrial size decreased after heartworm extraction in 8 dogs.

## 임상 시사점
Heartworm 감염견의 심초음파에서는 normalized right atrial diameter, TR gradient, RV 크기와 PA acceleration time을 함께 기록하면 중증도와 PH 가능성을 가늠하는 데 도움이 된다. Caval syndrome 개체에서는 extraction 후 right atrial size 변화를 재검해 반응을 확인할 수 있다 (소규모 후향적 근거).

## 연구 설계와 한계
단일 기관 후향적 의무기록 검토(n=44)로 class 3-4를 하나로 묶어 비교했고, extraction 전후 비교는 8마리에 한정된다. 영상 측정의 관찰자 간 재현성과 cut-off 값은 초록에 제시되지 않았으며 외부 검증이 없다.

## 원문 초록
OBJECTIVE: To characterize echocardiographic findings of right heart and pulmonary artery remodeling in dogs with heartworm disease.

METHODS: Medical records were searched for those of client-owned dogs with heartworm infection diagnosed from July 2020 through February 2023 and with no major systemic diseases not attributable to heartworm infection. Results for variables of interest were compared for dogs grouped by heartworm clinical class (classes 1 and 2 [mild] vs classes 3 and 4 [advanced]), pulmonary hypertension probability (low and intermediate vs high), and tricuspid regurgitation pressure gradient (mild, moderate, or severe). Pre- and postextraction variables were also compared for dogs with caval syndrome.

RESULTS: 44 dogs were included. Compared with dogs with mild heartworm disease (n = 23), dogs with advanced heartworm disease (21) had greater right atrial and right ventricular enlargement, less right pulmonary artery distensibility, shorter pulmonary artery acceleration time, higher main pulmonary artery-to-aorta ratio, and lower right ventricular systolic function. Similar results were identified for dogs with high (vs intermediate or low) pulmonary hypertension probability. Normalized right atrial diameter had the highest discriminatory performance for identifying advanced heartworm disease (area under the curve, 0.95; 95% CI, 0.88 to 1.00), followed by tricuspid regurgitation pressure gradient, normalized right ventricular dimension, and other variables. Right atrial size significantly decreased after heartworm extraction (n = 8 dogs).

CONCLUSIONS: Advanced heartworm disease is associated with marked right heart and pulmonary artery remodeling.

CLINICAL RELEVANCE: Echocardiographic assessment of right heart remodeling may assist clinicians in evaluating heartworm disease severity and identifying dogs with clinically important cardiopulmonary involvement.
