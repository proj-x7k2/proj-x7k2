# Computed tomography perfusion parameters may aid preoperative differentiation of canine pheochromocytomas from adrenocortical tumors.

**American journal of veterinary research (2026)** · 후향적 연구, n=25 · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42815534/) · DOI: 10.2460/ajvr.26.06.0280
관련 주제: [[topics/endocrine-imaging]]

## 국문 요약
건강한 Beagle 5마리(대조군)와, triple-phase CT·CT perfusion(CTP)·adrenalectomy·조직병리를 거친 client-owned 개 20마리(종양 22개: adrenocortical adenoma 8, adrenocortical carcinoma 7, pheochromocytoma 7)를 대상으로 CTP에서 유도한 time to highest observed adrenal tissue enhancement(T-peak)와 arterial blood flow(AF)를 비교한 후향적 연구다. T-peak는 두 adrenocortical tumor군 모두에서 pheochromocytoma보다 길었고, 36.0초 미만 cutoff가 100% 민감도·특이도를 보였다(사후 분석). AF는 두 adrenocortical tumor군에서 대조군보다 낮았고 pheochromocytoma에서는 adrenocortical carcinoma보다 높았으며, AUC 0.923·100.60 mL/min/100mL 초과 cutoff에서 85.7% 민감도·100% 특이도를 보였다. 편측 종양 17마리에서는 tumor-to-contralateral AF ratio가 pheochromocytoma와 adrenocortical tumor를 완전히 분리했다.

## English summary
This retrospective study compared CT perfusion (CTP)-derived time to highest observed adrenal tissue enhancement (T-peak) and arterial blood flow (AF) in 5 healthy control Beagles and 20 client-owned dogs with 22 adrenal tumors (8 adrenocortical adenomas, 7 adrenocortical carcinomas, 7 pheochromocytomas) who underwent triple-phase CT, CTP, adrenalectomy, and histopathology. T-peak was longer in both adrenocortical tumor groups than in pheochromocytomas, with a post hoc cutoff < 36.0 seconds yielding apparent 100% sensitivity and specificity. AF was lower in both adrenocortical tumor groups than in controls and higher in pheochromocytomas than in adrenocortical carcinomas (AUC 0.923; cutoff > 100.60 mL/min/100 mL yielding 85.7% sensitivity, 100% specificity). Among 17 dogs with unilateral tumors, the tumor-to-contralateral AF ratio completely separated pheochromocytomas from adrenocortical tumors.

## 임상 시사점
부신 종양의 수술 전 감별이 필요한 개에서 기존 triple-phase CT 소견에 CT perfusion 지표(T-peak, AF, 특히 양측 비교가 가능한 tumor-to-contralateral AF ratio)를 추가하면 pheochromocytoma와 adrenocortical tumor를 구분하는 데 도움이 될 수 있으나, 제시된 cutoff는 소규모 단일기관 자료에서 나온 것이므로 그대로 진단 기준으로 쓰기보다는 보조 정보로 활용하는 것이 안전하다.

## 연구 설계와 한계
건강한 Beagle 5마리의 과거 CTP 자료를 대조군으로 쓰고, 2024년 11월~2026년 5월 사이 CT·수술·조직병리를 모두 거친 client-owned 개 20마리(종양 22개)를 평가한 단일기관 후향적 연구다. 조직학적 확진을 기준으로 했다는 강점이 있으나, 각 종양 유형이 7~8예로 매우 적고 대조군이 역사적 자료라는 한계가 있어, 저자들도 제시된 cutoff가 더 큰 독립 코호트에서 검증이 필요하다고 명시했다.

## 원문 초록
OBJECTIVE: To characterize CT perfusion (CTP)-derived time to highest observed adrenal tissue enhancement (T-peak) and arterial blood flow (AF), evaluate associations with triple-phase CT findings, and assess the diagnostic performance of T-peak, AF, and the tumor-to-contralateral adrenal AF ratio for differentiating pheochromocytomas from adrenocortical tumors.

METHODS: Historical CTP data from 5 healthy Beagles served as controls. Client-owned dogs evaluated from November 21, 2024, through May 31, 2026, underwent triple-phase CT, CTP, adrenalectomy, and histopathology. Time to highest observed adrenal tissue enhancement was the interval from contrast administration to the greatest sampled adrenal attenuation; AF was calculated with a single-input maximum-slope model.

RESULTS: 20 dogs with 22 tumors (8 adrenocortical adenomas, 7 adrenocortical carcinomas, and 7 pheochromocytomas [PHEOs]) were included. Time to highest observed adrenal tissue enhancement was longer in both adrenocortical tumor groups than in PHEOs; a post hoc cutoff < 36.0 seconds yielded apparent 100% sensitivity and specificity. Arterial blood flow was lower in both adrenocortical tumor groups than in controls and higher in PHEOs than in adrenocortical carcinomas. Arterial blood flow had an area under the curve of 0.923; a cutoff > 100.60 mL/min/100 mL yielded apparent 85.7% sensitivity and 100% specificity. Among 17 dogs with unilateral tumors, the tumor-to-contralateral AF ratio completely separated PHEOs from adrenocortical tumors. Arterial blood flow correlated positively with CT wash-in and washout indices, whereas T-peak correlated negatively with arterial- and venous-phase percentage enhancement washout.

CONCLUSIONS: Time to highest observed adrenal tissue enhancement and AF differed among adrenal tumor types and were associated with CT enhancement characteristics.

CLINICAL RELEVANCE: Computed tomography perfusion may provide adjunctive information for preoperative adrenal tumor characterization, but the proposed thresholds require validation in larger independent cohorts.
