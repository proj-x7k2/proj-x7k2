# The use of contrast-enhanced echocardiography in the evaluation of feline left ventricular wall thickness.

**Journal of veterinary cardiology (2026)** · 전향적 연구, n=50 · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42914951/) · DOI: 10.1016/j.jvc.2026.09.004
관련 주제: [[topics/cardiac-imaging]]

## 국문 요약
Client-owned 고양이 50마리를 전향적으로 모집해 standard echocardiography(S-Echo)와 정맥 조영제 투여 후 contrast-enhanced echocardiography(C-Echo)로 left ventricular wall thickness(LVWT)를 측정하고 두 방법을 Bland-Altman 및 Passing-Bablok regression으로 비교했다. 모든 segment에서 두 방법의 측정값이 유의하게 달랐고(중앙값 차이 0.56mm, 범위 -1.4~4.9mm; P<0.0001), proportional bias(slope 1.21)가 있었다. Intra-/inter-operator reproducibility는 두 방법 모두 excellent(ICC 0.90-0.94 S-Echo, 0.86-0.99 C-Echo)였으나 repeatability coefficient는 C-Echo(0.28-0.61mm)가 S-Echo(0.89-1.03mm)보다 낮았고 coefficient of variation도 C-Echo가 낮았다.

## English summary
Fifty client-owned cats were prospectively enrolled and underwent standard (S-Echo) and intravenous contrast-enhanced echocardiography (C-Echo) for LVWT measurement. The methods differed significantly in all segments (median difference 0.56 mm; P<0.0001) with proportional bias (slope 1.21). Reproducibility was excellent for both (ICC 0.90–0.94 S-Echo; 0.86–0.99 C-Echo), but repeatability coefficients (0.28–0.61 mm vs 0.89–1.03 mm) and coefficients of variation (intra-observer 5.86% vs 7.43%; inter-observer 3.03% vs 7.64%) were lower with C-Echo. Accuracy against a reference standard was not assessed.

## 임상 시사점
C-Echo는 endocardial border가 불분명하거나 papillary muscle·false tendon 때문에 LVWT 측정이 애매한 고양이에서 측정 반복성을 높일 수 있다. 다만 C-Echo 측정값이 S-Echo보다 작게 나오는 경향이 있어 HCM cut-off를 그대로 적용할 수 없고, 같은 개체를 추적할 때는 같은 방법으로 일관되게 측정해야 한다.

## 연구 설계와 한계
50마리 전향적 방법 비교 연구로 방법 간 일치도와 관찰자 내·간 재현성을 평가했다. 저자들이 밝힌 대로 S-Echo와 C-Echo 모두 reference standard 대비 accuracy를 평가하지 않아 C-Echo의 측정값이 더 "정확"한지는 알 수 없다. 초록에는 HCM 진단 분류에 미치는 영향이나 조영제 이상반응이 제시되지 않았다.

## 원문 초록
INTRODUCTION/OBJECTIVES: Accurate measurement of feline left ventricular wall thickness (LVWT) is challenging due to endocardial and myocardial irregularities, false tendons, and prominent papillary muscles. In human medicine, contrast-enhanced echocardiography (C-Echo) is used to improve left ventricular wall delineation and is more accurate, repeatable, and reliable than standard echocardiography (S-Echo). Our objective was to compare C-Echo and S-Echo measurements of feline LVWT and determine repeatability, reproducibility, and reliability of each modality.

ANIMALS, MATERIALS AND METHODS: Fifty client-owned cats were prospectively enrolled in the study. Cats underwent S-Echo and C-Echo after intravenous injection of contrast agent. Methods were compared using Bland-Altman and Passing-Bablok regression. Interoperator and intra-operator reproducibility, repeatability, and reliability were quantified using intraclass correlation coefficients, repeatability coefficients, and coefficients of variation.

RESULTS: Standard echocardiography and C-Echo differed significantly for all segments with a median difference of 0.56 mm (range: -1.4 to 4.9 mm; P<0.0001). Regression showed proportional bias (slope: 1.21, confidence interval: 1.11-1.31) with minimal systematic bias (intercept: -0.26, confidence interval: -0.64 to 0.12). Intra-operator and interoperator reproducibility was excellent for both modalities (intraclass correlation coefficient: 0.90-0.94 for S-Echo; 0.86-0.99 for C-Echo), while repeatability coefficients were lower for C-Echo (0.28-0.61 mm) than for S-Echo (0.89-1.03 mm), and coefficients of variation were lower for C-Echo (intra-observer: 5.86%, inter-observer: 3.03%) than for S-Echo (7.43% and 7.64%).

STUDY LIMITATIONS: Accuracy of S-Echo and C-Echo compared with a reference standard was not assessed.

CONCLUSIONS: Contrast-enhanced echocardiography is feasible and provides excellent left ventricular wall visualization with lower LVWT measurements and superior repeatability, reproducibility, and reliability.
