# Two-dimensional shear wave elastography assessment of testicular stiffness in healthy cats.

**Theriogenology (2026)** · 전향적 연구, n=19 · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42772030/) · DOI: 10.1016/j.theriogenology.2026.118206
관련 주제: [[topics/urogenital-imaging]]

## 국문 요약
건강한 고양이의 정상 고환 강성(stiffness) 기준값이 아직 확립되지 않은 가운데, 선택적 양측 orchiectomy를 위해 내원한 중성화하지 않은 수컷 고양이 19마리를 대상으로 수술 전 B-mode·Doppler·2D shear wave elastography(2D-SWE) 검사를 시행했다. 종축 스캔에서 testicular mediastinum 상하에 각각 2개씩의 원형 region of interest(ROI)를 두고 전체 고환을 포괄하는 ROI도 함께 측정해 shear wave speed(SWS)를 비교했다. 조직병리로 모든 예에서 정상 고환 조직이 확인되었으며, 평균 SWS는 우측 2.45±0.62m/s, 좌측 2.73±0.51m/s로 좌우 차이가 없었고(p=0.097), submediastinal(2.51±0.55m/s)과 supramediastinal(2.66±0.42m/s) 영역 간에도 유의한 차이가 없었다(p=0.129). 전체·submediastinal·supramediastinal ROI 방식 간에도 유의한 차이가 없었다(p=0.080). 국소 ROI와 확장 ROI 간 탐색적 일치도 분석에서는 평균 편향이 작았으나 상호 대체 가능성까지 입증하지는 못했다.

## English summary
Reference values for testicular stiffness in healthy cats have not been well established. This study characterized testicular stiffness using two-dimensional shear wave elastography (2D-SWE) in 19 client-owned intact male cats presented for elective bilateral orchiectomy, using B-mode, Doppler, and 2D-SWE with a linear transducer before surgery. In longitudinal scans, two circular regions of interest (ROIs) were placed above and two below the testicular mediastinum, along with a whole-testis ROI, to compare mean shear wave speed (SWS). Histopathology confirmed normal testicular tissue in all cases. Mean SWS was 2.45 ± 0.62 m/s in the right testis and 2.73 ± 0.51 m/s in the left, with no significant side difference (p = 0.097), and no significant difference between submediastinal (2.51 ± 0.55 m/s) and supramediastinal (2.66 ± 0.42 m/s) regions (p = 0.129) or among whole-testis, submediastinal, and supramediastinal ROI approaches (p = 0.080). Exploratory agreement analysis between focal and extended ROI approaches showed small mean biases but did not establish interchangeability.

## 임상 시사점
고양이 고환 2D-SWE에서 좌우, mediastinum 상하, ROI 크기(국소 vs 전체 고환)에 따른 강성 차이가 뚜렷하지 않으므로, 정상 대조값으로는 어느 위치에서 측정하든 큰 편차 없이 사용할 수 있을 가능성을 시사한다. 다만 이 연구가 제시한 SWS 값(2.4~2.7m/s대)은 정상 소견 판단의 출발점일 뿐이며, 고환 질환 감별을 위한 cut-off로 쓰기 위해서는 질환군과의 비교 연구가 필요하다.

## 연구 설계와 한계
건강한 수컷 고양이 19마리(38개 고환)를 대상으로 orchiectomy 전 시행한 전향적 연구로, 조직병리로 정상 고환임을 확인한 뒤 여러 ROI 전략 간 SWS를 비교했다. 정상 고양이만을 대상으로 했으므로 질환 고환과의 감별 진단 성능은 평가되지 않았고, 재현성(관찰자 간·기기 간) 평가와 임상적 참고구간 확립은 향후 연구 과제로 남겨졌다.

## 원문 초록
Two-dimensional shear wave elastography (2D-SWE) provides quantitative information on tissue stiffness over a broad field of view, but physiological values for feline testes remain poorly defined. This study aimed to characterize testicular stiffness in clinically healthy cats and to compare different regions of interest (ROIs), and explore agreement between focal and extended sampling strategies. Nineteen client-owned intact male cats presented for elective bilateral orchiectomy were included after clinical, ultrasonographic, and histopathological screening. Before surgery, both testes underwent B-mode, Doppler, and 2D-SWE examination using a Mindray DC-80A ultrasound system with a linear transducer. In longitudinal scans, two circular ROIs were placed above and two below the testicular mediastinum, and an additional ROI encompassed the whole testis. Mean shear wave speed (SWS) values were calculated for each sampling strategy. Histopathology confirmed normal testicular tissue in all cases. Mean SWS was 2.45 ± 0.62 m/s in the right testis and 2.73 ± 0.51 m/s in the left, with no significant difference between sides (p = 0.097). Mean values were 2.51 ± 0.55 m/s in submediastinal regions and 2.66 ± 0.42 m/s in supramediastinal regions, without significant differences (p = 0.129). Whole-testis, submediastinal, and supramediastinal ROIs yielded mean SWS values of 2.62, 2.51, and 2.66 m/s, respectively, with no significant differences among approaches (p = 0.080). Exploratory agreement analyses showed small mean biases between the extended and focal ROI approaches, but did not establish interchangeability. These findings support the feasibility of 2D-SWE for assessing feline testicular stiffness and suggest a relatively homogeneous stiffness distribution in healthy feline testes. Further studies are needed to assess repeatability, establish reference intervals, and investigate diagnostic performance for feline testicular disease in clinical practice.
