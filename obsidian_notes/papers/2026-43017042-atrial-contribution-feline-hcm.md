# Beyond the ventricle: the atrial contribution to feline hypertrophic cardiomyopathy and implications for diagnosis, prognosis, and emerging therapy.

**Frontiers in Veterinary Science (2026)** · 종설(narrative review) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43017042/)
관련 주제: [[topics/cardiac-imaging]]

## 국문 요약
고양이 hypertrophic cardiomyopathy(HCM)에서 congestive heart failure, arterial thromboembolism, 생존은 모두 left atrium의 크기·기능과 밀접하며 left ventricular wall thickness와 독립적으로 예후와 연관된다는 점을 정리한 narrative review이다. 최근 종단 근거에서 left ventricular hypertrophy 진단 기준에 도달하기 전에 left atrial fractional shortening 저하가 먼저 검출될 수 있다고 소개한다. 저자들은 atrial pathobiology, atrial imaging, atrial-driven outcomes, rapamycin 등 disease-modifying therapy에 대한 함의, 진료 적용의 5개 주제로 논의하고 atrial endpoint를 중심에 둔 연구 의제를 제안한다.

## English summary
This narrative review argues that the left atrium, rather than the ventricle, determines the key clinical outcomes in feline HCM (congestive heart failure, arterial thromboembolism and survival). It cites longitudinal evidence that reduced left atrial fractional shortening may be detectable before the ventricular wall-thickness criterion is met. The authors discuss atrial pathobiology, imaging, outcomes, implications for emerging therapy such as delayed-release rapamycin, and propose a stage-anchored framework and a research agenda prioritising atrial trial endpoints.

## 임상 시사점
HCM 고양이 심초음파에서 LVWT뿐 아니라 left atrial size·function(LA FS, speckle-tracking 등)을 일상적으로 기록해 두면 staging과 추적에 도움이 될 수 있다. 다만 종설이므로 구체적 cut-off는 원문 근거를 개별 확인해야 한다.

## 연구 설계와 한계
체계적 방법론이 명시되지 않은 narrative review로 문헌 선택 편향 가능성이 있으며, 저자들의 제안(framework)은 전향적 검증을 거치지 않았다. 초록에는 개별 연구의 수치가 제시되지 않았다(14.7% 유병률, 11.3% 10년 thromboembolic risk는 저자가 인용한 값).

## 원문 초록
Hypertrophic cardiomyopathy is the most common heart disease of cats and is conventionally characterised by its ventricular phenotype. The clinical events that determine quality and duration of life in affected cats, however, are atrial in their proximate pathophysiology. Congestive heart failure arises from elevated left atrial pressure transmitted to the pulmonary venous bed, arterial thromboembolism arises from blood stasis in a remodelled and dysfunctional left atrial appendage, and survival tracks left atrial size and function independently of left ventricular wall thickness. Recent longitudinal evidence indicates that lower left atrial fractional shortening can be detected before the diagnostic threshold for left ventricular hypertrophy is met, suggesting the atrium registers the disease before the ventricular wall-thickness criterion does. Despite this body of evidence, contemporary diagnostic frameworks, prognostic models, and the regulatory endpoint underpinning the recent conditional approval of delayed-release rapamycin remain anchored on the ventricle. This narrative review examines the atrial contribution to feline hypertrophic cardiomyopathy through five themes: atrial pathobiology, atrial imaging, atrial-driven outcomes, the implications for emerging disease-modifying therapy, and the practical implications for the cardiology consultation. We outline a framework for stage-anchored therapy decisions and a research agenda prioritising atrial trial endpoints and multicentre replication. In a disease affecting 14.7% of apparently healthy cats, with an 11.3% 10-year thromboembolic risk in preclinical cohorts, we propose that the atrium should now be at the centre of feline hypertrophic cardiomyopathy rather than at its periphery.
