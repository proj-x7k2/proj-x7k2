# EXPRESS: Meningiomas in four cats with pseudocystic fluid accumulation including one microcystic meningioma.

**Journal of feline medicine and surgery (2026)** · 증례군, n=4 · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42786736/) · DOI: 10.1177/1098612X261494688
관련 주제: [[topics/neuro-imaging]]

## 국문 요약
진행성 prosencephalic 증상을 보인 고양이 4마리에서 MRI상 extraaxial mass에 동반된 pseudocystic fluid accumulation이 확인되어 pseudocystic meningioma가 의심되었다. Case 1은 실제 cyst에 가까운 양상이었고, Case 2·3·4는 crescent 모양으로 subdural fluid accumulation에 더 부합했다. 4마리 모두 craniotomy로 종양을 제거했으며, 조직학적으로 Case 1·2·4는 fibrous meningioma, Case 3는 microcystic meningioma로 분류되었다(수의학 문헌에 처음 보고). Case 4의 낭액 분석에서는 단백 함량이 높고 종양성 meningeal cell이 확인되었으며, 낭벽 조직학상 상피 lining 없이 dura mater에 접한 종양성 meningothelial cell이 관찰되었다. 4마리 모두 수술 후 신경학적으로 호전되었고, Case 2·3의 6개월 추적 MRI에서는 국소 뇌막 비후가 관찰되었다.

## English summary
Four cats presenting with progressive prosencephalic signs had an extraaxial mass with an associated pseudocystic component on MRI, raising suspicion for pseudocystic meningioma. Case 1's fluid accumulation resembled a true cyst, whereas Cases 2, 3, and 4 showed a crescent-shaped pattern more consistent with subdural fluid accumulation. All four cats underwent tumor removal via craniotomy. Histologically, the meningiomas were classified as fibrous in Cases 1, 2, and 4, and as microcystic in Case 3 (the first such report in the veterinary literature). Fluid analysis in Case 4 revealed protein-rich fluid with neoplastic meningeal cells, and histology of the cyst wall showed tumoral meningothelial cells lining the dura mater without an epithelial lining. All cats improved neurologically after surgery, and follow-up MRI in Cases 2 and 3 at 6 months postoperatively showed focal meningeal thickening.

## 임상 시사점
고양이에서 extraaxial mass에 pseudocystic fluid accumulation이 동반된 경우 meningioma(fibrous 또는 드물게 microcystic형)를 감별진단에 포함하고, crescent 모양이면 실제 cyst보다는 subdural fluid accumulation 가능성을 먼저 고려하는 것이 합리적이다. 수술 후 6개월 추적 MRI에서 국소 뇌막 비후가 관찰될 수 있으나 이 증례들에서는 임상 경과에 영향을 주지 않았다.

## 연구 설계와 한계
고양이 4마리의 증례군 연구로, MRI 소견과 수술·조직병리 소견을 대조했다. 표본이 4예에 불과해 pseudocystic fluid의 발생 기전(진짜 cyst 형성 vs subdural fluid accumulation)과 임상적 의미를 일반화하기 어렵고, 장기 추적은 일부 증례(2예)에서만 이루어졌다.

## 원문 초록
Case series summaryFour cats were presented with progressive prosencephalic signs. An extraaxial mass associated with a pseudocystic component was identified in each case on MRI and pseudocystic meningiomas were suspected. The appearance of the fluid accumulation in Case 1 was more suggestive of an actual cyst, whereas the crescent shaped fluid accumulation was more compatible with subdural fluid accumulation in Cases 2,3 and 4. All cases underwent mass removal through craniotomy and were discharged from the hospital.The meningiomas were classified as fibrous in case 1,2 and 4, and as microcystic in case 3. The fluid analysis in Case 4 revealed a protein rich fluid with neoplastic meningeal cells. Histology of the wall surrounding the fluid in case 4 revealed tumoral meningothelial cells lined against dura mater, without epithelial lining.All cats improved neurologically after surgical removal of the tumour. Follow-up MRI in cases 2 and 3 performed 6 months post operatively identified focal thickening of the meninges in the two cases (2 and 3).Relevance and novel informationThis case series describes four cats with brain meningiomas associated with a pseudocystic component of unknown origin. Including one microcystic meningioma which has not been previously reported in the veterinary literature. The pseudocyst didn't seem to affect the clinical outcomes and postoperative MRI for these four cats.
