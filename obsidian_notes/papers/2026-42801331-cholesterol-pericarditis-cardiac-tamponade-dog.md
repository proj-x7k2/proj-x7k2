# Idiopathic cholesterol pericarditis in a dog presenting with cardiac tamponade.

**Journal of veterinary internal medicine (2026)** · 증례보고, n=1 · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42801331/) · DOI: 10.1111/jvim.12415
관련 주제: [[topics/cardiac-imaging]]

## 국문 요약
7년 6개월령 중성화 암컷 Pug 1마리가 기면·식욕부진·다음·복부팽만·호흡곤란으로 내원했다. Point-of-care ultrasonography에서 tricavitary effusion과 cardiac tamponade가 확인되었고, pericardiocentesis로 83 mL의 혈성 삼출액을 뽑았다. 임상검사·혈액검사·echocardiography·심전도·흉복부 CT·심낭액 배양을 포함한 검사에서 심혈관·내분비·종양·감염성 원인이 모두 배제되었고, 심낭액에서 높은 콜레스테롤 농도와 다수의 콜레스테롤 결정이 확인되어 cholesterol pericarditis로 진단되었다. 7주 후 subtotal pericardiectomy를 시행했고 조직검사에서 종양은 확인되지 않았으며, 4개월 뒤 재발성 흉수 관리를 위해 pleural port를 삽입했으나 10주 후 삶의 질 저하로 안락사했다.

## English summary
A 7-year-6-month-old spayed female Pug presented with lethargy, hyporexia, polydipsia, abdominal distension, and dyspnea. Point-of-care ultrasonography identified tricavitary effusions and cardiac tamponade, and pericardiocentesis yielded 83 mL of sanguineous fluid. A thorough diagnostic work-up including echocardiography, ECG, thoracic and abdominal CT, and pericardial fluid culture excluded cardiovascular, endocrine, neoplastic, and infectious causes; pericardial fluid analysis showed high cholesterol concentrations and numerous cholesterol crystals, supporting a diagnosis of idiopathic cholesterol pericarditis. Subtotal pericardiectomy was performed 7 weeks later, with histopathology showing no neoplasia, but recurrent pleural effusion required a pleural port and the dog was eventually euthanized for quality-of-life concerns.

## 임상 시사점
원인불명 pericardial effusion·cardiac tamponade 환자에서 echocardiography·CT로 종양성·감염성 원인이 배제되면, 심낭액 세포검사에서 콜레스테롤 결정 유무를 확인해 cholesterol pericarditis 감별을 고려할 가치가 있다.

## 연구 설계와 한계
단일 증례보고로, 진단은 임상검사·echocardiography·흉복부 CT·심낭액 분석(배양 포함)을 통한 다른 원인의 배제와 심낭액 콜레스테롤 소견에 근거했다. 증례 1예이므로 영상 소견의 일반화나 발생 빈도 추정은 불가능하며, 초록에는 CT·echocardiography의 구체적 소견(심낭 두께, 종괴 유무 등)이 상세히 기술되어 있지 않다.

## 원문 초록
A 7-year, 6-month-old, female neutered, Pug dog presented with lethargy, hyporexia, polydipsia, abdominal distension, and dyspnea. Point-of-care ultrasonography identified tricavitary effusions and cardiac tamponade. Pericardiocentesis yielded 83 mL of sanguineous scintillating fluid. Clinical examination, bloodwork, echocardiography, electrocardiography, thoracic and abdominal computed tomography scans, and pericardial fluid analysis including culture and sensitivity did not identify any cardiovascular, endocrine, neoplastic, or infectious causes of the pericardial effusion. Pericardial fluid analysis identified high cholesterol concentrations and numerous cholesterol crystals on cytology, supporting a diagnosis of cholesterol pericarditis. Subtotal pericardiectomy was performed 7 weeks later to address pericardial and pleural effusions. Histopathological pericardial biopsies identified pericarditis with no neoplastic tissue detected. A pleural port was placed 4 months later to manage recurrent pleural effusion. Elective euthanasia was performed 10 weeks later citing a compromised quality of life from increasingly frequent bouts of dyspnea. This is a case report of a dog with idiopathic cholesterol pericarditis presenting with cardiac tamponade.
