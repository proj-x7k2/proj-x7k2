# Diagnostic Value of the Vertebral Right Heart Index (VRHi) for Identifying Pulmonary Hypertension in Dogs with Heartworm Disease.

**Animals (2026)** · 기타(대조군 포함 진단 연구; 전향/후향 여부 초록에 없음), n=54 · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42939169/)
관련 주제: [[topics/cardiac-imaging]]

## 국문 요약
Gran Canaria의 개 54마리(건강 대조군 20마리, Dirofilaria immitis 자연감염 34마리)를 대상으로, 진정 없이 촬영한 thoracic radiograph에서 DICOM 소프트웨어로 vertebral heart size(VHS)와 Vertebral Right Heart Index(VRHi)를 측정했다. 감염견은 echocardiography상 pulmonary hypertension(PH) 가능성이 높은 군과 그렇지 않은 군으로 나뉘었고, VRHi는 세 군 사이에 유의하게 달랐으며 PH 고확률군에서 가장 높았다. VRHi는 right-sided remodelling을 반영하는 echocardiography 지표와 유의한 상관을 보였다. Right lateral projection VRHi의 AUC는 0.835로 가장 높았지만 기존 VHS(AUC 0.764)와 유의한 차이는 없었다(DeLong p=0.519).

## English summary
In 54 dogs (20 healthy controls, 34 naturally infected with D. immitis), VRHi and VHS were measured on non-sedated thoracic radiographs and compared with echocardiographic probability of pulmonary hypertension and right-sided remodelling indices. VRHi differed among the three groups, was highest in dogs with high PH probability, and correlated with right-sided remodelling. The right lateral VRHi had the highest AUC (0.835) but did not differ significantly from VHS (0.764; DeLong p=0.519). The authors suggest VRHi as a simple complementary radiographic parameter.

## 임상 시사점
Heartworm 감염견에서 radiograph를 이미 찍는다면 VRHi를 right lateral 상에서 함께 재 보는 것은 PH 가능성을 가늠하는 보조 수단이 될 수 있다. 다만 VHS보다 유의하게 낫다는 근거는 없고 cut-off도 초록에 제시되지 않아, PH 확인은 여전히 echocardiography로 해야 한다.

## 연구 설계와 한계
감염견 34마리를 PH 유무로 나눈 소규모 단일 지역 연구이며, 기준표준이 PH의 직접 측정이 아니라 echocardiography상 확률 분류이다. VRHi와 VHS의 AUC 차이가 유의하지 않아 우월성은 입증되지 않았고, 하위군별 표본 수와 cut-off, 민감도·특이도는 초록에 없다. PubMed 원자료의 DOI가 논문과 맞지 않는 값이어서 비워 두었다.

## 원문 초록
Pulmonary hypertension (PH) is a common complication of canine heartworm disease caused by Dirofilaria immitis and is associated with right-sided cardiac remodelling. This study evaluated the diagnostic value of the Right Vertebral Heart Index (VRHi) for identifying dogs with a high echocardiographic probability of PH and its association with echocardiographic indices of right-sided cardiac remodelling. Fifty-four dogs from Gran Canaria were included: 20 healthy controls and 34 dogs naturally infected with D. immitis, classified according to the presence or absence of a high echocardiographic probability of PH. Thoracic radiographs were obtained without sedation, and the vertebral heart size (VHS) and VRHi were measured using DICOM software. Heartworm infection was confirmed using a commercial antigen detection test. Echocardiography was performed to assess parasite burden, determine the probability of PH, and evaluate right-sided cardiac remodelling. Dogs with a high echocardiographic probability of PH showed more frequent clinical signs and greater echocardiographic evidence of right-sided cardiac remodelling than dogs without PH. The VRHi values differed significantly among the three study groups and were highest in dogs with a high echocardiographic probability of PH. The VRHi was significantly correlated with echocardiographic indicators of right-sided remodelling. The right lateral projection showed the highest AUC among VRHi measurements (AUC = 0.835), although its AUC did not differ significantly from that of conventional VHS (AUC = 0.764; DeLong p = 0.519). These findings suggest that VRHi may provide a simple and objective complementary radiographic parameter in dogs with heartworm disease and suspected PH.
