# Sinonasal aspergillosis-associated pneumocephalus in a dog: recovery after endoscopic interventional debridement and antifungal treatment.

**Journal of veterinary internal medicine (2026)** · 증례보고, n=1 · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42932827/)
관련 주제: [[topics/head-neck-imaging]]

## 국문 요약
급성 심한 apathy와 만성 비강 분비물로 의뢰된 16세 Border Collie 증례다. 2주 전 CT에서 좌측 nasal cavity와 frontal sinus의 severe turbinate destruction과 mucosal thickening으로 sinonasal aspergillosis(SNA)가 의심되었으나 치료는 시작되지 않았다. 내원 시 MRI에서 intrameningeal pneumocephalus, meningitis, frontal sinusitis와 두개강 쪽 frontal bone의 focal lysis가 확인되었고 CSF 분석은 정상이어서 pneumocephalus가 apathy의 원인으로 추정되었다. Rhinoscopy에서 fungal plaque가 확인되었고(qPCR: Aspergillus spp.), 전신 항진균제와 endoscopic debridement, frontal bone lysis에도 불구한 topical 항진균 치료 후 3주 추적 MRI에서 pneumocephalus가 완전히 소실되었다. 최종 영상에서는 결손 주변 scarring만 있고 활동성 감염은 없었다.

## English summary
A 16-year-old Border Collie with acute apathy and chronic nasal discharge had CT suggestive of sinonasal aspergillosis with severe turbinate destruction. MRI at presentation showed intrameningeal pneumocephalus, meningitis, frontal sinusitis and focal frontal bone lysis; CSF was unremarkable. Rhinoscopy and qPCR confirmed Aspergillus spp. After systemic antifungals, endoscopic debridement and topical antifungal treatment despite the bone defect, 3-week follow-up MRI showed complete resolution of pneumocephalus with residual scarring only.

## 임상 시사점
SNA 개에서 frontal bone lysis와 두개내 pneumocephalus가 보이더라도 topical 항진균 치료를 일률적으로 금기로 볼 필요는 없을 수 있다는 단일 증례 근거가 생겼다. 신경 증상이 새로 생긴 SNA 환자에서는 CT에 더해 MRI로 meningitis와 pneumocephalus를 평가하고, 3주 정도의 추적 MRI로 반응을 확인하는 접근을 고려할 수 있다.

## 원문 초록
A 16-year-old Border Collie was referred for acute severe apathy and chronic nasal discharge. Computed tomography (CT) performed 2 weeks earlier was suggestive of sinonasal aspergillosis (SNA) with severe turbinate destruction and mucosal thickening in the left nasal cavity and frontal sinus. No treatment was initiated. Magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) at presentation identified intrameningeal pneumocephalus, meningitis, frontal sinusitis with ventral focal frontal bone lysis of the cranial cavity. Cerebrospinal fluid analysis was unremarkable, suggesting pneumocephalus as the cause of apathy. Rhinoscopy confirmed fungal plaque (qPCR: Aspergillus spp.). Systemic antifungal treatment and endoscopic debridement were initiated. Despite frontal bone lysis, topical antifungal treatment also was administered. A 3-week follow-up MRI showed complete resolution of pneumocephalus. Final imaging identified scarring around the defect and no active infection. These findings indicate that intrameningeal pneumocephalus secondary to SNA can be reversible and should not preclude antifungal treatment. Endoscopic therapeutic intervention may enable full recovery.
