# Cardiovascular and echocardiographic effects of propofol induction, with or without dextroketamine, in healthy premedicated rabbits: a randomized study.

**Veterinary anaesthesia and analgesia (2026)** · 무작위대조군연구, n=22 · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42710340/) · DOI: 10.1016/j.vaa.2026.101332
관련 주제: [[topics/cardiac-imaging]], [[topics/sedation-restraint-imaging]]

## 국문 요약
건강한 New Zealand white rabbit 22마리를 대상으로, dexmedetomidine·butorphanol·midazolam 전처치 후 propofol 단독(PRO군) 또는 propofol+dextroketamine(PK군)으로 마취 유도했을 때의 심혈관·심초음파 효과를 비교한 무작위·맹검 실험 연구다. Transthoracic echocardiography(TTE)로 기저치, 전처치 후, 유도 직후, 10분 후 시점에 left ventricular dimension, fractional shortening, ejection fraction, cardiac output, longitudinal systolic function 지표를 측정했다. 심박수는 시간에 따라 감소했고(p<0.001), 평균동맥압은 PRO군(76→66mmHg)이 PK군(70→65mmHg)보다 더 크게 감소해 시간 효과(p=0.035)와 group×time 상호작용(p=0.049)이 유의했다. Tricuspid annular plane systolic excursion은 뚜렷한 group×time 상호작용을 보이지 않았고(p=0.141), mitral annular plane systolic excursion도 상호작용은 유의하지 않았으나(p=0.484) PRO군에서 전반적으로 더 높은 값을 보였다(p=0.011).

## English summary
In this randomized, blinded, experimental study of 22 healthy premedicated (dexmedetomidine, butorphanol, midazolam) New Zealand white rabbits, cardiovascular and echocardiographic effects of anesthetic induction with propofol alone (group PRO) versus propofol-dextroketamine (group PK) were compared using transthoracic echocardiography (TTE) at baseline, after premedication, immediately after induction, and 10 minutes later. Heart rate decreased over time (p<0.001). Mean arterial pressure decreased more in the PRO group (76 to 66 mmHg) than the PK group (70 to 65 mmHg), with significant time (p=0.035) and group-by-time interaction (p=0.049) effects. Tricuspid annular plane systolic excursion showed no significant group-by-time interaction (p=0.141); mitral annular plane systolic excursion also showed no significant interaction (p=0.484) despite overall higher values in the PRO group (p=0.011). Adding dextroketamine did not produce detectable treatment-related changes in conventional or longitudinal systolic function indices; both protocols preserved cardiac output and systolic function, though arterial pressure responses differed over time.

## 임상 시사점
토끼에서 심초음파를 시행하기 위해 propofol 유도를 사용할 때, dextroketamine 병용 여부와 무관하게 심수축 기능 지표(fractional shortening, ejection fraction 등)는 대체로 보존되지만, propofol 단독 유도군에서 평균동맥압 저하가 더 크게 나타날 수 있어 마취 유도 중·후 혈압 모니터링이 중요하다.

## 연구 설계와 한계
건강한 premedicated rabbit 22마리를 대상으로 한 무작위·맹검 실험 연구로, mixed-effects model로 반복측정을 분석했다. 건강한 실험동물만을 대상으로 했으므로 심질환이 있거나 임상적으로 불안정한 환자에게 결과를 그대로 적용하기는 어렵고, 초록에는 fractional shortening·ejection fraction·cardiac output의 구체적 수치는 제시되지 않았다.

## 원문 초록
OBJECTIVE: To compare the cardiovascular and echocardiographic effects of anesthetic induction with propofol alone or combined with dextroketamine in healthy rabbits using transthoracic echocardiography (TTE).

STUDY DESIGN: Randomized, blinded, experimental trial.

ANIMALS: A total of 22 healthy male New Zealand white rabbits.

METHODS: Preanesthetic medication consisted of dexmedetomidine (5 μg kg-1), butorphanol (0.5 mg kg-1), and midazolam (0.3 mg kg-1) injected intramuscularly. Animals were randomly assigned to be given intravenous propofol (3 mg kg-1 minute-1; group PRO) or propofol-dextroketamine (propofol 3 mg kg-1 minute-1 and dextroketamine 2 mg kg-1 bolus; group PK) for anesthetic induction. TTE measurements were obtained at baseline, after preanesthetic medication, immediately after induction, and 10 minutes later. Echocardiographic variables included left ventricular dimensions, fractional shortening, ejection fraction, cardiac output, and longitudinal systolic function indices. Heart rate (HR) and arterial blood pressure were also evaluated. Repeated measurements were analyzed using linear mixed-effects models.

RESULTS: HR decreased over time (p < 0.001). Mean arterial pressure showed a greater reduction in the PRO group (from 76 ± 6.5 to 66 ± 5.7 mmHg) than in the PK group (from 70 ± 9.5 to 65 ± 6.4 mmHg), resulting in significant effects of time (p = 0.035) and group-by-time interaction (p = 0.049). The estimated tricuspid annular plane systolic excursion interaction effect at T2 was 0.95 mm (95% confidence interval [CI], -0.09 to 1.99), although the overall group-by-time interaction was not significant (p = 0.141). Mitral annular plane systolic excursion showed no significant group-by-time interaction (p = 0.484), despite greater overall values in the PRO group (estimate -0.62 mm; 95% CI -1.05 to -0.18; p = 0.011).

CONCLUSION AND CLINICAL RELEVANCE: The addition of dextroketamine to propofol induction did not result in detectable treatment-related changes in conventional or longitudinal echocardiographic indices of systolic function. Both induction protocols were associated with preserved cardiac output and systolic function, although arterial pressure responses differed over time.
