# Computed tomography perfusion indicates reduced portal venous perfusion and relative arterial dominance in canine hepatocellular carcinoma.

**American journal of veterinary research (2026)** · 후향적 연구, n=20 · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42710534/) · DOI: 10.2460/ajvr.26.05.0219
관련 주제: [[topics/abdominal-imaging]]

## 국문 요약
수술로 절제되어 조직병리학적으로 massive gross phenotype hepatocellular carcinoma(HCC)로 확진된 개 20마리의 수술 전 CT perfusion(CTP) 데이터를 후향적으로 분석해, dual-input maximum-slope법으로 hepatic arterial flow(HAF)·portal venous flow(PVF)·hepatic perfusion index(HPI)를 산출했다. 종양은 정상 간 조직보다 PVF가 낮고(23.40 vs 127.15 mL/min/100mL, p<0.001) HPI는 더 높았으며(65.81% vs 29.66%, p<0.001), HAF는 유의한 차이가 없었다(52.98 vs 50.29 mL/min/100mL, p=0.154). 분화도별로는 HAF(well>moderate)와 PVF(poor<well)가 유의하게 달랐으나 poorly differentiated 종양이 3예뿐이라 해석에 주의가 필요했다. 저자들은 CTP가 정량적 동맥·문맥 관류 정보를 제공할 수 있지만 형태학적 영상, 수술 소견, 기존 진단·예후 평가법을 대체해서는 안 된다고 강조했다.

## English summary
This retrospective study analyzed preoperative CT perfusion (CTP) data from 20 dogs with surgically excised, histopathologically confirmed massive-phenotype hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC), calculating hepatic arterial flow (HAF), portal venous flow (PVF), and hepatic perfusion index (HPI) via a dual-input maximum-slope method. Tumors showed lower PVF (23.40 vs 127.15 mL/min/100mL, p<0.001) and higher HPI (65.81% vs 29.66%, p<0.001) than nontumorous liver, with no difference in HAF (p=0.154), indicating relative arterial dominance driven mainly by reduced portal flow. HAF and PVF differed among differentiation groups, but these findings should be interpreted cautiously given only 3 poorly differentiated tumors. The authors emphasize CTP should not replace morphologic imaging, surgical assessment, or established diagnostic/prognostic approaches.

## 임상 시사점
CT에서 massive형 HCC가 의심되는 경우 CTP를 통한 관류 패턴(문맥 관류 감소, 상대적 동맥 우세) 확인이 병변의 혈류역학적 특성을 이해하는 데 참고가 될 수 있으나, 저자들 스스로 밝혔듯 조직형·분화도·절제가능성·예후를 CTP만으로 판단해서는 안 된다.

## 연구 설계와 한계
수술적 절제와 조직병리 확진이 이루어진 massive형 HCC 20예에 국한된 단일 기관 후향적 연구로, nodular형·diffuse형 등 다른 HCC 아형에는 적용할 수 없다. 분화도별 비교는 poorly differentiated 종양이 3예에 불과해 통계적 검정력이 제한적이며, 저자들도 이 결과를 신중히 해석해야 한다고 명시했다.

## 원문 초록
OBJECTIVE: To characterize CT perfusion (CTP) in canine massive hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC) and explore associations with descriptive histomorphologic differentiation categories.

METHODS: This retrospective study included 20 client-owned dogs with surgically excised, histopathologically confirmed HCC of the massive gross phenotype and interpretable preoperative CTP datasets. Hepatic arterial flow (HAF), portal venous flow (PVF), and hepatic perfusion index (HPI) were calculated with a dual-input maximum-slope method. Freehand tumor regions of interest were drawn as large as possible along tumor margins while excluding visible vessels and necrotic or cystic regions. The CTP metrics were compared between tumor and nontumorous liver and among differentiation groups.

RESULTS: Tumors had lower PVF than nontumorous liver (23.40 vs 127.15 mL/min/100 mL; P < .001) and higher HPI (65.81% vs 29.66%; P < .001), whereas HAF did not differ (52.98 vs 50.29 mL/min/100 mL; P = .154). Among differentiation groups, HAF differed (global P = .011; well > moderate; adjusted P = .042), PVF differed (global P = .025; poor < well; adjusted P = .031), and HPI did not differ (global P = .664).

CONCLUSIONS: Canine HCC showed relative arterial dominance on CTP driven mainly by reduced PVF. Although HAF and PVF differed among differentiation categories, these findings should be interpreted cautiously because only 3 tumors were poorly differentiated.

CLINICAL RELEVANCE: CTP may provide quantitative arterial and portal perfusion information but should not replace morphologic imaging, surgical assessment, or other established diagnostic and prognostic approaches; it cannot define histologic type or differentiation or predict metastasis, resectability, or prognosis.
