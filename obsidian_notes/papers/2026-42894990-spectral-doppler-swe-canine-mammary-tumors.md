# Diagnostic accuracy of spectral Doppler indices and shear wave elastography in dogs with mammary tumors and their relationship to tumor proliferation.

**Polish journal of veterinary sciences (2026)** · 전향적 연구, n=64 · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42894990/) · DOI: 10.24425/pjvs.2026.3602
관련 주제: [[topics/oncologic-imaging]]

## 국문 요약
개 mammary tumor에서 spectral Doppler 지표와 shear wave elastography(SWE)의 진단 정확도, 그리고 종양 증식(Ki-67)과의 관계를 평가했다. 악성 상피성 종양군 49마리와 정상 mammary tissue 대조군 15마리를 대상으로, 숙련된 sonographer 1인이 mastectomy 전에 L20-5s linear transducer로 B-mode, Doppler, SWE를 시행했고 histopathology를 기준표준으로 삼았다. 악성군은 대체로 heterogeneous·hyperechoic이었고 mixed vascular pattern이 가장 흔했으며, 혈관 수는 histological grade에 따라 달라지지 않았다. Resistive index(RI)는 악성군에서 대조군보다 유의하게 높았으나 PI는 차이가 없었고, PSV와 EDV는 악성 종양에서 증가해 적절한 민감도·특이도를 보였다. Shear wave speed와 stiffness는 악성 종양에서 높았고, 5cm를 넘는 종양과 Grade 3 carcinoma에서 가장 높았다. Tissue Ki-67은 Doppler 속도 지표 및 SWE 측정값과 유의하게 연관되었다. 저자들은 Ki-67, RI, PSV, shear wave speed, elasticity가 정상 조직 대비 악성 종양 구분에서 특이도 100%였다고 보고하며 영상기법의 병용을 권고했다.

## English summary
This study evaluated spectral Doppler indices and shear wave elastography (SWE) in canine mammary tumors and their relation to tumor proliferation. Forty-nine dogs with malignant epithelial tumors and 15 healthy mammary tissue controls were scanned by a single experienced sonographer before mastectomy (L20-5s linear transducer), with histopathology as the gold standard. Malignant tumors were mostly heterogeneous and hyperechoic with a mixed vascular pattern; vascularization did not differ by histological grade. RI was higher in malignant tumors than controls, PI did not differ, and PSV and EDV were increased with adequate sensitivity and specificity. Shear wave speed and stiffness were higher in malignant tumors, highest in tumors over 5 cm and in Grade 3 versus Grade 2 carcinomas, and tissue Ki-67 correlated with Doppler velocimetry and SWE values. The authors reported 100% specificity for Ki-67, RI, PSV, shear wave speed and elasticity in separating malignant tumors from healthy tissue and recommend combining techniques.

## 임상 시사점
Mastectomy 전 초음파에서 RI·PSV 같은 Doppler 지표와 SWE stiffness가 높으면 악성 및 높은 증식 활성을 시사하는 보조 소견으로 참고할 수 있다. 다만 비교 대상이 정상 mammary tissue이고 양성 종양이나 염증성 병변과의 감별은 다루지 않았으므로, 이 수치로 aspiration cytology나 histopathology를 대체하거나 양성·악성을 가를 수는 없다.

## 연구 설계와 한계
악성 상피성 종양 49마리와 정상 조직 15마리를 비교한 단일기관 연구로 단일 sonographer가 검사했다. 대조군이 양성 종양이 아닌 정상 조직이어서 임상에서 실제로 어려운 양성 대 악성 감별 성능은 알 수 없고, 특이도 100%는 정상 조직 대비 결과이며 cut-off 값과 관찰자 간 재현성은 초록에 제시되지 않았다.

## 원문 초록
The combination of several sonographic techniques can provide important information on pathological feature of mammary tumors. This study aims to evaluate the diagnostic accuracy of spectral Doppler indices and shear wave elastography (SWE) in dogs with mammary tumors and their relationship to tumor proliferation. The study consisted of malignant epithelial tumors (Group MET; n=49) and healthy mammary tissues (Group C; n=15). Ultrasonographic findings (echotexture, echogenicity, mass width, etc.), tissue Ki-67 levels, Doppler ultrasonography and sonoelastographic measurements were performed. Ultrasonography was performed by a single experienced veterinary sonographer prior to mastectomy using a Resona i9 US device equipped with a linear transducer (L20-5s). Histopathological results were used as the gold standard. Tissue Ki-67 levels were measured using a canine Ki-67 Protein (Ki67P) ELISA kit. The distribution of echotexture and echogenicity in Group MET were mostly heterogeneous and hyperechoic. Also, the most frequent vascular pattern was a mixed pattern in Group MET, and the vascularization amount did not differ in terms of histological grades in canine mammary carcinomas. Vascular resistivity (RI) in Group MET was significantly higher than in Group C while pulsatility indexes (PI) was not significantly different between the groups. Peak systolic velocity (PSV) and end diastolic velocity (EDV) increased in malignant tumors and showed adequate sensitivity and specificity values to distinguish the malignant tumors than healthy mammary tissues. Shear wave speed and stiffness of malignant tumors were higher than healthy mammary tissue. The highest shear wave speed and tissue elasticity were observed in tumors larger than 5 cm. Elastography parameters in Grade 3 carcinomas were higher than in Grade 2 carcinomas. Tissue Ki-67 concentration was significantly associated with Doppler velocimetry parameters and SWE measurements. Also, Ki-67 was accurate in distingiuishing the healthy mammary tissue than canine mammary masses with moderate sensitivity and high specificity. Tissue Ki-67, RI, PSV, shear wave speed, and elasticity amounts have 100% specificity to distinguish malignant mammary tumors compared to healthy mammary tissues in dogs. It was concluded that a combination of imaging techniques should be applied for a more accurate diagnosis of mammary tumors.
