# A New Application of 64CuCl2 PET/CT: Imaging of Cats With Mammary Carcinoma.

**Veterinary and Comparative Oncology (2026)** · 전향적 연구(탐색적), n=8 · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42876319/) · DOI: 10.1111/vco.70125
관련 주제: [[topics/oncologic-imaging]]

## 국문 요약
자연발생 feline mammary carcinoma(FMC) 고양이 7마리와 건강 대조 1마리에게 64CuCl2를 정맥 투여하고 whole-body PET/CT를 시행해 feasibility를 평가한 탐색적 연구다. 모든 원발 종괴가 64Cu-avid였고 tumour-to-muscle ratio가 일관되게 높아 종양과 정상 조직의 구분이 가능했다. 영상으로 physical examination에서 충분히 드러나지 않던 locally invasive 및 metastatic disease 정보가 추가로 확인되었다. 생리적 분포는 liver·gallbladder 고섭취, skeletal muscle 저섭취였고, serum ceruloplasmin은 종양 섭취와 상관이 없었다.

## English summary
In this exploratory study, seven cats with naturally occurring mammary carcinoma and one healthy control underwent whole-body PET/CT after intravenous 64CuCl2. All primary masses were 64Cu-avid with elevated tumour-to-muscle ratios, and imaging revealed locally invasive and metastatic disease not fully evident on physical examination. Physiologic uptake was high in liver and gallbladder and low in skeletal muscle. Serum ceruloplasmin varied widely and did not correlate with tumour uptake.

## 임상 시사점
PET/CT는 일반 2차 병원에서 접근하기 어려운 modality이므로 내일 진료가 바뀌지는 않는다. 다만 FMC 병기 결정에서 physical examination과 기존 영상만으로는 놓칠 수 있는 침습·전이 병변이 있을 수 있다는 점을 염두에 둘 만하다.

## 연구 설계와 한계
종양 보유 7마리 + 대조 1마리의 소규모 탐색적 연구로 비교 modality나 진단 정확도 평가는 없다. 저자들이 밝혔듯 모든 영상 소견이 cytology·histopathology로 확인되지는 않았다.

## 원문 초록
Feline mammary carcinoma (FMC) is an aggressive and highly metastatic cancer that shares molecular and clinical characteristics with human triple-negative breast cancer (TNBC), making it a relevant comparative model for a disease lacking targeted therapies. This study investigated the feasibility and clinical utility of 64CuCl2 PET/CT imaging in cats with naturally occurring mammary carcinoma. Eight cats (seven tumour-bearing, one healthy control) were administered an intravenous dose of 64CuCl2 and underwent whole-body PET/CT imaging. Semi-quantitative analysis was performed using standardized uptake values (SUVs), and tumour-to-muscle ratios were calculated to assess lesion conspicuity. Serum ceruloplasmin concentrations were measured as a marker of systemic copper metabolism. All primary masses were 64Cu-avid and conspicuous, with consistently elevated tumour-to-muscle ratios across tumour-bearing cats, allowing clear differentiation between neoplastic and normal tissue. Imaging provided additional staging information by identifying locally invasive and metastatic disease that was not fully evident on physical examination. Physiologic tracer distribution was characterized by high uptake in the liver and gallbladder and low uptake in skeletal muscle, which served as a reliable reference tissue. Serum ceruloplasmin concentrations varied widely and did not correlate with tumour uptake. These findings suggest that 64CuCl2 PET/CT is a feasible functional imaging modality for FMC, potentially aiding in tumour detection. This was an exploratory study, and not all imaging results could be confirmed by cytology or histopathology. Nonetheless, our findings suggest that 64CuCl2 PET/CT should be investigated further as a method for probing copper utilization in FMC.
