# Case Report: Repeated imaging and surgical treatment of a vascular hamartoma arising from the cervical vertebral column of a cat.

**Frontiers in veterinary science (2026)** · 증례보고, n=1 · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42781373/) · DOI: 10.5603/RPOR.a2023.0009
관련 주제: [[topics/spinal-imaging]], [[topics/vascular-imaging]]

## 국문 요약
3세 중성화 수컷 Domestic Shorthair 고양이가 1년간 진행성 우측 편측 proprioceptive ataxia, ambulatory tetraparesis, cervical 통증을 보이다가 급격히 악화되어 내원했다. 신경학적 검사는 우측 C6-T2 myelopathy에 합당했고, MRI에서 C5 우측 lamina·vertebral body에서 기원한 골 증식을 동반한 우측 extradural myelocompressive lesion이 확인되어 심한 척수 압박과 좌측으로의 척수 편위를 일으켰다. 수술적 절제 후 조직병리로 vertebral vascular hamartoma가 확진되었으며, 술 후 일시적 악화를 거쳐 임상 증상은 호전되었다. 1년 후 신경학적 결손이 재발해 MRI·CT로 병변 재성장이 확인되었고, 2차 절제 후 조직병리에서 다시 vertebral vascular hamartoma가 확인되었다.

## English summary
A 3-year-old male neutered Domestic Shorthair cat presented with a 1-year history of progressive right-lateralized proprioceptive ataxia, ambulatory tetraparesis, and cervical spinal pain, with sudden deterioration shortly before presentation. Neurological examination was consistent with a right-sided C6-T2 myelopathy. MRI revealed a right-sided extradural myelocompressive lesion at the level of C5, associated with osseous proliferation arising from the right lamina and vertebral body, causing severe spinal cord compression and leftward spinal cord deviation. The mass was surgically resected, and histopathology confirmed a vertebral vascular hamartoma; clinical signs improved after a brief period of postoperative worsening. One year later, neurological deficits recurred, and MRI and CT confirmed regrowth of the vertebral lesion. A second surgical excision was performed, with histopathology again confirming vertebral vascular hamartoma.

## 임상 시사점
젊은 고양이에서 진행성 편측 myelopathy와 함께 MRI상 골 증식을 동반한 extradural spinal cord compression이 보인다면 vertebral vascular hamartoma를 감별진단에 포함해야 하며, 수술적 절제가 불완전할 경우 재성장 가능성이 있으므로 술 후 신경학적 결손 재발 시 MRI·CT를 이용한 재평가를 고려해야 한다.

## 연구 설계와 한계
증례 1예에 대한 반복 영상(MRI·CT)·2회 수술·조직병리를 기록한 증례보고로, 저자들은 이 병태에서 추적 영상과 반복 수술·조직병리 확인을 함께 기술한 첫 보고라고 밝혔다. 단일 증례이므로 vertebral vascular hamartoma의 전형적 영상 소견, 재성장 위험 요인, 완전 절제를 위한 최적 수술 범위는 이 연구만으로 일반화할 수 없다.

## 원문 초록
A 3-year-old male neutered Domestic Shorthair cat was presented with a 1-year history of progressive right-lateralized proprioceptive ataxia, ambulatory tetraparesis and cervical spinal pain, with sudden deterioration shortly before presentation. Neurological examination was consistent with a right-sided C6-T2 myelopathy. Magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) revealed a right-sided extradural myelocompressive lesion at the level of the C5 vertebra, associated with osseous proliferation arising from the right lamina and vertebral body, causing severe spinal cord compression and leftward deviation of the spinal cord. The mass was surgically resected, and histopathology confirmed a vertebral vascular hamartoma. Clinical signs improved after a brief period of postoperative worsening. One year later, neurological deficits recurred. MRI and computed tomography (CT) confirmed regrowth of the vertebral lesion. A second surgical excision was performed, and histopathology again confirmed a vertebral vascular hamartoma. To the authors' knowledge, this is the first report describing follow-up imaging and repeated surgical intervention, with histopathological confirmation at both procedures, in a cat with recurrent cervical vertebral vascular hamartoma. It highlights vertebral vascular hamartoma as an important differential diagnosis for young cats with progressive myelopathy, extradural spinal cord compression and vertebral osseous proliferation, and suggests that surgical intervention can result in meaningful clinical improvement despite possible regrowth after incomplete resection.
