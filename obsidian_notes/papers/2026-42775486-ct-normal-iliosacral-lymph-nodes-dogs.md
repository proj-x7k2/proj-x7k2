# Computed tomographic Characteristics of Presumed Normal Canine Iliosacral Lymph Nodes.

**Veterinary radiology & ultrasound (2026)** · 후향적 연구, n=71 · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42775486/) · DOI: 10.3906/vet-1507-78
관련 주제: [[topics/oncologic-imaging]]

## 국문 요약
개 71마리의 CT 영상을 ACVR 인증 영상의학전문의가 후향적으로 검토해 medial iliac(MILN)·internal iliac(IILN)·sacral(SLN) lymph node의 최소·최대 횡경(MinTD/MaxTD) 정상 참고치를 산출했다. MILN은 99%, IILN은 79%, SLN은 41%에서 확인되었고, 중앙값 MinTD/MaxTD는 MILN 4.2/7.4mm, IILN 2.5/4.0mm, SLN 2.9/4.9mm였다. MaxTD는 MILN의 19%·IILN의 1%에서 RECIST v1.1 기준 10mm를 초과했지만 MinTD는 거의 모든 림프절에서 10mm 미만이어서, 저자들은 치료반응 평가 시 MaxTD보다 MinTD가 RECIST v1.1의 비병적 림프절 정의(<10mm)에 더 부합한다고 결론지었다. 중성화하지 않은 개에서 대부분의 횡경이 더 컸고(p≤0.013), 대동맥 직경과 체중이 양의 상관관계를 보였으며 대동맥 직경 8.6mm 초과 개체는 MILN·IILN 직경이 더 컸다(p≤0.034).

## English summary
CT scans from 71 dogs were retrospectively reviewed by an ACVR-certified veterinary radiologist to establish minimum and maximum transverse diameter (MinTD/MaxTD) reference values for presumed normal medial iliac (MILN), internal iliac (IILN), and sacral (SLN) lymph nodes. MILNs were identified in 99% of dogs, IILNs in 79%, and SLNs in 41%. Median MinTD/MaxTD were 4.2/7.4 mm (MILN), 2.5/4.0 mm (IILN), and 2.9/4.9 mm (SLN). MaxTD exceeded the RECIST v1.1 10 mm threshold in 19% of MILN sites and 1% of IILN sites, while MinTD stayed below 10 mm in nearly all nodes, leading the authors to recommend MinTD as the preferred measurement for RECIST v1.1-based therapeutic response evaluation. Intact dogs had larger diameters for most measures (p ≤ 0.013), and aortic diameter correlated with body weight and larger MILN/IILN diameters.

## 임상 시사점
종양 병기 결정이나 RECIST v1.1 기반 치료반응 평가 시 iliosacral lymph node를 판독할 때 MaxTD보다 MinTD를 기준으로 삼는 것이 정상/병적 판단의 위양성을 줄일 수 있으며, 중성화 여부·대동맥 직경 등 개체 특성에 따라 정상 범위가 달라질 수 있음을 고려해야 한다.

## 연구 설계와 한계
단일기관 후향적 연구로, "정상으로 추정되는" 림프절을 대상으로 했다는 점에서 실제 조직학적 확진(정상 vs 미세전이) 없이 CT 형태만으로 정상 여부를 판단한 한계가 있다. IILN·SLN은 각각 79%·41%에서만 확인되어 해부학적 변이(편측성)에 따른 검출률 차이가 있었고, 초록에는 나이·견종 등 다른 교란변수에 대한 보정 여부가 명시되지 않았다.

## 원문 초록
Computed tomographic (CT) evaluation of medial iliac (MILN), internal iliac (IILN), and sacral (SLN) lymph nodes (LNs) in dogs provides vital staging information in certain neoplastic conditions. This retrospective study aimed to determine minimum transverse diameter (MinTD) and maximum transverse diameter (MaxTD) values for presumed normal iliosacral LNs and assess their utility in evaluating therapeutic response using response evaluation criteria in solid tumors (RECIST) v1.1. LN margins, number, and pre-contrast attenuation were also analyzed. The CT scans of 71 dogs were reviewed by an ACVR-certified veterinary radiologist. The MILNs were identified in 99% of dogs, predominantly bilaterally. The IILNs and SLNs were identified in 79% and 41% of dogs, respectively, with more variability in bilateral versus unilateral presence. Median values for MinTD/MaxTD were 4.2/7.4 mm for MILNs, 2.5/4.0 mm for IILNs, and 2.9/4.9 mm for SLNs. MinTD was <10 mm in all but one LN; MaxTD exceeded 10 mm in 19% of MILN sites and 1% of IILN sites. These results suggest that MinTD better reflects the RECIST v1.1 definition of non-pathologic LNs (i.e., LNs <10 mm) and is the recommended measurement when evaluating therapeutic response. Transverse diameters, except MILN MaxTD, were larger in intact dogs (p ≤ 0.013), and females had larger SLN MaxTD (p = 0.047). Aortic diameter and body weight were positively correlated (r = 0.7, p < 0.001), and dogs with an aortic diameter >8.6 mm had greater diameters for MILNs and IILNs (p ≤ 0.034). Separate MinTD values for IILNs and SLNs are presented herein, whereas prior studies reported them as a combined group.
