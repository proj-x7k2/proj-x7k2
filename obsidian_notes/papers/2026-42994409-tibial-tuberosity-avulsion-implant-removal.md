# Tibial Anatomy and Functional Outcome in Growing Dogs with Tibial Tuberosity Avulsion Fracture Repair Followed by Implant Removal.

**Veterinary and Comparative Orthopaedics and Traumatology (2026)** · 증례군(Cohort A 5마리 + 역사적 대조 Cohort B 6마리), n=5 · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42994409/) · DOI: 10.1055/a-2991-3052
관련 주제: [[topics/musculoskeletal-imaging]]

## 국문 요약
Tibial tuberosity avulsion fracture를 open reduction·fixation으로 치료한 성장기 개 5마리(Cohort A)에서 수술 후 23-28일에 implant를 제거했고, 양측 hindlimb radiograph와 objective gait analysis로 tibial anatomy와 기능을 평가했다. 수술한 limb은 정상 limb보다 tibial plateau angle(TPA)이 낮고 axial tibial tuberosity position은 크며 transverse tibial tuberosity position은 작았지만, total pressure index에는 유의한 차이가 없었다. Implant를 제거하지 않은 역사적 코호트(Cohort B)와 비교해 의미 있는 해부학적 차이는 없었으나, TPA가 0° 미만인 개는 Cohort A에서 없었고 Cohort B에서는 2/6마리였다. Refracture는 없었다.

## English summary
Five growing dogs that had implants removed 23-28 days after tibial tuberosity avulsion fracture repair underwent bilateral hindlimb radiographs and objective gait analysis. Operated limbs had a lower tibial plateau angle, greater axial and smaller transverse tuberosity position than normal limbs, yet total pressure index did not differ. Anatomy was not clearly different from a historical cohort with implants left in situ, although none of the Cohort A dogs had a negative TPA versus 2/6 in Cohort B. Early implant removal did not preserve normal tibial anatomy, but function was maintained.

## 임상 시사점
성장기 개의 tibial tuberosity avulsion 수술 후 추적에서는 양측 radiograph로 TPA와 tuberosity position을 정상 limb과 비교 측정해 두는 것이 좋다. 3-4주 implant 제거는 해부학적 변형을 막지 못했으므로 변형 예방 목적으로 기대하지 말고, 극단적 음의 TPA 여부와 기능 평가를 함께 보는 것이 합리적이다 (5마리 근거로 제한적).

## 연구 설계와 한계
Cohort A 5마리의 사후 방사선 측정·gait analysis를 역사적 Cohort B(6마리)와 비교한 소규모 연구로, 무작위 배정·동시 대조군이 없다. 표본이 매우 작아 통계적 검정력이 낮고, 비교군이 역사적 자료여서 Cohort B와의 차이 없음/있음 모두 확정적이지 않다. 추적 기간과 방사선 측정자 간 신뢰도는 초록에 없다.

## 원문 초록
OBJECTIVES: The aims of this study were to compare tibial anatomy between a cohort of dogs that had implants removed following repair of tibial tuberosity avulsion fracture and a historical cohort that had implants left in situ, and to objectively quantify patient function to determine if deformity resulted in impaired function.

METHODS: Dogs that had open reduction and fixation of tibial tuberosity avulsion fracture with subsequent implant removal (Cohort A) had radiographs of both hindlimbs and objective gait analysis. Tibial plateau angle, patellar position, axial and transverse tibial tuberosity position, and total pressure index were compared between operated and normal limbs. Measurements of tibial anatomy of the operated limbs were then compared with a historical cohort of dogs that did not have implants removed (Cohort B).

RESULTS: Five dogs in Cohort A had implants removed 23 to 28 days after tibial tuberosity avulsion fracture repair with no refractures. Operated limbs had a lower tibial plateau angle, greater axial tibial tuberosity position and smaller transverse tibial tuberosity position when compared with normal limbs, but there was no significant difference in total pressure index between operated and normal limbs. There were no clearly meaningful differences in tibial anatomy between Cohort A and B. However, Cohort A had no dogs with a tibial plateau angle less than 0 degrees, while 2/6 dogs in Cohort B had a negative tibial plateau angle.

CONCLUSION: Implant removal 23 to 28 days after tibial tuberosity avulsion fracture repair did not preserve normal tibial anatomy; however, objective function was maintained.
