# Influence of including or excluding endocardial borders on left ventricular wall thickness measurements in cats.

**Journal of veterinary internal medicine (2026)** · 후향적 연구, n=52 · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42820725/) · DOI: 10.11613/BM.2012.031
관련 주제: [[topics/cardiac-imaging]]

## 국문 요약
건강, HCM 의증(equivocal), HCM 표현형으로 이전에 분류된 고양이 52마리의 저장된 echocardiography 영상을 관찰자 3인이 4가지 서로 다른 leading-edge/trailing-edge 조합(leading-to-leading, leading-to-trailing, trailing-to-leading, trailing-to-trailing)으로 interventricular septum(IVS)·left ventricular free wall(LVFW) 두께를 측정해 비교한 단일기관 후향적 cross-sectional 연구다. Leading-edge-to-trailing-edge 측정법이 가장 큰 값을(IVS 중앙값 5.5mm), trailing-edge-to-leading-edge 측정법이 가장 작은 값을(IVS 4.3mm, LVFW 4.8mm) 보였으며(모두 p<.001), IVS에서 두 방법 간 중앙값 차이는 1.2mm였다.

## English summary
In a single-center retrospective cross-sectional study, three observers measured interventricular septal and left ventricular free wall thickness in 52 cats previously classified as healthy, equivocal for HCM, or having an HCM phenotype, using four different endocardial border combinations (leading-to-leading, leading-to-trailing, trailing-to-leading, trailing-to-trailing edge). Leading-edge-to-trailing-edge measurements were significantly larger (median IVS 5.5 mm) and trailing-edge-to-leading-edge measurements significantly smaller (median IVS 4.3 mm, LVFW 4.8 mm) than other methods (all p < .001), with a 1.2 mm median difference in IVS thickness between the two extreme methods. The authors conclude that left ventricular wall thickness varies meaningfully depending on which endocardial borders are included, and recommend standardizing the number of borders used.

## 임상 시사점
어떤 endocardial border 조합을 사용하느냐에 따라 같은 고양이의 IVS·LVFW 두께 측정값이 최대 1.2mm까지 달라질 수 있고, 이는 HCM 여부를 가르는 cut-off(흔히 약 6mm 전후) 근처에서 개체의 분류를 바꿀 수 있는 크기다. 따라서 LVWT를 측정할 때 어떤 edge convention을 쓰는지 명확히 하고, 추적검사에서도 동일한 방법을 일관되게 적용해야 하며, 특히 borderline 두께를 보이는 고양이에서는 이 점이 중요하다.

## 연구 설계와 한계
단일기관 후향적 cross-sectional 연구로 저장된 2D echocardiography 정지영상을 이용했으며, 관찰자 간·관찰자 내 반복성은 평가했으나 실제 영상 획득 당시의 beam angle·probe position 등 추가 변동 요인은 포함되지 않았다. 52마리 단일 코호트 자료로, 다른 기관·장비·관찰자 집단에서도 동일한 크기의 측정 차이가 나타나는지는 검증되지 않았다.

## 원문 초록
BACKGROUND: Consensus is lacking on which endocardial borders to include when measuring left ventricular wall thickness (LVWT) in cats.

HYPOTHESIS/OBJECTIVES: The number of endocardial borders included can influence whether an individual cat is classified as having hypertrophic cardiomyopathy (HCM) or not.

ANIMALS: Fifty-two cats that previously were diagnosed as either healthy, equivocal for HCM, or having an HCM phenotype.

METHODS: Single-center retrospective cross-sectional study. Three observers measured LVWT using 4 different LVWT measurement protocols: leading edge-to-leading edge (L-L); leading edge-to-trailing edge, trailing edge-to-leading edge, and trailing edge-to-trailing edge for the interventricular septum and L-L and trailing edge-to-leading edge methods for the left ventricular free wall (LVFW). Measurements were made using stored echocardiographic images on 2 occasions by 2 observers. A Friedman test and post-hoc Wilcoxon signed rank test with Bonferroni correction were performed to detect differences between measurement techniques.

RESULTS: Interventricular septum (IVS) wall thickness was significantly larger for leading-edge-to-trailing-edge measurement (median and [interquartile range (IQR)]: 5.5 [3.8-7.2] millimeters) compared with other methods (P < .001). The trailing-edge-to-leading-edge method produced significantly smaller results (4.3 [IQR, 3.4-6.2] millimeters for the interventricular septum and 4.8 [IQR, 3.4-6.2] millimeters for the LVFW) compared with other methods (P < .001). Median IVS thickness measured as leading-edge-to-trailing-edge was 1.2 millimeters larger than when measured as trailing-edge-to-leading-edge.

CONCLUSIONS AND CLINICAL IMPORTANCE: LVWT varies depending on the number of endocardial borders included. The number of endocardial borders included in LVWT measurements should be standardized.
