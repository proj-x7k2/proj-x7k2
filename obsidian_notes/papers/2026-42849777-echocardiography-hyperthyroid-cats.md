# Echocardiographic changes in hyperthyroid cats: Comparison of subclinical disease and congestive heart failure.

**Veterinary journal (London, England : 1997) (2026)** · 후향적 연구, n=98 · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42849777/) · DOI: 10.1016/j.tvjl.2026.106915
관련 주제: [[topics/cardiac-imaging]]

## 국문 요약
Hyperthyroidism이 있는 고양이에서 subclinical 심장 침범과 congestive heart failure(CHF)를 구분하는 요인이 명확하지 않다는 문제의식에서, subclinical 심장질환 고양이 51마리와 CHF로 내원한 고양이 47마리를 후향적으로 비교한 case-control 연구다. 모든 고양이는 standard echocardiography와 혈청 total thyroxine(TT4)을 포함한 생화학검사를 받았고, congestion 유형은 흉부 방사선 또는 point-of-care ultrasound로 판정했다. CHF군은 subclinical군보다 심방 크기와 좌심실 내경이 유의하게 더 컸고, left atrial dysfunction이 두 군을 구분하는 가장 강력한 지표였다. CHF군 내에서는 pleural/mixed effusion이 pulmonary oedema 단독보다 우세했으며 우심계·전신 volume overload가 더 심했다. TT4 농도는 두 군 간 유의한 차이가 없었다.

## English summary
This retrospective case-control study compared echocardiographic findings between 51 hyperthyroid cats with subclinical cardiac disease and 47 hyperthyroid cats presenting in congestive heart failure (CHF), addressing the poorly defined factors distinguishing subclinical cardiac involvement from decompensation. All cats underwent standard echocardiography and serum biochemistry including total thyroxine (TT4), with congestion type determined by thoracic radiography or point-of-care ultrasound. Cats with CHF had significantly larger atrial dimensions and increased left ventricular internal diameter, with left atrial dysfunction being the most robust discriminator between groups. Pleural or mixed effusion was the predominant CHF phenotype and was associated with greater right-sided and global volume overload than pulmonary oedema alone. TT4 concentrations did not differ significantly between groups or CHF subtypes.

## 임상 시사점
Hyperthyroid 고양이를 검사할 때 TT4 수치만으로는 심장 침범 정도를 예측할 수 없으므로, 특히 left atrial function 평가를 포함한 포괄적 echocardiography를 통해 CHF로의 진행 위험을 조기에 선별해야 하며, pleural/mixed effusion이 의심되면 우심계 volume overload 평가를 함께 고려해야 한다.

## 연구 설계와 한계
51마리(subclinical)와 47마리(CHF) 고양이의 의무기록을 이용한 후향적 case-control 연구로, congestion 유형 판정에 흉부 방사선과 point-of-care ultrasound가 혼용되어 검사 방법 간 일관성이 다를 수 있다. 후향적 설계상 echocardiography 측정의 관찰자 간 재현성이나 추적 기간에 따른 CHF 진행 여부는 평가되지 않았으며, hyperthyroidism 치료 상태(치료 전/후)가 결과에 미치는 영향도 초록에서는 알 수 없다.

## 원문 초록
BACKGROUND: Hyperthyroidism induces a wide spectrum of cardiovascular changes in cats, yet the factors distinguishing subclinical cardiac involvement from congestive heart failure (CHF) remain poorly defined. Previous studies have focused primarily on structural changes, with limited evaluation of atrial function or right-sided involvement.

OBJECTIVES: To compare echocardiographic variables between hyperthyroid cats with subclinical cardiac disease and those presenting in CHF, with emphasis on left atrial remodelling, left atrial function, and left ventricular dimensional changes, and right-sided involvement assessed by right atrial diameter. The comparison of clinical and biochemical variables was a secondary objective.

METHODS: A retrospective case-control study was performed using medical records of 51 hyperthyroid cats with subclinical cardiac disease and 47 hyperthyroid cats presenting in CHF. All cats underwent standard echocardiography, serum biochemistry including total thyroxine (TT4). Type of congestion was determined by thoracic radiography or point-of-care ultrasound, and CHF cats were classified as having pulmonary oedema only or pleural/mixed effusion.

RESULTS: Cats with CHF had significantly larger atrial dimensions and increased left ventricular internal diameter compared with subclinical cats. Left atrial dysfunction was the most robust discriminator between groups. Pleural or mixed effusion was the predominant CHF phenotype and, compared with pulmonary oedema alone, was associated with greater right-sided and global volume overload. TT4 concentrations did not differ significantly between cats with and without CHF. Similarly, biochemical variables showed no significant differences between subclinical or CHF cats or between CHF subtypes.

CONCLUSIONS: Atrial remodelling - particularly impaired atrial function and biatrial enlargement - represents the clearest echocardiographic marker of decompensation in hyperthyroid cats. The predominance of pleural or mixed effusion highlights the importance of assessing right-sided cardiac measurements. Comprehensive echocardiographic evaluation is essential for early identification of cats at risk of CHF, whereas TT4 concentration alone does not predict cardiac status.
