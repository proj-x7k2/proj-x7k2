# Quantitative Assessment of Maxillary Canine, Fourth Premolar, and First Molar Root Surface Areas and Ratios in 68 Brachycephalic Dogs Using Cone-Beam Computed Tomography.

**Journal of veterinary dentistry (2026)** · 후향적 연구, n=68 · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42853250/) · DOI: 10.1177/08987564261494957
관련 주제: [[topics/head-neck-imaging]]

## 국문 요약
Brachycephalic 개의 교합부전 교정 시 anchorage 설계에 필요한 root surface area(RSA) 자료가 없다는 문제의식에서, cone-beam computed tomography(CBCT) 영상 68마리(119개 dental arcade)를 후향적으로 분석해 상악 canine·fourth premolar(PM4)·first molar(M1)의 RSA와 비율을 정량화했다. 모든 arcade(119/119, 100%)에서 canine:PM4+M1 비율이 1 미만이었고, canine:PM4 비율은 61.35%에서, canine:M1 비율은 1.68%(모두 10kg 미만 소형견)에서만 1 미만이었다. 체중은 canine:PM4+M1 비율과 유의하게 양의 상관관계를 보였으나(P=.0006) 모든 체중군에서 비율은 1 미만으로 유지되었고, 소형견은 중형견(P=.006)·대형견(P=.03)보다 이 비율이 유의하게 낮았다.
저자들은 이 결과가 PM4와 M1을 결합한 reinforced anchorage unit으로서 상악 canine 교정 이동에 활용할 근거가 된다고 결론지었다.

## English summary
This retrospective study quantified root surface area (RSA) of the maxillary canine, fourth premolar (PM4), and first molar (M1) using CBCT scans from 68 brachycephalic dogs (119 dental arcades), addressing the lack of RSA data needed to plan orthodontic anchorage in this population. All arcades (119/119, 100%) had a canine-to-combined-PM4+M1 RSA ratio of less than 1; the canine-to-PM4 ratio was below 1 in 61.35% of arcades, while the canine-to-M1 ratio was below 1 in only 1.68%, all in dogs under 10 kg. Body weight was positively associated with the canine:PM4+M1 ratio (P = .0006), though the ratio remained below 1 across all weight classes, and small dogs had significantly lower ratios than medium (P = .006) and large dogs (P = .03). These findings support using the PM4 and M1 together as a reinforced anchorage unit for orthodontic movement of the maxillary canine in brachycephalic dogs.

## 임상 시사점
Brachycephalic 개에서 상악 canine malocclusion의 active force orthodontics를 계획할 때, PM4 단독보다는 PM4+M1을 결합한 anchorage unit을 우선 고려할 수 있으며, 특히 10kg 미만 소형 brachycephalic 견에서는 canine 자체의 RSA가 상대적으로 더 작아 anchorage 설계에 더 신중해야 한다.

## 연구 설계와 한계
68마리 brachycephalic 개의 CBCT 영상 119개 dental arcade를 이용한 후향적 관찰 연구로, 치아를 segmentation하여 RSA를 산출하고 체중군(<10kg, 10-25kg, >25kg)별로 비율을 비교했다. 단면 CBCT 자료에 기반한 정량 분석으로 실제 orthodontic 치료 후 anchorage 성공률이나 치아 이동 결과와는 직접 연관되지 않으며, brachycephalic 견종 간 두개안면 형태 다양성이 결과에 미치는 영향은 초록만으로는 평가할 수 없다.

## 원문 초록
Malocclusions involving the maxillary canine teeth are common in brachycephalic dogs and may require active force orthodontics. Orthodontic anchorage is influenced by root surface area (RSA), yet the RSA of orthodontically important teeth has not been quantified in brachycephalic dogs. The objectives were to quantify the RSA of the maxillary canine, fourth premolar (PM4), and first molar (M1) teeth using cone-beam computed tomography (CBCT), compare the canine RSA with the PM4, M1, and combined PM4 + M1 RSAs, and evaluate the relationship of body weight to these ratios. A retrospective observational study was performed using CBCT scans from 68 brachycephalic dogs (119 dental arcades). Teeth were segmented, and RSA was calculated. Dogs were grouped by body weight (<10 kg, 10-25 kg, >25 kg), and RSA ratios were compared between weight classes. All dental arcades (119/119, 100%) had a canine-to-combined PM4 + M1 RSA ratio (C:PM4 + M1) of less than 1. The canine-to-PM4 ratio was less than 1 in 61.35% of arcades, whereas the canine-to-M1 ratio was less than 1 in 1.68% of arcades, all in dogs weighing <10 kg. Body weight was positively associated with the C:PM4 + M1 ratio (P = .0006); however, the ratio remained less than 1 in all arcades. Small dogs had significantly lower C:PM4 + M1 ratios than medium (P = .006) and large dogs (P = .03). These findings support the use of the maxillary PM4 and M1 as a reinforced anchorage unit for orthodontic movement of the maxillary canine tooth in brachycephalic dogs.
