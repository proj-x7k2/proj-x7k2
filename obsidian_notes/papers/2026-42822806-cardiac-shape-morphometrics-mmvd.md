# Geometric morphometric analysis of cardiac shape changes associated with myxomatous mitral valve disease in Cavalier King Charles Spaniels.

**Veterinary journal (London, England : 1997) (2026)** · 후향적 연구, n=54 · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42822806/) · DOI: 10.1016/j.tvjl.2026.106901
관련 주제: [[topics/cardiac-imaging]]

## 국문 요약
Echocardiography와 right lateral thoracic radiography를 모두 시행한 Cavalier King Charles Spaniel(CKCS) 54마리(ACVIM stage A~D)의 흉부 방사선에서 10개 해부학적 landmark를 digitize해 2차원 geometric morphometric 분석(Generalized Procrustes Analysis, PCA, DFA, MANOVA, CVA)을 수행한 후향적 연구다. 상위 3개 principal component가 전체 shape variation의 78.68%를 설명했고, MANOVA·CVA·DFA 모두에서 stage 간 심장 silhouette 형태의 유의한 차이가 확인되었으며(특히 stage B2 vs D, B1 vs B2), vertebral heart score(VHS)·vertebral left atrial score(VLAS)도 stage 간 유의하게 달랐다. Shape 변화는 좌심방 경계의 비균질 확장, 심첨의 craniodorsal 이동, 진행성 globular remodeling으로 특징지어졌다.

## English summary
In this retrospective study of 54 Cavalier King Charles Spaniels (CKCS, ACVIM stages A-D) who underwent both echocardiography and right lateral thoracic radiography, two-dimensional geometric morphometric analysis (Generalized Procrustes Analysis, PCA, DFA, MANOVA, CVA) was performed on 10 anatomically homologous landmarks digitized from thoracic radiographs. The first three principal components explained 78.68% of total shape variation, and MANOVA, CVA, and DFA all showed significant intergroup shape differences (most pronounced between stages B2 and D, and between B1 and B2), with vertebral heart score (VHS) and vertebral left atrial score (VLAS) also differing significantly across stages. Shape changes were characterized by non-homogeneous expansion of the left atrial border, craniodorsal displacement of the cardiac apex, and progressive globular remodeling.

## 임상 시사점
VHS·VLAS 같은 전통적 선형 지표에 더해 심장 silhouette의 형태 자체(좌심방 경계 확장, 심첨 이동, globular remodeling)를 살펴보면, 특히 치료 시작 시점이 갈리는 B1→B2 전환에서 방사선만으로도 추가적인 단서를 얻을 수 있을 가능성이 있다. 다만 이 기법은 아직 landmark digitizing 등 특수 소프트웨어가 필요해 일상 진료에 바로 적용하기는 어렵다.

## 연구 설계와 한계
CKCS 54마리(stage A 9, B1 11, B2 10, C 12, D 12)를 대상으로 echocardiography로 ACVIM stage를 분류하고 right lateral thoracic radiography에서 geometric morphometric 분석을 수행한 단일 견종·단일기관 후향적 연구다. 견종이 CKCS로 한정되어 다른 견종에 일반화할 수 있는지 알 수 없고, 형태 변화가 실제 진단 민감도·특이도 개선으로 이어지는지는 평가하지 않았다.

## 원문 초록
Myxomatous mitral valve disease (MMVD) is a progressive cardiac disorder in Cavalier King Charles Spaniels (CKCS), characterized not only by cardiac enlargement but also by complex morphological remodeling of the cardiac silhouette. This study aimed to evaluate stage-dependent configurational changes in heart shape across the American College of Veterinary Internal Medicine (ACVIM) stages (A-D) using two-dimensional geometric morphometric analysis of thoracic radiographs. A total of 54 CKCS dogs (Stage A: n = 9, Stage B1: n = 11, Stage B2: n = 10, Stage C: n = 12, Stage D: n = 12) that underwent both echocardiography and right lateral thoracic radiography were included. Shape data were obtained from ten anatomically homologous landmarks digitized using TpsDig software. Following Generalized Procrustes Analysis in MorphoJ, principal component analysis (PCA), discriminant function analysis (DFA), multivariate analysis of variance using PAST, and canonical variate analysis (CVA) were performed to assess intergroup shape differences. Vertebral heart score (VHS), vertebral left atrial score (VLAS), age, and body weight were compared among ACVIM-classified MMVD groups using the Kruskal-Wallis and Dunn post-hoc tests. The first three principal components explained 78.68% of total shape variation (PC1: 32.55%, PC2: 26.86%, PC3: 19.27%), indicating substantial non-uniform remodeling of the cardiac silhouette. Multivariate analyses demonstrated statistically significant shape differences among groups, confirmed by MANOVA (Wilks'λ=0.673, p < 0.05) and CVA (Goodall's F=6.99, p < 0.001). DFA revealed robust discrimination between disease stages (Hotelling's T²=199.89, p < 0.05; permutation test p < 0.001), with the most pronounced differences observed between stages B2 and D (p < 0.001), as well as between preclinical stages B1 and B2 (p < 0.05). Furthermore, both VHS (H=45.09, p < 0.001) and VLAS (H=39.98, p < 0.001) significantly differed across stages, with VLAS showing a significant distinction between preclinical stages B1 and B2 (p < 0.05). Shape changes were characterized by non-homogeneous expansion of the left atrial border, craniodorsal displacement of the cardiac apex, and progressive globular remodeling of the heart silhouette. These findings demonstrate that radiographic geometric morphometrics provide a sensitive and objective framework for identifying clinically relevant morphological transitions in MMVD, particularly at the critical therapeutic decision threshold between ACVIM stages B1 and B2. Geometric morphometric analysis may therefore serve as a valuable complementary tool in the radiographic assessment and staging of MMVD in CKCS dogs.
