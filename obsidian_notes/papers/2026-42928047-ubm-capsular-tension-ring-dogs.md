# Ultrasound Biomicroscopy Analysis of Ciliary Cleft, Ciliary Body, and Ciliary Muscle Changes Following Capsular Tension Ring Implantation in Dogs.

**Veterinary ophthalmology (2026)** · 전향적 연구, n=23 eyes (16 dogs) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42928047/) · DOI: 10.1111/vop.70284
관련 주제: [[topics/head-neck-imaging]]

## 국문 요약
Phacoemulsification 후 IOL만 삽입한 군(11 eyes)과 capsular tension ring(CTR)을 병용한 군(12 eyes)을 ultrasound biomicroscopy(UBM)로 술 전, 술 후 2-4주, 3개월 이후에 평가했다. CTR군은 IOL군보다 ciliary cleft가 작았고, ciliary body가 corneoscleral 방향으로 전방 이동·압박되었으며 깊은 층의 ciliary muscle이 얇아졌고, 이 차이는 장기 추적에서도 유지되었다. CTR에 의한 ciliary body 압박은 12 eyes 중 5 eyes에서 관찰되었으나 IOP는 군 간 차이가 없었다.

## English summary
In 23 eyes of 16 dogs undergoing phacoemulsification with IOL alone or with a capsular tension ring, UBM was performed preoperatively and at 2-4 weeks and >3 months. CTR implantation was associated with a smaller ciliary cleft, anterior displacement and compression of the ciliary body, and thinning of deeper ciliary muscle layers, persisting long term. Ciliary body compression occurred in 5 of 12 CTR eyes; IOP did not differ between groups.

## 임상 시사점
CTR 삽입 후 UBM에서 ciliary cleft 감소와 ciliary body 압박이 보이더라도 이 연구 추적 기간에는 IOP 상승이나 임상적 부작용과 연결되지 않았으므로, 그 자체를 이상 소견으로 단정하지 말고 해석해야 한다. UBM은 안과 전문 장비 영역이라 일반 영상의학 진료에 직접 적용되는 범위는 제한적이다.

## 연구 설계와 한계
전향적 관찰 코호트로 군당 11-12 eyes이며 무작위 배정이 아니고, 4마리는 양쪽 군에 각각 한 눈씩 기여해 눈 간 독립성 문제가 있을 수 있다. 추적 기간 중 임상 부작용이 없었다고 했으나 장기 녹내장 위험은 평가할 수 없다.

## 원문 초록
OBJECTIVE: To evaluate the influence of capsular tension ring (CTR) implantation during phacoemulsification on ciliary cleft, ciliary body, and ciliary muscle morphology in dogs.

METHODS: This prospective observational cohort study included 23 eyes from 16 dogs that underwent phacoemulsification with IOL implantation alone (11 eyes/9 dogs) or combined with CTR implantation (12 eyes/11 dogs). Four dogs contributed one eye to each group. Ultrasound biomicroscopy (UBM) was performed preoperatively and at 2-4 weeks and more than 3 months postoperatively to evaluate the iridocorneal angle, ciliary cleft, ciliary body, and ciliary muscle.

RESULTS: No preoperative differences were identified between groups. Compared with the IOL group, CTR implantation was associated with a smaller ciliary cleft, anterior displacement and compression of the ciliary body toward the corneoscleral region and thinning of the deeper ciliary muscle layers during the postoperative period. These morphological differences remained consistent at the long-term follow-up. Ciliary body compression by the CTR was observed in 5 of 12 eyes. Intraocular pressure (IOP) did not differ between groups.

CONCLUSIONS: CTR implantation was associated with measurable morphological changes in the ciliary cleft, ciliary body, and ciliary muscle without affecting IOP. Although these changes were statistically significant, they were not associated with detectable adverse clinical effects during the follow-up period.
