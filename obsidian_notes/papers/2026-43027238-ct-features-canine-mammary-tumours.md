# Computed Tomography Features of Canine Mammary Tumours: A Retrospective Multicentre Study.

**Animals (2026)** · 후향적 연구, n=34 (종양 106개) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43027238/) · DOI: 없음(원자료의 DOI가 논문과 맞지 않아 비워 둠)

## 국문 요약
조직학적으로 확진된 mammary tumour 106개(악성 81, 양성 25)를 가진 암컷 개 34마리의 pre-/post-contrast CT를 평가해, modified BI-RADS lexicon 기반 표준 CT 판독 프로토콜과 악성 연관 소견을 분석한 다기관 후향적 연구이다. Heterogeneous·rim enhancement pattern이 악성과 강하게 연관되었고(p<0.001; 민감도 88.9%, 특이도 40%), perilesional oedema는 특이도 84%·민감도 33.3%였으며 종양 용적 >1.90 cm³는 악성과 유의하게 연관되었다(p=0.046). 0-4점 누적 점수의 AUC는 0.711이었고, 개체 내 군집을 보정한 뒤에도 score ≥2 high-risk 분류는 악성과 유의하게 연관되었다(OR 1.70; 95% CI 1.11-2.58; p=0.014; 민감도 82.7%, 특이도 58%).

## English summary
This multicentre retrospective study evaluated pre- and post-contrast CT of 106 histologically confirmed mammary tumours (81 malignant, 25 benign) in 34 female dogs, proposing a modified BI-RADS-based reporting protocol. Heterogeneous and rim enhancement were strongly associated with malignancy (sensitivity 88.9%, specificity 40%), perilesional oedema was specific (84%) but insensitive (33.3%), and volume >1.90 cm³ was associated with malignancy. The 0-4 point score had an AUC of 0.711; after accounting for within-dog clustering, score ≥2 remained associated with malignancy (OR 1.70; 95% CI 1.11-2.58).

## 임상 시사점
유선 종양 병기 CT를 찍을 때 enhancement pattern, perilesional oedema, 용적을 표준 항목으로 기록해 두면 판독 일관성에 도움이 된다. 다만 특이도가 낮고(점수 ≥2에서 58%) AUC도 0.711에 그쳐, CT 소견만으로 양성·악성을 가르지 말고 cytology/histology를 대체하지 않는 보조 정보로 써야 한다.

## 연구 설계와 한계
34마리·종양 106개의 후향적 다기관 연구로, 악성 비율이 높아(81/106) 양성 대조가 적다. 점수 체계는 같은 데이터에서 도출되어 외부 검증이 없으며, 판독자 간 일치도는 초록에 제시되지 않았다.

## 원문 초록
Mammary tumours are among the most common neoplastic disorders in female dogs, and although computed tomography (CT) is widely used for oncological staging, CT evaluation of primary mammary tumours remains relatively underexplored. This multicentre retrospective study aimed to develop a standardised CT reporting protocol using a modified BI-RADS™ lexicon and to evaluate the qualitative and quantitative features associated with malignancy. Pre- and post-contrast CT scans were evaluated for 34 female dogs with 106 histologically confirmed mammary tumours, of which 81 were malignant and 25 were benign. The heterogeneous and rim enhancement patterns were strongly associated with malignant lesions (p < 0.001; 88.9% sensitivity, 40% specificity). Perilesional oedema exhibited high specificity (84%) but low sensitivity (33.3%). A tumour volume >1.90 cm3 was significantly associated with malignancy (p = 0.046). The proposed cumulative scoring system (0-4 points) showed an AUC of 0.711. After accounting for within-dog clustering, the dichotomous high-risk classification (score ≥ 2) remained significantly associated with malignancy (OR = 1.70; 95% CI: 1.11-2.58; p = 0.014). Tumours classified as high-risk (score ≥ 2) showed an 84.2% observed proportion of malignancy, with 82.7% sensitivity and 58% specificity. This CT-based protocol could help to standardise reporting and identify tumours with a higher risk of malignancy.
