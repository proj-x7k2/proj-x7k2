# Anatomical study of the thorax and abdomen of the domestic cat: Correlation of cross-sectional anatomy with contrast-enhanced computed tomography and 3D reconstruction.

**Research in veterinary science (2026)** · 실험 연구, n=10 · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42710191/) · DOI: 10.1016/j.rvsc.2026.106389
관련 주제: [[topics/abdominal-imaging]] · [[topics/thoracic-imaging]]

## 국문 요약
성체 고양이 10마리를 대상으로 흉복부 단면 해부와 조영증강 CT, 3차원(3D) 재구성 영상을 상호 대조해 상세한 해부학 atlas를 제작했다. 5마리는 정해진 흉복부 절단면에서 신선·고정 상태로 절편을 만들어 관찰했고, 나머지 5마리는 요오드 조영제 정맥주사 후 단순·조영증강 CT를 시행해 DICOM 데이터를 3D 재구성(흉곽 골격, 심장, 주요 혈관, 복부 장기)했다. 흉복부의 주요 골격·근육·장기·혈관 구조가 해부학적 절편과 CT 영상 간에 모두 식별·대조되었으며, 조영증강 CT는 특히 혈관 해부와 장기 간 관계의 시인성을 크게 향상시켰다. 저자들은 이 해부학·영상·3D 재구성 atlas가 수의학 해부 교육, 영상 판독, 수술 계획에 신뢰할 만한 참고자료가 될 것이라고 결론지었다.

## English summary
This anatomical study developed a detailed atlas of the feline thorax and abdomen in 10 adult domestic cats by correlating cross-sectional anatomical slices with contrast-enhanced CT images and three-dimensional (3D) reconstructions. Five cats were serially sectioned at defined thoracic and abdominal levels (examined fresh and fixed), while the remaining five underwent plain and contrast-enhanced CT after intravenous iodinated contrast administration, with CT datasets reconstructed in 3D (thoracic skeleton, heart, major vessels, abdominal viscera). All major bones, muscles, visceral organs, and vascular structures were successfully identified and correlated between anatomical sections and CT images, with contrast-enhanced CT markedly improving visualization of vascular anatomy and visceral relationships. The authors conclude this atlas provides a reliable reference for veterinary anatomy education, diagnostic imaging interpretation, and surgical planning in cats.

## 임상 시사점
고양이 흉복부 CT를 판독하거나 수술 계획을 세울 때, 이 연구의 단면-CT-3D 재구성 대조 atlas를 정상 해부 기준 참고자료로 활용할 수 있다.

## 연구 설계와 한계
성체 고양이 10마리(절편용 5마리, CT용 5마리)를 이용한 해부학·영상 상호 대조 연구로, 임상 질환군이 아닌 정상 해부 기준을 제시하는 데 목적이 있다. 절편 개체와 CT 개체가 서로 다른 5마리씩으로 나뉘어 동일 개체 내 직접 대조는 아니며, 병적 상태에서의 적용 가능성이나 품종·연령·체형에 따른 변이는 다루지 않았다.

## 원문 초록
Computed tomography (CT) has become an essential diagnostic and educational tool in veterinary medicine; however, accurate interpretation relies on precise knowledge of normal cross-sectional anatomy. This study aimed to develop a detailed anatomical atlas of the feline thorax and abdomen by correlating cross-sectional anatomical slices with contrast-enhanced CT images and three-dimensional (3D) reconstructions. Ten adult domestic cats of both sexes were used. Five cats were serially sectioned at defined thoracic and abdominal levels, and the sections were examined in both fresh and fixed states. The remaining five cats underwent plain and contrast-enhanced CT examinations following intravenous administration of iodinated contrast medium. CT datasets were converted to DICOM format and used to generate 3D reconstructions of the thoracic skeleton, heart, major vessels, and abdominal viscera. All major bones, muscles, visceral organs, and vascular structures of the thorax and abdomen were successfully identified and correlated between anatomical sections and CT images. Contrast-enhanced CT markedly improved visualization of vascular anatomy and visceral relationships. The reconstructed anatomical, radiological, and 3D reconstruction atlas provides a reliable reference for veterinary anatomy education, diagnostic imaging interpretation, and surgical planning in cats.
