# Hydrocolpos in a mixed-breed dog: a case report.

**Frontiers in veterinary science (2026)** · 증례보고, n=1 · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42757221/) · DOI: 10.25107/2474-1655.2223
관련 주제: [[topics/urogenital-imaging]]

## 국문 요약
난산으로 중성화한 지 6개월 된 3.5세 잡종견이 지속적 stranguria로 내원했다. Plain abdominal radiograph에서 방광을 두측으로 밀어내고 하행결장을 압박하는 intrapelvic soft-tissue attenuating mass가 확인되었으나 복부 초음파는 결론을 내리지 못했다. CT에서 요도와 하행결장을 압박하는 tubular, hypodense structure로 병변이 확인되어 imperforate hymen, segmental vaginal aplasia, 또는 vestibulovaginal disorder에 의한 hydrocolpos로 판단했다. Vaginoscopy에서 vaginal atresia와 ostium urethrae externum을 가리는 stem-shaped mass가 확인되었고, retrograde cystography에서 폐쇄부 뒤로 현저히 확장된 요도가 관찰되었다. 최소침습적 polyp 절제·요도 확장술 후 2개월간 정상 배뇨를 보였으나 재발해, episiotomy와 hydrocolpos-vaginal mucosa 간 stoma 형성으로 영구적으로 증상이 해소되었다. 저자들은 병력상 외상성 기원의 이차성 vaginal atresia로 인한 hydrocolpos의 첫 증례로 추정했다.

## English summary
A 3.5-year-old, 5.2-kg mixed-breed dog presented with persistent stranguria 6 months after neutering for dystocia. Plain abdominal radiographs showed an intrapelvic soft-tissue attenuating mass displacing the bladder cranially and constricting the descending colon; abdominal ultrasonography was inconclusive. CT revealed the mass as a tubular, hypodense structure compressing the urethra and descending colon, consistent with hydrocolpos from an imperforate hymen, segmental vaginal aplasia, or vestibulovaginal disorder. Vaginoscopy showed vaginal atresia with a stem-shaped mass obscuring the external urethral orifice, and retrograde cystography demonstrated a markedly dilated urethra behind the obstruction. Minimally invasive polyp resection and urethral widening gave 2 months of normal micturition before recurrence; episiotomy with evacuation of the hydrocolpos and creation of a stoma between the hydrocolpos mucosa and vaginal mucosa achieved permanent resolution. The authors propose this as the first reported case of secondary, trauma-induced vaginal atresia and resulting hydrocolpos.

## 임상 시사점
중성화·난산 병력이 있는 암컷에서 지속적 stranguria와 골반강 내 관상(tubular) 연부조직 병변이 보일 때, 초음파만으로 결론이 나지 않으면 CT로 병변의 관상 구조와 요도·결장 압박 관계를 확인하고 vaginoscopy·retrograde cystography로 폐쇄 부위를 국소화하는 단계적 접근이 진단에 도움이 될 수 있다.

## 연구 설계와 한계
단일 증례보고로, 진단 과정(방사선·초음파·CT·vaginoscopy·retrograde cystography)과 2단계 수술적 치료·경과가 상세히 기술되었으나 단일 증례이므로 일반화에는 한계가 있다. 병인(외상성 vaginal atresia)은 병력에 근거한 추정이며 조직학적으로 직접 입증되지는 않았다.

## 원문 초록
A 3.5-year-old, 5.2-kg mixed-breed dog was presented with persistent stranguria after being neutered due to dystocia 6 months ago. Plain abdominal radiographs revealed an intrapelvic soft-tissue attenuating mass that displaced the urinary bladder cranially and constricted the descending colon. Abdominal ultrasonography yielded inconclusive results. Computed tomography revealed the mass as a tubular, hypodense structure compressing the urethra and the descending colon. This finding was defined as hydrocolpos induced by an imperforate hymen, segmental vaginal aplasia, or a vestibulovaginal disorder. Vaginoscopy revealed vaginal atresia and a stem-shaped mass obscuring the ostium urethrae externum. Retrograde cystography showed a significantly dilated urethra behind the obscured ostium urethrae externum. A pedunculated mass (polyp) was suspected to hinder the urinary outflow. A minimal invasive resection of the polyp and widening of the urethra were performed. Normal micturition was observed for 2 months. In the 9th week, the patient returned with tenesmus. An invasive procedure involving episiotomy, evacuation of the hydrocolpos, and creation of a stoma between the mucous membrane of the hydrocolpos and the vaginal mucosa led to permanent resolution of the clinical signs. Only a few case reports exist regarding congenital hydrocolpos. As the history indicates, a traumatic origin is considered likely; therefore, we assume that this is the first case report of secondary, trauma-induced vaginal atresia, and the resulting hydrocolpos.
