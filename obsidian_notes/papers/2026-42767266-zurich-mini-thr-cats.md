# Zurich Mini Cementless Total Hip Replacement in Cats: Specific Considerations and Short-Term Outcomes in 11 Cats (17 Hips).

**Veterinary and comparative orthopaedics and traumatology : V.C.O.T (2026)** · 후향적 연구, n=11 (17 hips) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42767266/) · DOI: 10.1055/a-2956-0882
관련 주제: [[topics/musculoskeletal-imaging]]

## 국문 요약
고양이는 acetabular retroversion 제한, femoral canal-acetabulum 크기 불일치, slipped capital femoral epiphysis(SCFE)에 따른 sclerosis 등 종 특이적 해부 특성 때문에 total hip replacement(THR)가 드물게 시행된다. 이 후향적 연구는 2022~2025년 Maine Coon 9마리·Domestic Shorthair 2마리(총 11마리, 17 hips)에서 시행한 cementless Zurich Mini THR의 임상·방사선 결과를 보고했다. 적응증은 SCFE 11례, hip dysplasia 5례, femoral neck fracture malunion 1례였다. Intraoperative fluoroscopy로 cup 위치를 최적화했으며, 수술 중 경미한 cortical fissure 2례는 예방적 cerclage wire로 안정화했다. 수술 후 femoral fracture 2례(11.8%, 그중 1례는 절단으로 이어짐)와 수술과 무관한 것으로 판단된 외상성 고관절 탈구 1례(10개월 후)가 발생했다. 8주째 남은 임플란트는 모두 통증 없이 안정적이었고 방사선상 완전~진행성 osteointegration을 보였다.

## English summary
Total hip replacement (THR) in cats is uncommon due to species-specific anatomical features including limited acetabular retroversion, femoral canal-acetabulum size mismatch, and sclerosis secondary to slipped capital femoral epiphysis (SCFE). This retrospective study reports clinical and radiographic outcomes of 17 cementless Zurich Mini THR procedures in 11 cats (9 Maine Coons, 2 Domestic Shorthairs) performed 2022-2025, for SCFE (n=11), hip dysplasia (n=5), and femoral neck fracture malunion (n=1). Intraoperative fluoroscopy was used to optimize cup positioning. Two intraoperative cortical fissures were stabilized with prophylactic cerclage wires. Postoperative complications included two femoral fractures (11.8%, one resulting in amputation) and one traumatic hip luxation at 10 months, deemed unrelated to surgery. At 8 weeks, all remaining implants were pain-free, stable, and showed complete or advanced osteointegration on radiographs.

## 임상 시사점
SCFE나 hip dysplasia가 있는 고양이에서 THR을 고려할 때 intraoperative fluoroscopy로 cup 위치를 확인하는 것이 유용하며, 술후 방사선 추적에서 8주 시점 osteointegration 진행 여부를 통해 임플란트 안정성을 판단할 수 있다. 다만 femoral fracture(11.8%)가 드물지 않은 합병증으로 술후 방사선 평가 시 주의가 필요하다.

## 연구 설계와 한계
단일 술자(저자 1인)의 11마리(17 hips) 후향적 증례군으로, 대조군이나 장기(8주 이후) 추적 결과는 제시되지 않았다. Maine Coon에 편중된 품종 분포(9/11)로 다른 품종·체형에 대한 일반화는 제한적이며, 단기 결과만을 다루므로 장기 임플란트 생존율은 알 수 없다.

## 원문 초록
Total hip replacement (THR) in cats remains uncommon due to species-specific anatomical features, including limited acetabular retroversion, mismatch between femoral canal and acetabular size, and technical difficulty during femoral preparation related to sclerosis secondary to slipped capital femoral epiphysis (SCFE), the main indication in this species. These factors complicate implant selection, canal preparation, and cup orientation. This retrospective study reports clinical and radiographic outcomes of 17 cementless Mini THR procedures performed in 11 cats (nine Maine Coons and two Domestic Shorthairs) between 2022 and 2025. Indications included SCFE (n = 11), hip dysplasia (n = 5), and femoral neck fracture malunion (n = 1). Intraoperative fluoroscopy was used to optimize cup positioning. Two intraoperative complications occurred, consisting of minimal cortical fissures stabilized with prophylactic cerclage wires. Postoperative complications included two femoral fractures (11.8%), one resulting in limb amputation. One traumatic hip luxation occurred 10 months after surgery and was considered unrelated to the procedure. At 8 weeks, all remaining implants were pain-free, stable, and showed complete or advanced osteointegration. With careful planning, intraoperative imaging, and appropriate postoperative management, THR can provide excellent short-term outcomes in cats, although limited implant modularity may restrict optimal fit in some patients.
