# Case Report: Distal tibial physeal fracture secondary to septic physitis and osteomyelitis in a Boxer puppy.

**Frontiers in veterinary science (2026)** · 증례보고, n=1 · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42718565/) · DOI: 10.1038/s41572-026-00707-9
관련 주제: [[topics/musculoskeletal-imaging]]

## 국문 요약
19주령 Boxer 강아지가 급성 비체중부하 파행과 원위 사지 부종을 주소로 내원했다. 초기 정면·측면 tibia 방사선에서 원위 tibial physis의 불규칙한 확장과 심한 주변 연부조직 부종이 확인되어 septic physitis에 합당한 소견으로 판단했고, 15시간 후 재촬영한 방사선에서 병적 골절로 추정되는 Salter-Harris type I 골절이 새로 확인되었다. 수술적 탐색에서 subperiosteal abscess가 발견되어 배농·배양을 시행했고 Pasteurella spp.가 동정되어 항생제 치료를 받았으며, 수술 후 14주째 부종 소실, 방사선학적 골유합, 정상 사지 기능이 확인되었다. 이는 개에서 septic physitis에 의한 사지 physeal fracture가 보고된 최초 증례다.

## English summary
A 19-week-old Boxer puppy presented with acute non-weight-bearing lameness and distal limb swelling. Initial orthogonal tibial radiographs showed irregular widening of the distal tibial physis with severe surrounding soft-tissue edema, consistent with septic physitis; repeat radiographs 15 hours later revealed a new, presumed pathologic Salter-Harris type I fracture of the distal tibial physis. Surgical exploration revealed a subperiosteal abscess, which was drained and cultured (Pasteurella spp. isolated), and the fracture was stabilized with cross-pin fixation. Resolution of swelling, radiographic healing, and normal limb function were achieved by 14 weeks postoperative. This is the first documented case of an appendicular physeal fracture secondary to suspected septic physitis in a dog.

## 임상 시사점
강아지에서 급성 비체중부하 파행과 사지 부종이 있을 때 physis widening 소견이 짧은 시간 내 병적 골절로 진행할 수 있으므로, 초기 방사선에서 physitis가 의심되면 단기간(수 시간~하루 내) 재촬영을 고려하고 septic physitis를 감별진단에 포함해야 한다.

## 연구 설계와 한계
단일 증례보고로, 소견의 일반화 가능성은 제한적이다. 저자들도 이 병태가 망아지(foal)에서는 보고된 바 있으나 강아지에서는 처음 보고되는 것이라고 명시했으며, 유사 증례의 발생 빈도나 위험 요인에 대한 정보는 이 보고만으로 알 수 없다.

## 원문 초록
Salter-Harris fracture secondary to septic physitis has been reported in foals but not puppies. In this case, a 19-week-old, 13.4 kg male intact Boxer puppy was presented with a 2-day history of acute non-weight-bearing lameness of the left pelvic limb, accompanied by distal limb swelling and lethargy. Orthogonal tibial radiographs were performed, and irregular widening of the distal tibial physis with severe edema of the surrounding soft tissues consistent with septic physitis were identified. Fifteen hours later, distal tibial radiographs were repeated, and a Salter-Harris type I fracture of the distal tibial physis, presumed to be pathologic in origin, was diagnosed. Surgical exploration revealed a subperiosteal abscess, which was drained and cultured. The physeal fracture was stabilized with cross-pin fixation, which was reinforced with a splint and bandage for the duration of recovery. Pasteurella spp. was isolated, and amoxicillin-clavulanate was prescribed for 72 days. Resolution of swelling, radiographic fracture healing, and return to normal limb function were achieved after 14 weeks postoperative, at which time the pins were removed and submitted for aerobic and anaerobic culture and susceptibility. No bacterial isolates were identified. This is the first documented case of an appendicular physeal fracture secondary to suspected septic physitis in a dog, and septic physitis should be considered a differential for puppies with spontaneous Salter-Harris fracture.
