# EXPRESS:Management of Distal Hindlimb Injuries in Cats Using an Exvet External Skeletal Fixation System: A Multicenter Retrospective Study.

**Journal of feline medicine and surgery (2026)** · 후향적 연구, n=35 · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42934855/) · DOI: 10.1177/1098612X261502918
관련 주제: [[topics/musculoskeletal-imaging]]

## 국문 요약
4개 기관에서 Exvet Tarsal Fixator(ETF)로 치료한 고양이 distal hindlimb injury를 후향적으로 분석했다. 평가한 73마리 중 35마리(36 injuries)가 포함되었고, 24마리(68.6%)는 closed reduction, 11마리(31.4%)는 limited open 방식이었다. 32/35(91.4%)가 수술 직후 weight bearing을 시작했고, 합병증은 7/35(20.0%; minor 5, major 2)였다. 평균 34일(중앙값 28일)에 fixator를 제거했을 때 25/35(71.4%)가 full functional outcome, 10/35(28.6%)가 acceptable outcome이었다.

## English summary
In this four-centre retrospective study, 35 of 73 cats with distal hindlimb injuries treated with the Exvet Tarsal Fixator met inclusion criteria; 68.6% were closed reductions. 91.4% bore weight immediately, complications occurred in 20.0% (5 minor, 2 major), and at fixator removal (mean 34 days) 71.4% had a full and 28.6% an acceptable functional outcome. The authors support safe and effective use of the ETF.

## 임상 시사점
영상 자체보다 수술 결과에 초점을 둔 연구이지만, closed reduction 비율이 높으므로 술중 fluoroscopy/방사선으로 정복을 확인하는 워크플로우가 중요함을 시사한다. 방사선 소견의 구체적 결과는 초록에 제시되지 않았다.

## 연구 설계와 한계
대조군이 없는 후향적 증례 분석이며 73마리 중 35마리만 포함되어 선택 편향이 가능하다. 기능적 결과가 주관적 분류(full vs acceptable)이고 방사선학적 union 평가의 세부 수치가 초록에 없다. 영상이 핵심 평가변수는 아니어서 근거는 제한적이다.

## 원문 초록
OBJECTIVE: To evaluate the clinical use of an Exvet external skeletal fixation (ESF) system applied using minimally invasive techniques for the management of distal hindlimb injuries in cats.

METHODS: Cats with distal hindlimb injuries treated using the Exvet Tarsal Fixator (ETF) at four institutions between September 2022 and January 2026 were included. Data collected included fracture type, surgical approach, postoperative complications and additional treatments, time to first limb use, time to fixator removal, radiographic findings, and clinical outcome.

RESULTS: Of 73 cats evaluated, 35 cats with 36 distal hindlimb injuries met the inclusion criteria. Twenty-four cats (68.6%) were managed with a closed reduction, while 11 cats (31.4%) were managed with a limited open surgical fashion. Thirty-two of 35 cats (91.4%) started weight bearing immediately after surgery. Complications were recorded in 7 of 35 cases (20.0%), of which 5 cases (14.3%) were classified as minor and 2 cases (5.7%) as major complications. At a mean ESF removal time of 34 days (median: 28 days; range: 21-56 days), a full functional outcome was achieved in 25 of 35 cases (71.4%), while 10 cases (28.6%) showed an acceptable functional outcome.

CONCLUSION AND RELEVANCE: The ETF is an anatomically designed ESF implant specifically developed for feline distal hindlimb injuries. The findings of this multicenter retrospective study support its safe and effective use for the management of feline distal hindlimb injuries.
