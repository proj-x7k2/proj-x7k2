# Assessment of radiographic healing after tibial plateau leveling osteotomy in young and older dogs using the modified RUST scoring system.

**Frontiers in veterinary science (2026)** · 후향적 연구, n=100 · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42903056/) · DOI: 10.3389/fvets.2025.1615947
관련 주제: [[topics/musculoskeletal-imaging]], [[topics/ai-imaging]]

## 국문 요약
체중 20-40kg, 편측 TPLO를 받은 2-5세(Group 1, n=49)와 8-12세(Group 2, n=51) 개의 수술 8주 후 방사선 사진을 large language model(LLM), 외과의, 외과 resident가 독립적으로 4-point modified RUST(mRUST) 체계로 채점해 연령군 간 차이를 비교했다. mRUST 점수는 세 평가자 모두에서 연령군 간 차이가 없었다(LLM p=0.352, 외과의 p=0.908, resident p=0.588). 평가자 간 일치도는 ICC 0.4504로 fair 수준에 그쳤다.

## English summary
Dogs weighing 20–40 kg that underwent unilateral TPLO at 2–5 years (n=49) or 8–12 years (n=51) were retrospectively identified, and 8-week postoperative radiographs were scored with the 4-point modified RUST system by a large language model, a surgeon and a surgical resident. Scores did not differ between age groups for any rater (LLM p=0.352, surgeon p=0.908, resident p=0.588). Interrater agreement was only fair (ICC 0.4504). The authors conclude that chronological age was not associated with mRUST score at 8 weeks and that LLM scoring shows promise but needs refinement.

## 임상 시사점
8주차 방사선상 골치유 정도는 2-5세와 8-12세 TPLO 개 사이에서 mRUST로 차이가 없었으므로, 연령만을 이유로 고령견의 osteotomy 치유가 늦을 것이라 가정하거나 운동 제한을 달리할 근거는 이 연구에서 나오지 않았다. 평가자 간 일치도가 fair 수준이었고 LLM 채점은 아직 임상 사용에 이르지 못했으므로 mRUST 점수는 동일 평가자가 일관되게 적용해야 한다.

## 연구 설계와 한계
2023-2025년 TPLO를 받은 개를 후향적으로 선별했으며 endocrinopathy나 비전형적 TPLO·회복 증례는 제외했다. 평가 시점이 8주 한 번뿐이어서 이후 치유 경과나 임상 기능 결과는 알 수 없고, 평가자 간 일치도가 낮아 점수 자체의 신뢰도에 한계가 있다. 초록에 사용한 LLM의 종류와 프롬프트 방식은 제시되지 않았다.

## 원문 초록
OBJECTIVE: To compare radiographic osteotomy healing scores after tibial plateau leveling osteotomy (TPLO) in young (2-5 years) and old (8-12 years) dogs using the modified Radiographic Union Score for Tibial fractures (mRUST) system, and to evaluate agreement between large language model-assisted interpretation (LLM), surgeon, and surgical resident scoring.

STUDY DESIGN: Client-owned, 20-40 kg dogs treated with unilateral TPLO at ages 2-5 or 8-12 years between 2023 and 2025 were retrospectively identified. Dogs with endocrinopathy or atypical TPLO or recovery were excluded. Dogs were assigned to Group 1 (2-5 years) or Group 2 (8-12 years). 8-week postoperative radiographs were scored independently by an LLM, a surgeon, and a surgical resident using the 4-point mRUST system. Scores were compared between age groups using the Wilcoxon rank-sum test, and interrater agreement was assessed using an intraclass correlation coefficient (ICC).

RESULTS: Group 1 had 49 dogs; Group 2 had 51 dogs. Group 1 had a mean (SD) age of 3.82 (1.11) years, while Group 2 had a mean (SD) age of 9.24 (0.99) years. mRUST scores did not differ between age groups across LLM (p = 0.352), surgeon (p = 0.908), or resident assessments (p = 0.588). LLM, surgeon, and resident scores had only fair agreement (ICC = 0.4504, p < 0.001).

CONCLUSION: Chronological age was not associated with mRUST score following TPLO at the 8-week postoperative evaluation. The mRUST system demonstrated moderate agreement between human observers. LLM scoring showed promise; further refinement is needed for clinical application.
