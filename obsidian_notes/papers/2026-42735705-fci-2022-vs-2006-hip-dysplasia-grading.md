# The updated 2022 Fédération Cynologique Internationale method may lead to changes in hip dysplasia grading in some dogs.

**American journal of veterinary research (2026)** · 후향적 연구, n=1318 (Australian Shepherd 1020, Boxer 298) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42735705/) · DOI: 10.2460/ajvr.26.06.0260
관련 주제: [[topics/musculoskeletal-imaging]]

## 국문 요약
2023년 3월~2024년 9월 canine hip dysplasia(HD) 스크리닝을 위해 제출된 방사선 중 FCI 품질 기준을 충족한 Australian Shepherd 1,020마리와 Boxer 298마리의 방사선을 Fédération Cynologique Internationale(FCI) 2022년 방법(FCI22)과 2006년 방법(FCI06) 양쪽으로 재평가해 Norberg angle(NA)과 HD 등급을 비교했다. FCI22로 측정한 평균 NA는 FCI06보다 0.7도 낮았고, 전체 2,636개 고관절 중 68.3%(1,801개)는 등급이 그대로 유지되었다. Australian Shepherd에서는 17.2%가 상향, 12.6%가 하향 조정된 반면 Boxer에서는 8.9%가 상향, 29.4%가 하향 조정되어 품종 간 차이가 뚜렷했으며, 재분류는 주로 2006년 기준 D등급이었던 고관절(82.4%)에서 발생했다. 저자들은 FCI22가 두 품종에서 대부분의 HD 등급을 실질적으로 바꾸지는 않지만, 경계값 근처의 고관절과 품종별 패턴이 개별 스크리닝 결과에 영향을 줄 수 있다고 결론지었다.

## English summary
This retrospective observational study compared Norberg angle (NA) measurements and hip dysplasia (HD) grades obtained from the same radiographs using both the FCI 2022 (FCI22) and FCI 2006 (FCI06) methods in 1,020 Australian Shepherds and 298 Boxers screened between March 2023 and September 2024. Mean NA22 was 0.7° lower than NA06, and 68.3% (1,801/2,636) of hips retained the same HD grade. In Australian Shepherds, 17.2% of hips were upgraded and 12.6% downgraded; in Boxers, 8.9% were upgraded and 29.4% downgraded, with reclassification occurring primarily among hips initially graded D under FCI06 (82.4%). The authors conclude FCI22 is unlikely to substantially alter HD grades overall, but borderline NA values and breed-specific patterns may affect individual screening outcomes.

## 임상 시사점
HD 스크리닝에 FCI22 기준을 도입할 경우, 특히 Boxer처럼 등급 변화 폭이 큰 품종에서는 기존 FCI06 결과와 직접 비교하기 어려울 수 있다는 점을 보호자와 번식 상담 시 설명할 필요가 있다.

## 연구 설계와 한계
동일 방사선을 두 가지 채점법으로 재평가한 후향적 관찰 연구로, Australian Shepherd와 Boxer 두 품종에 국한되어 다른 품종에는 일반화하기 어렵다. FCI 품질 기준을 충족한 방사선만 포함했으므로 품질 미달 영상에서의 재현성은 다루지 않았고, 판독자 간 일치도(interobserver reliability)에 대한 정보는 초록에 제시되지 않았다.

## 원문 초록
OBJECTIVE: To compare Norberg angle (NA) measurements and hip dysplasia (HD) grades obtained from the same radiographs using the Fédération Cynologique Internationale (FCI) 2022 (FCI22) and FCI 2006 methods (FCI06).

METHODS: This retrospective observational study included radiographs submitted for canine HD screening from March 17, 2023, through September 27, 2024. Radiographs meeting FCI quality standards were evaluated under both methods, with NA measurements and HD grades recorded accordingly. Grade changes were classified as upgraded, downgraded, or unchanged.

RESULTS: Radiographs from 1,020 Australian Shepherds and 298 Boxers were reviewed. The mean Norberg angle measured according to the 2022 method (NA22) was 0.7° lower than that measured according to the 2006 method (NA06). Overall, 1,801 of 2,636 hips (68.3%) retained the same HD grade with the FCI22. In Australian Shepherds, 350 of 2,040 hips (17.2%) were upgraded, and 257 of 2,040 hips (12.6%) were downgraded. In Boxers, 53 of 596 hips (8.9%) were upgraded, and 175 of 596 hips (29.4%) were downgraded. Reclassification occurred primarily among hips initially graded D in 2006 (D06), with 286 of 347 hips (82.4%) reclassified.

CONCLUSIONS: Differences between NA22 and NA06 had limited impact on HD grading except for hips with near-threshold NA values. The FCI22 kept most HD grades unchanged. Across both breeds, upgrades occurred primarily among grade D hips. Breed differences in grade changes may reflect interbreed anatomical differences.

CLINICAL RELEVANCE: The FCI22 method is unlikely to substantially alter HD grades in these 2 breeds. Borderline hips and breed-specific grading patterns may affect individual screening outcomes.
