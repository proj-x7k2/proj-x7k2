# Gastrointestinal transit time of healthy dogs before and after enema assessed by contrast radiographs with barium-impregnated polyethylene spheres.

**Veterinary journal (London, England : 1997) (2026)** · 실험 연구, n=6 · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42849778/) · DOI: 10.1016/j.tvjl.2026.106924
관련 주제: [[topics/abdominal-imaging]]

## 국문 요약
건강한 개 6마리를 대상으로 barium-impregnated polyethylene sphere(BIPS)를 이용한 radiographic contrast 검사에서 enema 시행 전후의 위장관 통과시간을 비교한 전향적 실험 연구다. 12시간 금식 후 0~12시간 동안 연속 방사선 촬영으로 BIPS 위치를 추적해 gastric emptying time과 descending colon 도달 시간을 측정했으며, 같은 개체에서 BIPS 단독(BIPS군)과 BIPS+enema(BIPS+ENEMA군)를 비교했다. BIPS군은 H0~H1에 gastric emptying이 시작되어 H8 무렵 종료되었으나, BIPS+ENEMA군은 emptying이 H1 무렵 시작되어 H12 이후까지 지속되었고 검사 종료 시점 descending colon 내 sphere 비율도 더 낮았다(통계적 유의성은 없음). BIPS와 enema 모두 혈액검사·전해질·혈당에 이상을 일으키지 않았다. 저자들은 BIPS를 이용한 radiographic contrast 검사에서 enema가 필수 전처치는 아니라고 결론지었다.

## English summary
This prospective experimental study in six healthy dogs compared gastrointestinal transit before and after an enema using barium-impregnated polyethylene sphere (BIPS) contrast radiography. After a 12-hour fast, serial radiographs over 0-12 hours tracked BIPS location to calculate gastric emptying time and time to reach the descending colon, comparing BIPS alone versus BIPS plus enema in the same dogs. The BIPS-only group showed gastric emptying starting at H0-H1 and ending around H8, whereas the BIPS+enema group showed delayed emptying starting around H1 and extending beyond H12, with a lower percentage of spheres reaching the descending colon by the end of observation (differences not statistically significant). Neither BIPS nor enema caused laboratory abnormalities or hypoglycemia. The authors concluded that an enema is not a required pre-procedure step when using BIPS for radiographic contrast studies.

## 임상 시사점
개의 위장관 통과시간 평가를 위해 BIPS contrast radiography를 계획할 때, enema 전처치를 생략해도 검사가 가능하며 오히려 enema가 gastric emptying을 지연시킬 수 있으므로 검사 전 불필요한 enema를 피하는 것이 합리적이다.

## 연구 설계와 한계
건강한 개 6마리를 대상으로 같은 개체 내에서 BIPS 단독과 BIPS+enema를 비교한 전향적 실험 연구로, 표본이 매우 작아(n=6) 관찰된 gastric emptying 지연·descending colon 도달률 차이가 통계적으로 유의하지 않았다. 건강한 개 대상 연구이므로 위장관 질환이 있는 환자에서의 통과시간 양상은 이 연구만으로 알 수 없으며, enema의 종류·용량에 따른 효과 차이도 평가되지 않았다.

## 원문 초록
In this prospective experimental study, we aimed to assess the gastrointestinal (GI) transit of healthy dogs with and without an enema, using barium-impregnated polyethylene spheres (BIPS). Six healthy dogs underwent two experimental steps: evaluation of the GI transit using BIPS (BIPS group) before and after enema (BIPS+ENEMA group). All animals were fasted for 12 h, the transit was assessed in serial radiographs from 0 to 12 h, and BIPS were counted in each segment of the GI tract, calculating the gastric emptying time and the time the spheres reached the descending colon. Blood cell count, serum biochemistry, electrolytes, and blood glucose levels were assessed during the experiment to evaluate the safety of both BIPs and enema. BIPS were easily identified in all segments of the GI tract. The BIPS group presented gastric emptying starting at H0 and H1, ending around H8. BIPS + ENEMA group showed a delayed gastric emptying, which began around H1 and extended to more than H12, and had a lower percentage of spheres in the descending colon at the end of the evaluation; however, the differences were not statistically significant. BIPS and enema did not cause changes in laboratory evaluation, and the 12-hour fast did not cause hypoglycemia. Based on the findings, when using BIPS for radiographic contrast, an enema is not a required procedure. The use of the enema and BIPS can be considered safe, with no apparent adverse effects in dogs.
