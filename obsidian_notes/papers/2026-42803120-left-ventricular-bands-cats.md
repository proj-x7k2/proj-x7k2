# EXPRESS: Echocardiographic prevalence and attachment-site patterns of left ventricular bands in cats.

**Journal of feline medicine and surgery (2026)** · 전향적 연구, n=200 · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42803120/) · DOI: 10.1177/1098612X261495725
관련 주제: [[topics/cardiac-imaging]]

## 국문 요약
Left ventricular band(LVB)는 mitral valve leaflet에 붙지 않고 좌심실 내 두 심근 구조를 잇는 선상 구조물이다. Transthoracic echocardiography를 받은 고양이 200마리를 대상으로 한 전향적 관찰 연구에서 199/200마리(99.5%)에서 최소 1개 이상의 LVB가 확인되었고, 마리당 중앙값 4개(범위 0~10)였다. 총 884개의 LVB 중 51.9%가 interventricular septum에 부착부를 가졌고 94.3%는 papillary muscle 또는 accessory papillary muscle-like 구조와 연관되었다. LVB 개수는 성별(P=0.47), 진정 여부(P=0.26), 정상/국소 삽입부 비후군과 hypertrophic cardiomyopathy(HCM) 표현형군 간(P=0.98) 모두 유의한 차이가 없었다.

## English summary
Left ventricular bands (LVBs) are discrete intracavitary linear structures connecting two myocardial structures within the left ventricle without insertion into the mitral valve leaflets. In this prospective observational study of 200 cats undergoing transthoracic echocardiography, at least one LVB was detected in 199/200 cats (99.5% prevalence), with a median of 4 LVBs per cat (range 0-10). Of 884 total LVBs identified, 51.9% had at least one septal attachment and 94.3% involved a papillary muscle or accessory papillary muscle-like structure. LVB counts did not differ significantly by sex (P=0.47), sedation status (P=0.26), or between the normal/focal insertion-site thickening group and the hypertrophic cardiomyopathy (HCM) phenotype group (P=0.98).

## 임상 시사점
고양이 심초음파에서 좌심실 내 선상 구조물(LVB)은 거의 모든 개체에서 관찰되는 정상 변이이므로, mitral valve leaflet에 부착되지 않은 septal-papillary muscle 간 밴드를 우연히 발견했다고 해서 병적 소견이나 HCM 관련 소견으로 과대 해석하지 않도록 주의해야 한다.

## 연구 설계와 한계
고양이 200마리(정상/국소 삽입부 비후군 89마리, HCM 표현형군 86마리)를 대상으로 한 전향적 관찰 연구로, LVB의 유무·개수·부착부 양상을 사전 정의된 분류 기준에 따라 평가했다. 초록에는 관찰자 간 일치도나 LVB의 장기 예후적 의미는 제시되지 않았다.

## 원문 초록
ObjectivesTo determine the echocardiographic prevalence and attachment-site patterns of left ventricular bands (LVBs) in cats and assess whether LVB counts differed by sex, sedation status, or echocardiographic group.MethodsIn this prospective observational study, 200 cats undergoing complete transthoracic echocardiographic examination were evaluated for the presence, number, and attachment-site patterns of LVBs. LVBs were defined as discrete intracavitary linear structures connecting two myocardial structures within the left ventricle without insertion into the mitral valve leaflets. Each visible connection was classified according to predefined attachment-site categories. For the phenotype comparison, cats with normal LV wall thickness or focal insertion-site thickening (n = 89) were compared with cats with a hypertrophic cardiomyopathy (HCM) phenotype (n = 86).ResultsA total of 884 LVBs were identified. At least one LVB was detected in 199/200 cats, yielding an echocardiographic prevalence of 99.5%. The median number was 4 LVBs per cat (range 0-10). The most frequently observed attachment patterns involved the interventricular septum and papillary muscle or accessory papillary muscle-like structures. Overall, 51.9% of LVBs had at least one septal attachment, and 94.3% involved a papillary muscle or accessory papillary muscle-like structure. LVB counts did not differ by sex (P = 0.47), sedation status (P = 0.26), or between the normal/focal insertion-site thickening and HCM phenotype groups (P = 0.98).Conclusions and relevanceLeft ventricular bands were identified in nearly all cats examined and represent a common echocardiographic finding. Most involved septal and papillary muscle-related attachment sites. Sex and sedation status were not significantly associated with LVB number, and no significant difference was identified between the combined normal/focal insertion-site thickening group and the HCM phenotype group. These findings provide a reference framework for the echocardiographic recognition and classification of LVBs in cats under clinical imaging conditions.
