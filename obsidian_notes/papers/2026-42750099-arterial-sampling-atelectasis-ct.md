# Difference in Collection Times, Blood Gas Variables, and Atelectasis Between Two Arterial Sampling Methods in Dogs.

**Journal of veterinary emergency and critical care (San Antonio, Tex. : 2001) (2026)** · 실험 연구, n=8 · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42750099/) · DOI: 10.1111/vec.70171
관련 주제: [[topics/imaging-technique-safety]]

## 국문 요약
건강한 Beagle 개 8마리를 대상으로 femoral artery(FA, lateral recumbency)와 dorsal pedal artery(DPA, sternal recumbency) 두 동맥혈 채혈법이 채혈 시간, 혈액가스 변수, 이후 시행한 thoracic CT상 atelectasis에 미치는 영향을 비교한 단일맹검 crossover 연구다. DPA 채혈 시간(중앙값 47.5초)이 FA(94.5초)보다 유의하게 짧았고(p=0.0156), 혈액가스 변수는 두 방법 간 차이가 없었다. FA 방법(lateral recumbency 필요)을 이용한 경우 cranial·middle·caudal lung lobe 모두에서 CT 상 atelectasis를 시사하는 CT 값이 DPA 방법보다 유의하게 높았다(각 p<0.01~0.0146). 저자들은 짧은 시간의 lateral recumbency만으로도 atelectasis와 연관될 수 있다고 결론지었다.

## English summary
In this single-blind, crossover experimental study of 8 healthy Beagle dogs, femoral artery (FA, lateral recumbency) and dorsal pedal artery (DPA, sternal recumbency) blood sampling methods were compared for collection time, blood gas variables, and subsequent thoracic CT-detected atelectasis under sedation. Collection time was significantly shorter with DPA (median 47.5 s) than FA (94.5 s, p=0.0156), with no significant differences in gas variables. CT values indicating atelectasis in the cranial, middle, and caudal lung lobes were significantly higher after the FA method than the DPA method (p<0.01 to 0.0146). The authors conclude that even brief lateral recumbency during FA sampling is associated with atelectasis.

## 임상 시사점
진정 하 흉부 CT를 앞둔 개에서 동맥혈 채혈이 필요하다면, lateral recumbency가 요구되는 femoral artery보다 sternal recumbency로 시행 가능한 dorsal pedal artery 채혈을 택하는 것이 채혈 시간도 짧고 이후 CT 판독 시 체위성 atelectasis로 인한 오판 가능성도 줄일 수 있다.

## 연구 설계와 한계
건강한 Beagle 8마리를 대상으로 한 실험적 단일맹검 crossover 연구로, 동일 개체에서 두 채혈법을 모두 시행해 비교했다. 소수의 건강한 실험견만을 대상으로 했고, 임상 환자(질환견)에서의 재현 여부나 atelectasis가 실제 진단 정확도(예: 폐 병변 발견율)에 미치는 영향은 다루지 않았다.

## 원문 초록
OBJECTIVE: To assess how two different arterial blood sampling methods, from the femoral artery (FA) or the dorsal pedal artery (DPA), influence the collection time, gas variables, and pulmonary atelectasis on computed tomography (CT) under sedation.

DESIGN: Experimental, single-blind, crossover study.

SETTING: Veterinary university research facility.

ANIMALS: Eight healthy Beagle dogs.

INTERVENTIONS: Butorphanol was administered subcutaneously before sampling. Arterial blood samples were collected using the FA or DPA in lateral or sternal recumbency, respectively, followed by hemostasis. Medetomidine was then administered intravenously, and thoracic CT was performed under sedation. Dogs remained in the same position throughout the procedure, from blood collection to completion of the thoracic CT scan. All variables were tested for normality using the Kolmogorov-Smirnov test. CT values were analyzed using the Student's t-test. PaO2 and sampling time were analyzed using the Wilcoxon signed-rank test. Statistical significance was set at p < 0.05.

MEASUREMENTS AND MAIN RESULTS: Collection time using the DPA method (47.5 s [31-69 s]) was significantly shorter than the FA method (94.5 s [65-126 s]) (p = 0.0156). No significant differences in gas variables were observed between the two methods. The mean CT values that indicated atelectasis in the cranial (p < 0.01), middle (p = 0.0107), and caudal (p = 0.0146) lung lobes using the FA method were significantly higher than those using the DPA method.

CONCLUSIONS: Blood collection was faster using the DPA method than the FA method, and even brief lateral recumbency during the FA method is associated with atelectasis.
