# Long-term CT and US imaging of canine prostatic carcinoma after prostatic artery embolization: a 900-day follow-up case report.

**Frontiers in veterinary science (2026)** · 증례보고, n=1 · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42866543/) · DOI: 10.1016/j.jvir.2019.02.014
관련 주제: [[topics/urogenital-imaging]], [[topics/vascular-imaging]]

## 국문 요약
예후가 불량하고 장기 치료 선택지가 제한적인 개 prostatic carcinoma에서, minimally invasive 대안인 prostatic artery embolization(PAE) 후 약 900일에 걸친 전립선 영상 변화를 기술한 증례보고다. 13세 중성화 수컷 치와와에서 PAE를 시행(Day 0)한 뒤 CT, ultrasonography(US), 흉부 방사선으로 전립선·방광·폐를 추적했다. 임상 증상은 잘 조절되어 개는 Day 918까지 생존했다. CT에서는 Day 33에 intraprostatic hypoattenuating lesion이 나타나 Day 173에 가스 축적과 함께 커졌고, US에서는 Day 271에 hypoechoic lesion이 Day 678에는 산발적 hyperechoic foci로 변화했다. PAE 후 전립선 실질 용적은 약 33% 감소해 Day 481까지 안정적으로 유지되었으며, 심각한 PAE 관련 합병증은 없었다.

## English summary
This case report describes approximately 900 days of imaging changes in the prostate of a dog with prostatic carcinoma following prostatic artery embolization (PAE), a minimally invasive alternative for a malignancy with poor prognosis and limited long-term treatment options. A 13-year-old neutered male Chihuahua underwent PAE (Day 0) and was monitored with CT, ultrasonography (US), and thoracic radiography. Clinical signs remained well controlled and the dog survived to Day 918. CT showed intraprostatic hypoattenuating lesions at Day 33 that enlarged with gas accumulation by Day 173; US showed hypoechoic lesions at Day 271 that evolved into scattered hyperechoic foci by Day 678. Prostatic parenchymal volume decreased by approximately 33% after PAE and remained stable until Day 481, with no serious PAE-related complications observed.

## 임상 시사점
개 prostatic carcinoma에서 PAE를 시행한 후 추적 영상을 판독할 때, 초기 hypoattenuating/hypoechoic lesion이 가스 축적이나 hyperechoic focus로 변화하는 것은 ischemic·cavitary remodeling의 예상 가능한 경과일 수 있으므로, 이를 재발이나 감염으로 오인하지 않도록 시간 경과에 따른 영상 변화 패턴을 참고할 수 있다.

## 연구 설계와 한계
단일 증례보고로, PAE 후 관찰된 장기 영상 변화와 국소 질병 조절 간의 인과관계를 일반화할 수 없다. 전립선 용적 감소·영상 변화가 이 개체의 종양 생물학적 특성(조직형, 병기 등)에 따른 결과인지, PAE의 보편적 효과인지는 추가 증례 축적이 필요하다.

## 원문 초록
Canine prostatic carcinoma is a highly aggressive malignancy with a poor prognosis and limited long-term treatment options. Prostatic artery embolization (PAE) has emerged as a minimally invasive alternative, but long-term intraprostatic imaging changes after PAE in dogs remain poorly documented. We performed PAE on a 13-year-old neutered male Chihuahua with prostatic carcinoma. Designating PAE as Day 0, we monitored the prostate, bladder, and lungs with computed tomography (CT), ultrasonography (US), and thoracic radiography for approximately 900 days. Clinical signs remained well controlled; the dog survived until Day 918. CT showed intraprostatic hypoattenuating lesions (Day 33) that enlarged with gas accumulation (Day 173); US showed hypoechoic lesions (Day 271) that became scattered hyperechoic foci (Day 678). Following PAE, the prostatic parenchymal volume decreased by approximately 33% and remained stable until Day 481. No serious PAE-related complications were observed. These findings suggest that PAE induced sustained ischemic, cavitary, and chronic remodeling changes within the prostate, which may have contributed to the prolonged local disease control observed in this dog. To our knowledge, this represents the longest time-course imaging reported after PAE in a dog with prostatic carcinoma and illustrates the potential of PAE as a feasible, minimally invasive treatment option in general veterinary practice.
