# Patient-Specific Computational Fluid Dynamics of Pulmonary Arterial Hemodynamics in Dogs with and Without Pulmonic Stenosis.

**Animals : an open access journal from MDPI (2026)** · 실험 연구, n=10 · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42791699/) · DOI: 10.1002/clc.23151
관련 주제: [[topics/cardiac-imaging]]

## 국문 요약
Pulmonic stenosis(PS)는 개의 흔한 선천성 심장병이지만 echocardiography·contrast-enhanced CT angiography(CTA)만으로는 혈류역학적 결과를 온전히 파악하기 어렵다는 문제의식에서, PS가 있는 개 5마리와 없는 대조군 5마리의 CTA·echocardiography 자료로 3차원 폐동맥 형상을 재구성해 환자 맞춤형 computational fluid dynamics(CFD) 모델을 만들었다. PS군은 대조군보다 transvalvular pressure gradient·최대 혈류 속도·폐동맥 저항이 뚜렷하게 높았고, 고속 jet과 협착 후 국소 재순환(post-stenotic recirculation)이 대조군에는 없이 PS군에서만 관찰되었다. Mean wall shear stress(WSS)·time-averaged WSS(TAWSS)는 PS군에서 높아지는 경향을 보인 반면 oscillatory shear index(OSI)는 군 간 유사했고, relative residence time(RRT)은 PS군에서 유의하게 낮았다(p=0.032). 저자들은 CFD가 기존 영상검사를 보완하는 정보를 제공할 수 있으나, 정량적으로 정밀한 임상 적용 가능 수치를 제공하는 데에는 아직 한계가 있다고 결론지었다.

## English summary
Pulmonic stenosis (PS) is one of the most common congenital cardiac diseases in dogs, but echocardiography and contrast-enhanced CT angiography (CTA) alone incompletely characterize its hemodynamic consequences. Using CTA and echocardiographic data from five dogs with PS and five controls without PS, this study reconstructed three-dimensional pulmonary artery geometries to generate patient-specific computational fluid dynamics (CFD) models. Dogs with PS showed markedly increased transvalvular pressure gradients, peak flow velocities, and pulmonary artery resistance, with high-velocity jets and localized post-stenotic recirculation absent in controls. Mean wall shear stress (WSS) and time-averaged WSS (TAWSS) tended to be higher in the PS group, while oscillatory shear index (OSI) was similar between groups; relative residence time (RRT) was significantly lower in the PS group (p = 0.032). The authors concluded that patient-specific CFD provides information complementary to conventional imaging, though its ability to yield precise, clinically applicable quantitative values remains limited.

## 임상 시사점
PS가 있는 개에서 CTA·echocardiography로 형상 정보를 얻어 CFD 모델을 만들면 압력차·혈류 속도 같은 기존 지표 외에 wall shear stress·국소 재순환 패턴 같은 혈류역학적 세부 정보를 추가로 확인할 수 있으나, 현재로서는 연구용 도구에 가까워 개별 환자의 수술·중재 결정을 CFD 수치만으로 내리기는 이르다. PS 중증도를 CTA로 평가할 때 협착 후 재순환·저항 변화가 있을 수 있음을 염두에 두는 정도로 참고할 수 있다.

## 연구 설계와 한계
PS 개 5마리·대조군 5마리(총 10마리)의 CTA·echocardiography 자료를 이용해 환자별 3차원 폐동맥 모델을 만들고 CFD 시뮬레이션을 수행한 실험 연구다. 표본이 각 군 5마리로 매우 적어 통계적 일반화에 한계가 있고, 초록에는 CTA·echocardiography 촬영 프로토콜의 세부 사항과 CFD 모델의 검증(validation) 방법이 제시되지 않았다.

## 원문 초록
Pulmonic stenosis (PS) is one of the most common congenital cardiac diseases in dogs. Although echocardiography and computed tomographic angiography (CTA) provide valuable diagnostic and anatomical information, the hemodynamic consequences of PS remain incompletely characterized. This study describes patient-specific pulmonary artery hemodynamics in dogs with and without PS using computational fluid dynamics (CFD). Contrast-enhanced CTA and echocardiographic data from five dogs with PS and five control dogs without PS were used to reconstruct three-dimensional pulmonary artery geometries and generate patient-specific CFD models. Pressure drop (Δp), blood flow velocity, mean wall shear stress (WSS), time-averaged WSS (TAWSS), oscillatory shear index (OSI), relative residence time (RRT), and pulmonary artery resistance were quantified and compared between groups. Dogs with PS exhibited markedly increased transvalvular pressure gradients, peak flow velocities, and pulmonary artery resistance, together with high-velocity jets and localized post-stenotic recirculation that were absent in control dogs. Mean WSS and TAWSS tended to be higher in the PS group, whereas OSI values were similar between groups. In contrast, RRT was significantly lower (p = 0.032) in dogs with PS. These findings demonstrate that patient-specific CFD enables detailed characterization of pulmonary artery hemodynamics in canine PS, providing information that complements conventional imaging. Although its clinical utility in veterinary medicine has yet to be fully established, CFD can provide valuable insight into flow behavior and disease pathophysiology; however, its ability to yield precise, clinically applicable quantitative values remains limited. This approach shows promise for future applications in the clinical evaluation of PS and other cardiovascular diseases, although further studies are warranted to determine its clinical relevance in veterinary medicine.
