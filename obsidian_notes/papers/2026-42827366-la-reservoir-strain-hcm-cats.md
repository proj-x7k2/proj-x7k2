# EXPRESS: Evaluation of Left Atrial Reservoir Strain by Two-Dimensional Speckle-Tracking Echocardiography in Cats with Stage B1 Hypertrophic Cardiomyopathy.

**Journal of feline medicine and surgery (2026)** · 후향적 연구, n=61 · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42827366/) · DOI: 10.1177/1098612X261497177
관련 주제: [[topics/cardiac-imaging]]

## 국문 요약
건강한 고양이(Stage A, n=30)와 ACVIM stage B1 hypertrophic cardiomyopathy(HCM) 고양이(n=31) 총 61마리를 후향적으로 분석해 2D speckle-tracking echocardiography(2D-STE)로 측정한 left atrial reservoir strain(LA S_R)을 비교한 연구다. LA/Ao ratio·LA diameter·LA area·LA fractional shortening·LA ejection fraction 등 기존 2차원 지표와 함께 automated function imaging software로 LA S_R을 오프라인 측정했다. Stage B1 HCM 고양이는 건강한 대조군보다 LA S_R이 유의하게 낮았고(19.0% vs 38.0%, P<0.001), intra-observer ICC 0.974·inter-observer ICC 0.894로 재현성이 높았다.

## English summary
In this retrospective study of 61 client-owned cats (30 healthy Stage A controls, 31 ACVIM Stage B1 hypertrophic cardiomyopathy [HCM] cats), left atrial reservoir strain (LA S_R) measured offline by two-dimensional speckle-tracking echocardiography (2D-STE) using automated function imaging software was compared alongside conventional LA size and function variables. Cats with Stage B1 HCM had significantly lower LA S_R than healthy controls (19.0% vs 38.0%, P < 0.001), with high intra-observer (ICC 0.974) and inter-observer (ICC 0.894) reproducibility.

## 임상 시사점
Stage B1 HCM이 의심되는 고양이에서 conventional 2D echocardiography 지표와 함께 LA S_R을 추가로 측정하면 좌심방 기능의 미세한 저하를 더 민감하게 포착할 수 있는 보조 지표로 활용할 수 있으나, 측정에는 automated function imaging software와 숙련된 판독이 필요하다.

## 연구 설계와 한계
병원 의료기록에서 후향적으로 식별한 client-owned 고양이 61마리(건강 대조군 30, stage B1 HCM 31)를 대상으로 한 단일기관 연구로, conventional echocardiography와 offline 2D-STE 측정을 비교했다. 후향적 설계이고 LA S_R과 실제 예후(심부전 진행, 생존)의 연관성은 평가하지 않아, 진단 보조 지표를 넘어 예후 지표로서의 가치는 이 연구만으로는 알 수 없다.

## 원문 초록
OBJECTIVES: Left atrial reservoir strain (LA S_R), assessed by two-dimensional speckle-tracking echocardiography (2D-STE), reflects left atrial (LA) function and may reveal subtle alterations in LA mechanics in cats with American College of Veterinary Internal Medicine (ACVIM) stage B1 hypertrophic cardiomyopathy (HCM) compared with healthy cats. This study aimed to determine whether LA S_R differs between cats with HCM (stage B1) and healthy cats using 2D-STE.

METHODS: Sixty-one client-owned cats were retrospectively identified and included from medical databases. These cats were classified as healthy controls (Stage A; n=30) or preclinical HCM (Stage B1; n=31) according to ACVIM consensus guidelines. All cats underwent a comprehensive conventional echocardiographic examination, including two-dimensional, M-mode, color Doppler, pulsed-wave Doppler, and tissue Doppler imaging, as part of clinical care or screening protocols. Conventional two-dimensional assessment of LA size and function included the LA/Ao ratio, LA diameter, LA area, LA fractional shortening (LA FS), and LA ejection fraction (LA EF). LA S_R was subsequently measured offline using automated function imaging software. Reproducibility was assessed via intra- and inter-observer intraclass correlation coefficients (ICC). Receiver operating characteristic (ROC) curve analysis was performed to compare the discriminatory performance of LA S_R and the evaluated LA variables between the predefined study groups.

RESULTS: Cats with ACVIM stage B1 HCM had significantly lower LA S_R than healthy controls (19.0% [IQR, 14.5-22.0%] vs 38.0% [IQR, 30.25-42.50%], respectively; P < 0.001). LA S_R reproducibility was high with an intra-observer ICC of 0.974 and an inter-observer ICC of 0.894.

CONCLUSIONS AND RELEVANCE: Assessment of LA S_R was feasible in cats with stage B1 HCM. LA S_R values differed between affected cats and healthy controls, with lower values observed in the affected group and good reproducibility. These findings suggest that LA S_R may provide complementary information on LA function in cats with this stage of disease.
