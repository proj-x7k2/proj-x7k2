# Computed Tomographic Findings and Clinical Manifestations of Acute Severe Hypersensitivity to a Non-Ionic Iodinated Contrast Agent in a Castrated Male Dog: A Case Report.

**Animals : an open access journal from MDPI (2026)** · 증례보고, n=1 · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42738496/) · DOI: 10.1292/jvms.16-0550

## 국문 요약
입술의 cutaneous mast cell tumor(절제연 불완전)로 전이 평가를 위해 CT를 시행한 10세 중성화 수컷 Maltese에서, non-ionic iodinated contrast 조영제 투여 후 급성 중증 과민반응이 발생한 증례다. 조영 후 2분 지연영상에서 hepatic congestion 의심, 혈관 허탈, 뚜렷한 esophageal wall edema, 강한 intestinal mucosal enhancement가 나타났고, 4분 지연영상에서는 심장 챔버·신실질 내 조영제 정체가 확산성으로 지속되며 renal excretory phase가 소실되었다. 마취 회복 중 중증 저혈압·호흡부전이 발생해 심폐소생술·epinephrine·dexamethasone으로 응급 처치했고, facial edema·hematochezia·설사·일과성 ataxia를 거쳐 dobutamine 투여로 혈압·관류가 정상화되었다. 저자들은 임상 증상이 뚜렷해지기 전 순차적 CT 영상이 조영제 유발 과민반응의 조기 영상 지표가 될 수 있다고 제안했다.

## English summary
This case describes acute severe hypersensitivity to non-ionic iodinated contrast medium in a 10-year-old castrated male Maltese undergoing CT for metastatic evaluation of an incompletely excised cutaneous mast cell tumor. The 2-minute delayed CT scan showed suspected hepatic congestion, vascular collapse, marked esophageal wall edema, and intense intestinal mucosal contrast enhancement; the 4-minute delayed scan showed diffuse contrast stagnation within the cardiac chambers and renal parenchyma with absence of the renal excretory phase. Severe hypotension and respiratory failure developed during anesthetic recovery, managed with cardiopulmonary resuscitation, epinephrine, and dexamethasone; the dog transiently showed facial edema, hematochezia, diarrhea, and ataxia before dobutamine restored systemic blood pressure and perfusion. The authors suggest that sequential CT imaging documented evolving shock-related changes before overt clinical deterioration, potentially serving as an early imaging indicator of contrast-induced hypersensitivity.

## 임상 시사점
조영 CT 중 지연기 영상에서 esophageal wall edema, 장점막 과다조영증강, 신장 excretory phase 소실 같은 소견이 관찰되면 임상 증상이 뚜렷해지기 전이라도 조영제 과민반응·shock의 조기 신호로 의심하고 즉시 활력징후 모니터링·응급 대응 태세를 갖추는 것이 안전할 수 있다.

## 연구 설계와 한계
단일 증례보고로, 순차적 CT 소견과 임상 경과가 상세히 기술되었으나 단일 증례이므로 이 CT 소견들이 실제로 과민반응을 예측하는 민감도·특이도는 알 수 없고, 다른 환자·조영제 프로토콜로 일반화하기는 어렵다.

## 원문 초록
Severe adverse reactions induced by non-ionic contrast media remain an uncommon complication in veterinary medicine. This report describes the clinical manifestations and sequential computed tomography alterations indicative of acute shock captured during 2 min and 4 min delayed phases in a 10-year-old, castrated male Maltese dog. The patient, previously diagnosed with a cutaneous mast cell tumor on the lip exhibiting incomplete histopathological margins, underwent CT for metastatic evaluation. The patient had no clinical evidence of systemic disease, and no anesthetic adverse events were observed during past patellar luxation surgery. However, the 2 min delayed CT scan revealed suspected hepatic congestion, vascular collapse, marked esophageal wall edema, and intense intestinal mucosal contrast enhancement. Subsequently, the 4 min delayed CT scan demonstrated diffuse contrast medium stagnation within the cardiac chambers and renal parenchyma, accompanied by the absence of the renal excretory phase. During anesthetic recovery, severe hypotension and respiratory failure emerged, suggesting the development of hypersensitivity. Following immediate intervention with cardiopulmonary resuscitation, epinephrine, and dexamethasone, the patient temporarily exhibited facial edema, hematochezia, diarrhea, and transient ataxia. However, dobutamine administration successfully restored systemic blood pressure and tissue perfusion, returning the patient to a normal clinical status. Consequently, this case highlights potential pathophysiological mechanisms, key CT findings, and emergency management considerations for contrast-induced hypersensitivity. Notably, sequential CT imaging documented evolving shock-related changes before the onset of overt clinical deterioration, suggesting that these findings may represent early imaging indicators of contrast-induced hypersensitivity.
