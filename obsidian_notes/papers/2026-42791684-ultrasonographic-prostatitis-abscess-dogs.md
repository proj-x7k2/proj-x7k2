# Ultrasonographic Appearance of Acute Prostatitis and Prostatic Abscesses in Dogs.

**Animals : an open access journal from MDPI (2026)** · 후향적 연구, n=35 · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42791684/) · DOI: 10.1016/j.tvjl.2019.02.008
관련 주제: [[topics/urogenital-imaging]]

## 국문 요약
Prostatitis는 개에서 흔한 전립선 질환이지만 초음파 소견이 충분히 정리되어 있지 않다는 문제의식에서, 세포학 또는 조직학적으로 급성 prostatitis가 확진된 client-owned 개 35마리의 병력을 후향적으로 검토했다. 가장 흔한 초음파 소견은 실질 echotexture의 불균질, intraparenchymal cystic lesion, capsular edema, periprostatic steatitis, 전립선 엽 간 비대칭이었다. 35마리 중 25마리에서 prostatic abscess가 확인되었으며 크기·형태가 다양했고, abscess 직경은 체중과 양의 상관관계(p=0.001, rho=0.6), toxic granulation을 보이는 neutrophil 비율과는 음의 상관관계(p=0.02, rho=-0.4)를 보였다. 대부분의 개는 내과적 또는 내외과 병합 치료 후 회복했으며, 추적 검사에서 염증성 주변 변화는 호전되었으나 실질의 불균질한 echotexture는 자주 남았다.

## English summary
Prostatitis is one of the most common prostatic disorders in dogs, although its ultrasonographic features remain poorly characterized. This retrospective study reviewed the records of 35 client-owned dogs with cytologically or histologically confirmed acute prostatitis. The most common ultrasonographic findings were inhomogeneous parenchymal echotexture, intraparenchymal cystic lesions, capsular edema, periprostatic steatitis, and asymmetry of the prostatic lobes. Prostatic abscesses were detected in 25 of 35 dogs and showed marked variability in size and morphology. Abscess diameter was positively correlated with body weight (p = 0.001, rho = 0.6) and negatively correlated with the percentage of neutrophils showing toxic granulation (p = 0.02, rho = -0.4). Most dogs recovered following medical or combined medical-surgical treatment; follow-up examinations showed resolution of inflammatory periprostatic changes, whereas inhomogeneous parenchymal echotexture frequently persisted.

## 임상 시사점
개에서 전립선 실질의 불균질 echotexture, intraparenchymal cystic lesion, capsular edema, periprostatic steatitis, 엽 간 비대칭이 동반된 경우 acute prostatitis·abscess를 우선 고려하고, 큰 체중의 개에서는 더 큰 abscess를 예상할 수 있다. 치료 후 추적 초음파에서 실질 echotexture 불균질이 남아있는 것은 흔한 소견이므로 이것만으로 치료 실패나 재발로 단정하지 않아야 한다.

## 연구 설계와 한계
Client-owned 개 35마리를 대상으로 한 후향적 연구로, 세포학·조직학으로 확진된 acute prostatitis 증례의 초음파·임상·검사실 소견을 정리했다. 후향적 단일/다기관(명시 안 됨) 설계로 초음파 판독자 간 일치도나 표준화된 촬영 프로토콜은 초록에 제시되지 않았고, 대조군(정상 전립선) 비교는 이루어지지 않아 각 소견의 민감도·특이도는 평가할 수 없다.

## 원문 초록
Prostatitis is one of the most common prostatic disorders in dogs, although its ultrasonographic features remain poorly characterized. The aim of this retrospective study was to describe the ultrasonographic findings of canine prostatitis and prostatic abscessation and to investigate their association with clinical and laboratory abnormalities. Medical records of 35 client-owned dogs with cytologically or histologically confirmed acute prostatitis were reviewed. Clinical, ultrasonographic, laboratory, bacteriological, treatment, and follow-up data were collected. The most common ultrasonographic findings were inhomogeneous parenchymal echotexture, intraparenchymal cystic lesions, capsular edema, periprostatic steatitis, and asymmetry of the prostatic lobes. Prostatic abscesses were detected in 25 dogs and showed marked variability in size and morphology. Abscess diameter was positively correlated with body weight (p = 0.001; rho = 0.6) and negatively correlated with the percentage of neutrophils with toxic granulation (p = 0.02; rho = -0.4). Most dogs recovered following medical or combined medical-surgical treatment. Follow-up examinations demonstrated resolution of the inflammatory changes surrounding the prostate, whereas parenchymal inhomogeneous echotexture frequently persisted. These findings provide a comprehensive ultrasonographic description of canine prostatitis and support the use of ultrasonography as a valuable tool for diagnosis and follow-up.
