# Longitudinal assessment of left and right ventricular systolic function in dogs undergoing chemotherapy with doxorubicin.

**Veterinary journal (London, England : 1997) (2026)** · 전향적 연구, n=25 · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42843445/) · DOI: 10.1016/j.tvjl.2026.106911
관련 주제: [[topics/cardiac-imaging]]

## 국문 요약
Doxorubicin 화학요법의 cardiotoxicity가 좌심실뿐 아니라 우심실에도 영향을 줄 수 있다는 사람 cardio-oncology의 시사점을 바탕으로, 암에 걸린 개 25마리(완료 추적 12마리)를 대상으로 conventional echocardiography와 speckle tracking(global longitudinal strain, GLS; tissue motion annular displacement, TMAD)을 투여 전, 7·21·60·120·180일에 반복 측정한 전향적 종단 연구다. 좌심실 volume·fractional shortening·ejection fraction·global circumferential strain·GLS는 시간에 따른 유의한 변화가 없었으나, 180일째 MAPSEi lateral(P=0.002), MAPSEi septal(P=0.0293), global TMAD%(P=0.046)가 기저치보다 감소했고 E' wave velocity 감소와 누적 doxorubicin 용량-E:A ratio 간 음의 상관관계가 확인되어 용량의존적 diastolic function 저하가 시사되었다. 우심실에서는 TAPSEi(P<0.001), RV S' wave(P=0.036), FAC(P=0.04), RV GLS(P=0.002), RV TMAD(P=0.019)가 시간에 따라 점진적으로 감소했다.

## English summary
Building on evidence from human cardio-oncology that doxorubicin cardiotoxicity may affect global cardiac function including the right ventricle, this prospective longitudinal study evaluated 25 dogs with cancer undergoing doxorubicin chemotherapy (12 completing all follow-ups) using conventional echocardiography and speckle tracking (global longitudinal strain, GLS; tissue motion annular displacement, TMAD) before treatment and at 7, 21, 60, 120, and 180 days. Left ventricular volumes, fractional shortening, ejection fraction, global circumferential strain, and GLS showed no significant change over time, but MAPSEi lateral (P = 0.002), MAPSEi septal (P = 0.0293), and global TMAD% (P = 0.046) decreased by day 180 compared with baseline, along with decreased E' wave velocity and a negative correlation between cumulative doxorubicin dose and E:A ratio, suggesting a dose-dependent effect on diastolic function. In the right ventricle, TAPSEi, RV S' wave, FAC, RV GLS, and RV TMAD all showed progressive reductions over time.

## 임상 시사점
Doxorubicin 투여 중인 개를 추적 echocardiography로 모니터링할 때, 기존 지표(ejection fraction, fractional shortening)만으로는 subclinical cardiotoxicity를 놓칠 수 있으므로 MAPSE·TAPSE·TMAD 등 annular displacement 기반 지표와 양심실 평가를 함께 포함하는 것이 조기 발견에 유리할 수 있다.

## 연구 설계와 한계
암에 걸린 개 25마리를 대상으로 한 전향적 종단 연구이나 모든 추적 시점(180일)을 완료한 개체는 12마리로 줄어들어 후반 시점의 통계적 검정력이 제한된다. Doxorubicin을 단독 또는 다른 약물과 병용 투여한 개체가 섞여 있어 cardiotoxicity 소견이 doxorubicin 단독 효과인지 확실하지 않으며, 추적 기간이 180일로 제한되어 더 장기적인 cardiotoxicity 진행 여부는 알 수 없다.

## 원문 초록
Cardiotoxicity is a recognized complication of chemotherapy with doxorubicin and is classically associated with left ventricular dysfunction; however, evidence from human cardio-oncology suggests that global cardiac function, including the right ventricle, may also be affected, and that advanced echocardiographic techniques such as global longitudinal strain (GLS) and tissue motion annular displacement (TMAD) may detect subtle myocardial dysfunction earlier than conventional indices. This prospective longitudinal study evaluated 25 dogs with cancer undergoing doxorubicin chemotherapy, administered alone or in combination with other drugs, using conventional echocardiography and speckle tracking before treatment and at 7, 21, 60, 120 and 180 days after the first dose. Twelve dogs completed all evaluations. No significant changes were observed over time in left ventricular volumes, fractional shortening, ejection fraction, global circumferential strain or GLS. However, reductions in MAPSEi lateral (P = 0.002), MAPSEi septal (P = 0.0293) and global TMAD% (P = 0.046) were observed at 180 days compared with baseline, along with decreased E' wave velocity and a negative correlation between cumulative doxorubicin dose and the E:A ratio, suggesting a dose-dependent effect on diastolic function. In the right ventricle, progressive reductions in TAPSEi (P < 0.001), RV S'n wave (P = 0.036), FAC (P = 0.04), RV GLS (P = 0.002) and RV TMAD (P = 0.019) were observed over time. These findings indicate subclinical reductions in indices of systolic function of both ventricles, highlighting the sensitivity of MAPSE, TAPSE and TMAD for early detection of myocardial alterations.
