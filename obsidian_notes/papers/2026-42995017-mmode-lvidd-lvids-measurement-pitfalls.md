# Pitfalls of M-mode echocardiography end-diastolic and end-systolic measurements: a study of 154 dogs with myxomatous mitral valve disease.

**Journal of Veterinary Cardiology (2026)** · 후향적 연구, n=154 · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42995017/) · DOI: 10.1016/j.jvc.2026.09.009
관련 주제: [[topics/cardiac-imaging]]

## 국문 요약
MMVD 개 154마리의 echocardiography를 후향적으로 재분석해 LVIDd와 LVIDs의 측정 시점이 값에 미치는 영향을 조사했다. LVIDd를 ACVIM 권고대로 QRS onset에서 측정한 값(D1)은 최대 chamber diameter에서 측정한 값(D2)보다 152마리에서 유의하게 낮았다(P<0.001). D2를 쓰면 12마리가 체중 보정 LVIDd 기준값 1.7을 넘었다. Septum과 free wall의 non-synchronous contraction(NSC)이 있는 25마리에서는 maximum septal contraction 시점(S1)의 LVIDs가 minimum septal-to-free wall distance(S2)보다 유의하게 높았다(P<0.001).

## English summary
In a retrospective analysis of 154 dogs with MMVD, LVIDd measured at QRS onset (ACVIM-recommended, D1) was significantly lower than LVIDd at the largest chamber diameter (D2) in 152 dogs; with D2, 12 dogs exceeded the body-weight-normalized LVIDd threshold of 1.7. In 25 dogs with non-synchronous septal/free wall contraction, LVIDs at maximum septal contraction (S1) was significantly higher than at minimum septal-to-free wall distance (S2). The authors conclude that measurement timing influences LVIDd/LVIDs and could hypothetically alter MMVD staging.

## 임상 시사점
Stage B1/B2 경계(LVIDd 정규화 1.7) 근처의 개는 측정 시점 하나로 분류가 달라질 수 있으므로, 어느 시점에서 쟀는지 기록하고 추적 검사에서 동일한 방법을 유지할 것. NSC가 보이는 개에서는 LVIDs를 septal contraction 최대점만으로 재지 않도록 주의한다.

## 연구 설계와 한계
단일 후향적 분석으로 비교 대상이 서로 다른 측정법이며 실제 예후나 reference standard 대비 정확도는 평가되지 않았다. 저자들이 밝힌 한계는 operator subjectivity와 NSC를 관찰로만 판정한 점이다. D2 기준 분류 변화는 "hypothetical"로 서술되었고 D2는 ACVIM 권고 방법이 아니다. NSC 하위군은 25마리로 작다.

## 원문 초록
INTRODUCTION/OBJECTIVES: Echocardiographic assessment of myxomatous mitral valve disease (MMVD) includes measurement of the left ventricular internal dimension at the end-diastole (LVIDd) and at the end-systole (LVIDs), using established cardiac cycle markers. The LVIDd is measured at the QRS onset, which may not coincide with the maximal internal dimension. The LVIDs is obtained at maximum septal contraction, which in dogs with non-synchronous contraction (NSC) between the septal and free wall, may not correspond to the minimal internal dimension. This study investigates alternative measurement methods and their potential impact on MMVD staging.

ANIMALS, MATERIALS AND METHODS: A retrospective analysis of echocardiographic examinations from 154 dogs with MMVD was performed. Twenty-five dogs displayed NSC and were selected for LVIDs assessment. The LVIDd was measured at the onset of the QRS complex (method D1, recommended by the American College of Veterinary Internal Medicine [ACVIM]) and at the largest chamber diameter (method D2). In dogs with NSC, the LVIDs was measured using maximum septal contraction point (method S1) and minimum septal-to-free wall distance (method S2).

RESULTS: The LVIDd values were significantly lower with method D1 in 152 dogs (P<0.001). Using method D2, 12 dogs exceeded the the body-weight-normalized LVIDd threshold of 1.7. In NSC dogs, LVIDs values were significantly higher with method S1 (P<0.001).

STUDY LIMITATIONS: Limitations included operator subjectivity and observational NSC.

CONCLUSIONS: The measurement method influences LVIDd and LVIDs values. Although method D2 is not ACVIM- recommended, its application could hypothetically alter staging outcomes, highlighting the effect of measurement timing.
