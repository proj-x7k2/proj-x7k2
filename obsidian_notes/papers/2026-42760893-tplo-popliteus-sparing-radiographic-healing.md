# Higher early radiographic bone healing scores with popliteus muscle preservation during tibial plateau levelling osteotomy in dogs.

**The Journal of small animal practice (2026)** · 무작위대조군연구, n=100 · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42760893/) · DOI: 10.1111/jsap.70204
관련 주제: [[topics/musculoskeletal-imaging]]

## 국문 요약
Cranial cruciate ligament rupture로 tibial plateau levelling osteotomy(TPLO)를 받는 개 100마리를 popliteus muscle을 절제하는 전통적 술식(n=50)과 근육을 보존하는 변형 술식(n=50)으로 무작위 배정해 수술 45일 후 방사선학적 골유합 점수를 비교한 전향적·맹검·무작위 임상연구다. Popliteus-sparing군은 10점 bone healing scale에서 보정 평균 1.95점, modified radiographic union scale에서 1.87점 더 높은 골유합 점수를 보였다. 수술 직후~45일 사이 tibial plateau angle 변화(rock-back)로 평가한 osteotomy 안정성은 두 군 간 임상적으로 유의한 차이가 없었다. 저자들은 popliteus muscle 보존이 골유합 방사선 점수를 개선하면서도 안정성을 저해하지 않는 안전한 대안이라고 결론지었다.

## English summary
In this prospective, blinded, randomized clinical trial, 100 dogs with unilateral cranial cruciate ligament rupture undergoing TPLO were allocated to traditional surgery with popliteus muscle detachment (n=50) or a modified popliteus-sparing technique (n=50). At 45 days, popliteus-sparing dogs showed higher radiographic bone healing scores, with adjusted mean differences of 1.95 points (10-point bone healing scale) and 1.87 points (modified radiographic union scale). No clinically relevant difference in tibial plateau angle change (rock-back) was found between groups. The authors conclude popliteus-sparing TPLO is a safe alternative associated with improved early radiographic healing scores without evidence of increased osteotomy instability.

## 임상 시사점
TPLO 시행 시 popliteus muscle을 보존하는 변형 술식을 고려하면 45일차 방사선 골유합 평가에서 더 높은 점수를 기대할 수 있으나, 이것이 실제 임상 회복·기능 개선으로 이어지는지는 아직 불명확하므로 술식 선택의 유일한 근거로 삼기는 이르다.

## 연구 설계와 한계
2022~2025년 시행된 전향적·맹검·무작위대조군연구로, 두 가지 검증된 방사선 골유합 채점 체계를 이용해 45일 시점 골유합을 평가했다. 저자들 스스로 "방사선학적 차이가 임상적 회복이나 사지 기능 개선으로 이어지는지는 알 수 없다"고 명시했으며, 45일이라는 단일 조기 시점 평가로 장기 결과는 다루지 않았다.

## 원문 초록
OBJECTIVE: To compare early radiographic bone healing 45 days after tibial plateau levelling osteotomy performed with popliteus muscle detachment versus a modified technique preserving the muscle and to determine whether muscle preservation influences post-operative osteotomy stability through measurement of rock-back.

MATERIALS AND METHODS: In this prospective, blinded, randomised clinical study (2022 to 2025), 100 client-owned dogs with unilateral cranial cruciate ligament rupture were allocated to traditional surgery (n = 50) or popliteus-sparing surgery (n = 50). Radiographic assessment was performed preoperatively, immediately post-operatively and 45 days after surgery. Bone healing was evaluated using two validated radiographic scoring systems. Osteotomy stability was assessed by measuring the change in tibial plateau angle between the immediate post-operative and 45-day radiographs. Multivariable linear regression models were used to adjust for baseline clinical variables. A P-value <.05 was considered statistically significant.

RESULTS: Dogs undergoing popliteus-sparing surgery demonstrated higher bone healing scores at 45 days, with an adjusted mean difference of 1.95 (95% confidence interval 0.91 to 2.99) on the 10-point bone healing scale and 1.87 (95% confidence interval 0.88 to 2.86) on the modified radiographic union scale. There was no clinically relevant difference in post-operative tibial plateau angle change between groups, with an adjusted mean difference of 0.26 degrees (95% confidence interval -0.10 to 0.63). No major intraoperative vascular complications were recorded.

CLINICAL SIGNIFICANCE: Tibial plateau levelling osteotomy performed without detachment of the popliteus muscle represents a safe and effective alternative to the traditional technique. Popliteus muscle preservation during tibial plateau levelling osteotomy was associated with higher radiographic bone healing scores at 45 days, without evidence of increased osteotomy instability. Whether this radiographic difference translates into improved clinical recovery or limb function remains unknown.
