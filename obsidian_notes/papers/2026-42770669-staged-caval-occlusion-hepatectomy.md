# Staged caval occlusion enabling en bloc right divisional hepatectomy in two dogs with hepatocellular carcinoma.

**Veterinary surgery : VS (2026)** · 증례보고, n=2 · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42770669/) · DOI: 10.1111/vsu.70143
관련 주제: [[topics/vascular-imaging]], [[topics/abdominal-imaging]]

## 국문 요약
Right divisional hepatocellular carcinoma(HCC)가 caudal vena cava(CVC)를 압박하거나 침범해 기존 collateral venous return이 없던 개 2마리(11세 중성화 수컷, Siberian Husky·Shih Tzu)에서, en bloc right divisional hepatectomy와 segmental CVC resection을 가능하게 하기 위해 staged pre-renal CVC occlusion을 시행했다. CT에서 각각 91.8×122.3×101.4mm, 77.0×94.8×70.2mm 크기의 right divisional mass와 CVC 압박/침범이 확인되었고, 경피적으로 조절 가능한 hydraulic occluder를 pre-renal CVC 주위에 삽입한 뒤 saphenous venous pressure·신장 수치·뒷다리 부종을 모니터링하며 외래에서 79일과 44일에 걸쳐 점진적으로 폐쇄했다. CT로 완전 폐쇄와 collateralization을 확인한 뒤 en bloc 우엽 절제·CVC 분절 절제를 시행했으며, 두 마리 모두 육안적 완전 절제와 조직학적 HCC 완전 절제가 확인되었다. 합병증은 경미했고(일시적 창상 삼출, 일시적 복수), 수술 후 생존 기간은 각각 352일과 2467일이었다.

## English summary
In two dogs (11-year-old neutered males, a Siberian Husky and a Shih Tzu) with right divisional hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC) causing caudal vena cava (CVC) compression or involvement without pre-existing collateral venous return, staged pre-renal CVC occlusion was performed to enable en bloc right divisional hepatectomy with segmental CVC resection. CT showed right divisional masses (91.8 × 122.3 × 101.4 mm and 77.0 × 94.8 × 70.2 mm, respectively) with CVC compression or involvement. A percutaneously controlled hydraulic occluder was placed around the pre-renal CVC, and gradual outpatient occlusion was performed over 79 and 44 days while monitoring saphenous venous pressure, renal values, and hindlimb edema. Complete CVC occlusion and collateralization were confirmed by CT before en bloc right divisional hepatectomy with segmental CVC resection. Macroscopically and histologically complete HCC resection was achieved in both dogs, with only transient morbidity (wound exudation in one, transient ascites in the other). Survival after hepatectomy was 352 and 2467 days.

## 임상 시사점
CVC를 압박하거나 침범한 right divisional HCC에서 기존 collateral venous return이 없어 즉시 절제가 위험한 경우, CT로 종양 범위와 CVC 침범을 평가한 뒤 percutaneous hydraulic occluder로 pre-renal CVC를 단계적으로 폐쇄하고 CT로 완전 폐쇄·collateralization을 확인하는 접근이 선별된 증례에서 안전한 en bloc 절제로 이어질 수 있음을 시사한다. 이 술식을 고려하는 경우 술 전 CT가 종양 크기·CVC 침범 정도 평가뿐 아니라 폐쇄 진행 상태를 추적하는 데에도 핵심적인 역할을 한다.

## 연구 설계와 한계
11세 중성화 수컷 개 2마리를 대상으로 한 단기 증례군(short case series)으로, CT를 이용해 종양 크기·CVC 침범을 평가하고 staged occlusion 경과와 collateralization 완성 여부를 추적했다. 증례 수가 2예에 불과해 이 술식의 적용 가능 범위(종양 크기·침범 정도의 한계, 실패 위험 요인)나 일반화 가능성은 이 연구만으로 판단할 수 없으며, 초록에는 CT 프로토콜의 세부 사항(조영 시기, 슬라이스 두께 등)이 제시되지 않았다.

## 원문 초록
OBJECTIVE: To describe staged pre-renal caudal vena cava (CVC) occlusion before en bloc right divisional hepatectomy with segmental caval resection in two dogs with right divisional hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC) involving the CVC.

ANIMALS: Two client-owned, 11-year-old neutered male dogs (Siberian Husky and Shih Tzu).

STUDY DESIGN: Short case series.

METHODS: Dog 1 (Siberian Husky) had prior hemoperitoneum and biopsy-confirmed HCC; dog 2 (Shih Tzu) had persistent liver enzyme elevations and a suspected hepatic tumor. Computed tomography (CT) showed right divisional masses (91.8 × 122.3 × 101.4 mm; 77.0 × 94.8 × 70.2 mm) with CVC compression or involvement and no pre-existing collateral venous return. A percutaneously controlled hydraulic occluder was placed around the pre-renal CVC. Gradual outpatient occlusion was performed over 79 and 44 days while monitoring saphenous venous pressure, renal values, and hindlimb edema. Complete CVC occlusion and collateralization were confirmed by CT before en bloc right divisional hepatectomy with segmental CVC resection.

RESULTS: Macroscopically complete resection was achieved in both dogs. Complete HCC resection was confirmed histopathologically. Morbidity was limited to transient wound exudation in dog 1 and transient ascites in dog 2. No recurrence or metastasis was identified in dog 1 during follow-up. In dog 2, no clinical signs suggestive of recurrence or metastasis were reported. Survival after hepatectomy was 352 and 2467 days.

CONCLUSION: Staged pre-renal CVC occlusion may establish collateral venous return and permit planned en bloc right divisional hepatectomy with segmental CVC resection in carefully selected dogs with right divisional HCC involving the CVC.
