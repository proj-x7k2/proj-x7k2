# Intraoperative cholangiography during open cholecystectomy in 73 dogs with benign gallbladder disease.

**Veterinary surgery : VS (2026)** · 후향적 연구, n=73 · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42717636/) · DOI: 10.1111/vsu.70157
관련 주제: [[topics/abdominal-imaging]]

## 국문 요약
2014~2022년 benign gallbladder disease로 open cholecystectomy를 받은 개 73마리(수술 전 common bile duct(CBD) obstruction 동반 증례 포함)를 대상으로 bile duct flushing(BDF)과 subtraction fluoroscopy 하 intraoperative cholangiography(IOC)를 시행한 결과를 후향적으로 분석했다. IOC는 73마리 중 72마리에서 평가 가능했고(1마리는 카테터 천공으로 평가 불가), complete CBD obstruction이 12.5%(9/72), retained debris로 인한 partial obstruction이 20.8%(15/72)에서 확인되었으며 그중 4마리는 조영제 주입 중 debris가 이동해 일과성으로 원위부 CBD를 막았다. 간관(hepatic duct)까지 조영된 66마리 중 7마리(10.6%)에서 hepatic duct filling defect가 확인되었고, 1마리는 조영제 관련 shock이 의심되었으나 치료로 회복되었다. 전체 코호트의 주술기 사망률은 6.8%(5/73)였으며 황달이 없던 개는 0%(0/29), 황달이 있던 개는 11.4%(5/44)였다.

## English summary
This retrospective case series reviewed 73 dogs with benign gallbladder disease (including cases with preoperative common bile duct (CBD) obstruction) that underwent open cholecystectomy, bile duct flushing (BDF), and intraoperative cholangiography (IOC) under subtraction fluoroscopy between 2014 and 2022. IOC was evaluable in 72/73 dogs; complete CBD obstruction was identified in 12.5% (9/72) and partial obstruction with retained debris in 20.8% (15/72), with debris transiently obstructing the distal CBD during contrast injection in 4 cases. Among 66 dogs with hepatic duct opacification, 7 (10.6%) had filling defects; one dog developed suspected contrast-associated shock that resolved with treatment. Perioperative mortality was 6.8% (5/73) overall — 0% in dogs without jaundice and 11.4% in dogs with jaundice.

## 임상 시사점
개 담낭절제술 시 BDF만으로는 놓칠 수 있는 CBD·hepatic duct의 잔류 debris나 폐쇄를 IOC가 상당한 빈도(약 1/3)로 추가 검출하므로, CBD 폐쇄가 의심되거나 황달이 있는 증례에서는 IOC를 보조 수단으로 고려할 근거가 된다. 다만 조영제 관련 shock 같은 합병증 가능성도 함께 설명해야 한다.

## 연구 설계와 한계
단일 기관 8년간의 의무기록을 후향적으로 검토한 증례군 연구로, IOC 시행 여부·해석이 표준화되었는지, 대조군(비-IOC) 비교가 있었는지는 초록에 명시되지 않았다. 주술기 사망률이 황달 유무에 따라 크게 달랐던 것은 IOC 자체의 효과라기보다 기저 질환 중증도를 반영할 가능성이 있어, IOC가 실제 수술 결과를 개선했는지는 이 연구만으로 단정할 수 없다.

## 원문 초록
OBJECTIVE: To evaluate intraoperative cholangiography (IOC) during open cholecystectomy in dogs with benign gallbladder disease, including cases with preoperative common bile duct (CBD) obstruction, and to describe the short-term outcomes.

STUDY DESIGN: Retrospective case series.

ANIMALS: A total of 73 client-owned dogs.

METHODS: Medical records (2014-2022) were reviewed. All dogs underwent open cholecystectomy, bile duct flushing (BDF) with saline, and IOC under subtraction fluoroscopy. Cholangiograms were assessed for complete CBD obstruction (no duodenal passage), partial obstruction (CBD filling defects indicating retained debris), and filling defects in hepatic ducts. Perioperative mortality (≤14 days) and post-discharge findings during 3 months of follow-up were recorded.

RESULTS: IOC was evaluable in 72 of 73 dogs; assessment was precluded by catheter perforation in one dog. Complete CBD obstruction was identified in 12.5% (9/72). Partial obstruction with retained debris occurred in 20.8% (15/72); debris migrated to transiently obstruct the distal CBD during contrast injection in four of 15. Among the 66 dogs in which IOC opacified the right and left hepatic ducts, seven (10.6%) had filling defects in the hepatic ducts. One dog developed suspected contrast-associated shock that resolved with treatment. Perioperative mortality was 6.8% (5/73) in the total cohort; 0% (0/29) in dogs without jaundice, and 11.4% (5/44) in dogs with jaundice. Among 61 dogs with follow-up, no deaths occurred within 3 months.

CONCLUSION: During open cholecystectomy, IOC frequently identified retained debris and other biliary abnormalities.

CLINICAL SIGNIFICANCE: IOC may be a useful adjunct to BDF when CBD or hepatic duct obstruction or retained debris is suspected. Routine IOC may improve surgical outcomes, particularly in dogs with preoperative CBD obstruction.
