# Magnetic resonance imaging identifies prognostic features in dogs and cats with ischemic cerebrovascular accidents.

**American journal of veterinary research (2026)** · 후향적 연구, n=63 · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42727605/) · DOI: 10.2460/ajvr.26.06.0268
관련 주제: [[topics/neuro-imaging]]

## 국문 요약
급성 비진행성 뇌기능장애로 내원한 개 47마리·고양이 16마리(총 63마리)를 대상으로, MRI 소견(arterial spin labeling(ASL) perfusion 포함)과 ischemic cerebrovascular accident(ICVA) 아형, 예후 인자를 후향적으로 분석했다. Middle cerebral artery 전 영역 또는 소뇌 반구 전체를 침범하는 새로운 "complete territorial ICVA" 아형이 63마리 중 8마리(12.7%)에서 확인되었고, 중등도~중증 mass effect와 흔히 동반되었다. Non-lacunar infarct는 모두 ASL에서 focal hypoperfusion을 보인 반면 lacunar infarct는 관류 이상이 없었으며, mass effect를 동반한 complete territorial ICVA("malignant ICVA")는 조기 사망과 강하게 연관되었다(OR 400). 24시간 이내 영상을 획득한 non-lacunar infarct 19마리 중 2마리(10.5%)에서만 ischemic penumbra가 확인되어, ASL이 구제 가능한 허혈 조직 판별에는 제한적임을 시사했다.

## English summary
This retrospective single-center study analyzed MRI findings, including arterial spin labeling (ASL) perfusion, in 63 dogs and cats (47 dogs, 16 cats) with acute, nonprogressive brain dysfunction consistent with ischemic cerebrovascular accident (ICVA). A novel "complete territorial ICVA" subtype, involving an entire middle cerebral artery territory or cerebellar hemisphere, was identified in 8/63 animals (12.7%) and was commonly associated with moderate-to-severe mass effect. All nonlacunar infarcts showed focal ASL hypoperfusion, whereas lacunar infarcts showed none. Complete territorial ICVAs with mass effect ("malignant ICVAs") were strongly associated with early mortality (OR 400), while ischemic penumbra was identified in only 2/19 nonlacunar infarcts imaged within 24 hours.

## 임상 시사점
MRI에서 middle cerebral artery 영역 전체 또는 소뇌 반구 전체를 침범하고 mass effect가 동반된 "malignant ICVA" 소견이 확인되면 조기 사망 위험이 매우 높다는 점을 보호자 상담과 예후 설명에 활용할 수 있다.

## 연구 설계와 한계
2018~2024년 단일 기관에서 ICVA에 합당한 MRI 소견과 ASL perfusion 영상을 보유한 개·고양이 63마리를 후향적으로 분석한 관찰 연구다. 단일 기관 후향적 설계로 선택 편향 가능성이 있고, ASL을 이용한 penumbra 평가는 24시간 이내 영상을 획득한 일부(19마리)에서만 가능해 표본이 작다. 초록에는 malignant ICVA 판정에 사용된 구체적 mass effect 등급 기준이나 치료 개입 여부에 대한 상세 서술은 없다.

## 원문 초록
OBJECTIVE: To characterize MRI findings, including arterial spin labeling (ASL) perfusion features, in dogs and cats with ischemic cerebrovascular accidents (ICVAs) and to identify prognostic factors.

METHODS: Retrospective, single-center observational study of dogs and cats presenting with acute, nonprogressive brain dysfunction from 2018 through 2024, with MRI findings consistent with ICVA and available ASL perfusion imaging. Ischemic cerebrovascular accidents subtypes; MRI findings, including ASL perfusion characteristics; and outcomes were analyzed.

RESULTS: 63 animals (47 dogs and 16 cats) were included. A novel complete territorial ICVA subtype, involving either an entire middle cerebral artery territory or an entire cerebellar hemisphere, was identified in 8 of 63 animals (12.7%) and commonly associated with moderate-to-severe mass effect. All nonlacunar infarcts exhibited focal ASL hypoperfusion, whereas lacunar infarcts showed no detectable perfusion abnormalities. Ischemic penumbra was identified in only 2 of 19 animals (10.5%) with nonlacunar infarcts imaged within 24 hours of onset. Complete territorial ICVAs with moderate-to-severe mass effect were classified as malignant ICVAs because this pattern was strongly associated with early mortality (OR, 400; 95% CI, 14.5 to 11,039).

CONCLUSIONS: These findings expand the recognized spectrum of ICVA in dogs and cats and indicate that infarct extent and mass effect are important markers of disease severity and early outcome. ASL perfusion can demonstrate hypoperfusion in nonlacunar ICVAs but has limited ability to identify potentially salvageable ischemic tissue.

CLINICAL RELEVANCE: Recognition of complete territorial and malignant ICVAs may improve early prognostic assessment and inform clinical decision-making in dogs and cats with ICVA.
