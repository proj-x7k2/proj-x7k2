# Procedural sedation with subcutaneously administered medetomidine-vatinoxan-butorphanol in dogs - A randomized, controlled, clinical dose-finding study.

**Veterinary journal (London, England : 1997) (2026)** · 무작위대조군연구, n=53 · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42838285/) · DOI: 10.1016/j.tvjl.2026.106921
관련 주제: [[topics/sedation-restraint-imaging]]

## 국문 요약
Intramuscular(IM) 투여보다 subcutaneous(SC) 투여가 일부 개에서 더 잘 용인될 수 있다는 전제로, orthopedic radiography를 위한 진정 목적의 medetomidine-vatinoxan-butorphanol SC 투여의 dose-response를 평가한 무작위대조군 임상 연구다. 보호자 소유 건강한 개 53마리를 IM medetomidine 단독(M0.6-IM), IM medetomidine/vatinoxan(Z0.6-IM), SC medetomidine/vatinoxan 3개 용량군(Z0.6-SC, Z0.9-SC, Z1.2-SC)에 무작위 배정했고 모든 군에 butorphanol 0.2mg/kg을 병용했다. IM 투여군이 SC 투여군보다 진정 발현이 더 빨랐고, rescue sedation 필요 비율은 SC 저용량군(Z0.6-SC)에서 가장 높았다(4/11). 심박수는 모든 처치에서 기저치보다 유의하게 감소했으나 저혈압(MAP<60mmHg)은 관찰되지 않았고, vatinoxan을 병용한 모든 군(IM·SC)이 medetomidine 단독군보다 oral mucosal microcirculation(Laser Doppler Flow, T-HbO2)이 유의하게 더 양호했다.

## English summary
Based on the premise that subcutaneous (SC) drug administration may be better tolerated than intramuscular (IM) administration in some dogs, this randomized controlled clinical dose-finding study evaluated SC medetomidine-vatinoxan-butorphanol for sedation prior to orthopedic radiography. Fifty-three healthy client-owned dogs were randomized to IM medetomidine alone (M0.6-IM), IM medetomidine/vatinoxan (Z0.6-IM), or one of three SC medetomidine/vatinoxan dose groups (Z0.6-SC, Z0.9-SC, Z1.2-SC), all combined with butorphanol 0.2 mg/kg. Sedation onset was faster after both IM treatments than after SC treatments, and the need for rescue sedation was highest in the lowest-dose SC group (4/11, Z0.6-SC). Heart rate decreased significantly from baseline across all treatments without hypotension (MAP < 60 mmHg), and all vatinoxan-containing groups (IM and SC) showed significantly better oral mucosal microcirculation (Laser Doppler Flow, tissue hemoglobin saturation) than medetomidine alone.

## 임상 시사점
Orthopedic radiography를 위해 medetomidine-vatinoxan-butorphanol로 진정할 때 SC 투여는 IM 투여보다 진정 발현이 느리고 rescue sedation이 더 필요할 수 있으므로, 신속한 촬영이 필요한 상황에서는 IM 투여를 우선하고 SC 투여는 저용량(0.6 mg/m2)보다 높은 용량(0.9~1.2 mg/m2)을 고려하는 것이 적절하다.

## 연구 설계와 한계
보호자 소유 건강한 개 53마리를 5개 군(각 10~11마리)으로 나눈 무작위대조군 임상 연구로, 군당 표본이 작아 세부 용량군 간 통계적 차이를 확정하기에는 검정력이 제한적이다. 건강한 개 대상 연구이므로 통증·불안이 있는 환자나 다른 품종·연령군에서 동일한 dose-response가 나타나는지는 확인되지 않았으며, 실제 영상 품질(motion artifact 등)에 대한 평가는 초록에 제시되지 않았다.

## 원문 초록
Subcutaneous (SC) drug administration may be better tolerated when compared with the intramuscular (IM) route in some dogs. This study investigated the dose-response to subcutaneously administered medetomidine-vatinoxan-butorphanol in producing sedation for orthopedic radiography. Fifty-three privately-owned, healthy dogs were randomly allocated to receive IM medetomidine 0.6 mg/m2 (M0.6-IM), IM medetomidine/vatinoxan 0.6/12 mg/m2 (Z0.6-IM), SC medetomidine/vatinoxan 0.6/12 (Z0.6-SC), 0.9/18 (Z0.9-SC) or 1.2/24 mg/m2 (Z1.2-SC). All treatments included 0.2 mg/kg of butorphanol. Sedation onset and rescue sedation for radiography were recorded. Mean arterial pressures (MAP) and heart rates (HR) were measured and oral mucosal microcirculation quantified with Laser Doppler Flow (LDF) and tissue hemoglobin saturation percentage (T-HbO2). Compared with the SC groups, sedation occurred earlier after both IM treatments. Rescue sedation was administered for 4/11 (Z0.6-SC), 2/10 (Z0.9-SC), 1/10 (Z1.2-SC), 0/11 (Z0.6-IM) and 1/11 dogs (M0.6-IM), respectively. HR decreased significantly from baseline after all treatments while hypotension (MAP < 60 mmHg) was not observed. After M0.6-IM, median (range) LDF 55 (36 - 113) perfusion units (PU) and T-HbO2 64 (49 - 79) % were significantly lower than after Z0.6-IM: 155 (105 - 312) PU and 81 (67 - 87) %; Z0.9-SC: 196 (126 - 492) PU and 79 (72-94) %; Z0.9-SC: 163 (68 - 465) PU and 77 (72 - 84) % and Z1.2-SC: 146 (82 - 422) PU and 77 (69 - 85) %, with no significant differences between the vatinoxan-treatments. Subcutaneously administered medetomidine-vatinoxan-butorphanol appeared efficacious albeit less potent than the IM route in producing sedation in healthy dogs. The hemodynamic improvement by vatinoxan extended to SC co-administration with medetomidine-butorphanol.
