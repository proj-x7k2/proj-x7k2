# Bunny Burrito Restraint for Computed Tomography in Conscious Rabbits: An Effective Alternative

**Veterinary Radiology & Ultrasound (2026)** · 후향적 연구, n=93 · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42644764/) · DOI: 10.1111/vru.70230
관련 주제: [[topics/sedation-restraint-imaging]]

## 국문 요약
2021년 3월부터 2023년 12월까지 보호자 소유 토끼 93마리를 대상으로 진행된 후향적 다기관 관찰연구에서, conscious rabbit의 CT 검사 시 bunny burrito(수건으로 감싸는 보정법)와 plexiglass chamber 보정법, sedation을 비교했다. bunny burrito 그룹(36마리)은 plexiglass chamber 그룹(47마리)에 비해 pre-contrast 영상에서 motion artifact가 유의하게 적었고(p=0.02), dental disease 하위군에서 positioning이 더 우수했으며(p=0.03), post-contrast 초기 안정성도 더 좋았다(p=0.04, 신뢰구간 넓음). bunny burrito와 sedation(10마리) 간 영상 품질은 기술통계상 유사했으며, 검사 중이나 이후 합병증은 보고되지 않았다. 저자들은 bunny burrito 보정법이 plexiglass chamber보다 특정 상황에서 우수하며, sedation에 따른 위험 없이 CT 검사 접근성을 높이는 유효하고 안전한 대안이라고 결론지었다.

## English summary
This retrospective multicenter study evaluated the bunny burrito restraint technique as an alternative to plexiglass chamber restraint for CT imaging in 93 conscious client-owned rabbits (2021-2023). The bunny burrito group (n=36) showed significantly fewer motion artifacts in pre-contrast acquisitions, better positioning in the dental disease subgroup, and better early post-contrast stability compared to the plexiglass chamber group (n=47). Image quality was descriptively similar between bunny burrito and sedation (n=10), with no complications reported in either physical restraint group. The findings support bunny burrito restraint as a safe, effective, low-equipment alternative that reduces sedation-related risk in rabbit CT imaging.

## 임상 시사점
토끼 CT 검사에서 sedation이 어렵거나 위험도가 높은 환자라면 bunny burrito 보정법을 우선 고려할 수 있으며, 특히 dental disease 평가 시 plexiglass chamber보다 positioning 안정성이 우수하다.

## 연구 설계와 한계
93마리 client-owned rabbit을 대상으로 한 후향적 다기관 관찰연구로, bunny burrito(n=36)·plexiglass chamber(n=47)·sedation(n=10) 세 군을 logistic regression으로 비교했다. sedation 군의 표본이 10마리로 작아 통계적 비교 없이 기술통계로만 제시되었고, post-contrast 안정성 비교는 신뢰구간이 넓어 해석에 주의가 필요하다. 다기관 후향적 설계 특성상 기관별 CT 프로토콜과 보정 숙련도 차이가 결과에 영향을 주었을 가능성이 있다.

## 원문 초록
Computed tomography (CT) is a critical diagnostic tool in rabbits. Traditional CT imaging often requires sedation or general anesthesia, posing inherent risks such as mortality and gastrointestinal complications. An effective physical restraint method could enhance patient safety, reduce costs, and improve accessibility to CT imaging. This study evaluates the effectiveness of the well-known bunny burrito restraint technique as an alternative to the plexiglass chamber method for conducting CT examinations in conscious rabbits. A retrospective multicenter observational study was conducted on 93 client-owned rabbits undergoing CT between March 2021 and December 2023. Cases were classified into three restraint groups: bunny burrito (n = 36), plexiglass chamber (n = 47), and sedation (n = 10). CT image quality was assessed on the basis of motion artifacts, positioning, and stability across different acquisitions. Statistical analyses, including logistic regression, were performed to compare the two physical restraint methods. The bunny burrito technique had significantly lower motion artifacts in pre-contrast acquisition (p = 0.02), better positioning in the dental disease subgroup (p = 0.03), and better stability in early post-contrast period (p = 0.04, wide confidence interval) compared to the plexiglass chamber group. Image quality was similar between bunny burrito and sedation in descriptive results. No complications were reported during or after CT examinations. In conclusion, the bunny burrito method is an effective and safe restraint method for CT imaging in conscious rabbits that shows advantages over the plexiglass chamber in specific imaging contexts. It minimizes sedation-related risks with simplified equipment. This method represents a valuable advancement in veterinary diagnostic imaging.
