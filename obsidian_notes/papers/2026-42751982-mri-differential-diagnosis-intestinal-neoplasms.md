# Differential Diagnosis Using MRI of Canine Intestinal Neoplasms, Including Lymphoma, Adenocarcinoma, and Inflammatory Polyp: Case Series.

**Veterinary radiology & ultrasound : the official journal of the American College of Veterinary Radiology and the International Veterinary Radiology Association (2026)** · 증례군, n=12 · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42751982/) · DOI: 10.1111/vru.70257
관련 주제: [[topics/abdominal-imaging]]

## 국문 요약
개 장관 신생물(lymphoma 6예, adenocarcinoma 2예, inflammatory polyp 4예)의 MRI 소견을 후향적으로 평가한 최초의 수의영상의학 증례군 연구다. T1WI·T2WI·contrast-enhanced T1WI(C-T1WI)·diffusion-weighted imaging(DWI)에서의 신호강도, 조영증강 양상, 장폐색 동반 여부, apparent diffusion coefficient(ADC) 값을 비교한 결과, lymphoma는 C-T1WI에서 전례(6/6) hypointense 신호를 보인 반면 adenocarcinoma·inflammatory polyp은 그렇지 않았다(p=0.002). Lymphoma는 균질한 조영증강을 adenocarcinoma·inflammatory polyp보다 유의하게 더 자주 보였고(5/6 vs 0/2, 0/4, p=0.02), ADC 값도 lymphoma(0.58±0.18×10⁻³mm²/s)가 adenocarcinoma(1.27±0.007×10⁻³mm²/s, p=0.01)·inflammatory polyp(1.56±0.3×10⁻³mm²/s, p=0.0002)보다 유의하게 낮았다. T1WI·T2WI 신호강도, 정성적 DWI, 장폐색 동반 여부에서는 신생물 간 유의한 차이가 없었다.

## English summary
This retrospective case series is the first veterinary study to evaluate MRI findings of intestinal neoplasms (lymphoma n=6, adenocarcinoma n=2, inflammatory polyp n=4) in dogs. All lymphomas (6/6) showed hypointense signal intensity on contrast-enhanced T1WI, unlike adenocarcinomas and inflammatory polyps (p=0.002), and lymphomas showed homogeneous enhancement significantly more often than the other two groups (5/6 vs 0/2, 0/4; p=0.02). ADC values of lymphomas (0.58±0.18×10⁻³ mm²/s) were significantly lower than adenocarcinomas (1.27±0.007×10⁻³ mm²/s, p=0.01) and inflammatory polyps (1.56±0.3×10⁻³ mm²/s, p=0.0002). No significant differences were found in T1WI/T2WI signal intensity, qualitative DWI, or the presence of intestinal obstruction among the groups.

## 임상 시사점
개 장관 종괴에 대해 MRI를 시행하는 경우, contrast-enhanced T1WI에서의 균질한 hypointense 조영증강과 낮은 ADC 값(약 0.6×10⁻³mm²/s 내외)은 intestinal lymphoma를 시사하는 소견으로 adenocarcinoma·inflammatory polyp과의 감별에 참고할 수 있다 — 다만 각 군 표본이 매우 작다는 점(증례보고 수준)을 함께 고려해야 한다.

## 연구 설계와 한계
단일 기관에서 후향적으로 수집한 12예(lymphoma 6, adenocarcinoma 2, inflammatory polyp 4)의 소규모 증례군 연구로, 특히 adenocarcinoma(2예)·inflammatory polyp(4예) 군은 표본이 매우 작아 통계적 결론의 안정성이 제한적이다 — 제한적 근거. 저자들도 이것이 수의영상의학에서 장관 신생물 MRI 소견을 다룬 최초 연구라고 밝혀, 비교할 선행 문헌이 없다는 점도 한계다.

## 원문 초록
No studies have evaluated magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) findings of intestinal neoplasms in veterinary medicine. We hypothesized that MRI findings would differ among intestinal neoplasms in veterinary medicine. This study retrospectively assessed the MRI findings of intestinal neoplasms, including lymphomas (n = 6), adenocarcinomas (n = 2), and inflammatory polyps (n = 4) in dogs. The following MRI parameters were recorded: neoplasm signal intensity (SI) on T1-weighted images (T1WI), T2-weighted images (T2WI), contrast-enhanced T1WI (C-T1WI), and diffusion-weighted images (DWI); contrast enhancement pattern on C-T1WI; the presence of intestinal obstruction; and apparent diffusion coefficient (ADC) values. All lymphomas (6 of 6) showed hypointense SI on C-T1WI, whereas adenocarcinomas (0 of 2) and inflammatory polyps (0 of 4) did not (p = 0.002). Lymphomas (5 of 6) showed homogeneous enhancement significantly more frequently than adenocarcinoma (0 of 2) and inflammatory polyps (0 of 4) (p = 0.02). Regarding SI of T1WI, T2WI, and qualitative DWI, and the presence of intestinal obstruction, no significant differences were observed among intestinal neoplasms. The ADC values of lymphomas (0.58 ± 0.18 × 10-3 mm2/s) were significantly lower than adenocarcinomas (1.27 ± 0.007 × 10-3 mm2/s, p = 0.01) and inflammatory polyps (1.56 ± 0.3 × 10-3 mm2/s, p = 0.0002). Lymphomas showed homogeneous low enhancement and significantly lower ADC values compared with adenocarcinomas and inflammatory polyps by MRI. These MRI findings may help differentiate intestinal lymphoma from other intestinal neoplasms.
