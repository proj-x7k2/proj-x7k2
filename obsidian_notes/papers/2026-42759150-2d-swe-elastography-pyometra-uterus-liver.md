# The effectiveness of uterine wall and liver elasticity and liver enzyme ratios in understanding the etiopathology of cystic endometrial hyperplasia-pyometra complex in dogs.

**Veterinary journal (London, England : 1997) (2026)** · 전향적 연구, n=49 · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42759150/) · DOI: 10.1016/j.tvjl.2026.106879
관련 주제: [[topics/urogenital-imaging]] · [[topics/abdominal-imaging]]

## 국문 요약
Diestrus기 건강한 개 15마리(대조군)와 cystic endometrial hyperplasia-pyometra(CEH-P) complex를 가진 개 34마리(연구군)를 대상으로 복부 초음파(B-mode, Doppler, 2D shear wave elastography(2D-SWE))를 시행해 자궁벽·간의 탄성도를 정량 비교했다. CEH-P군에서는 uterine artery waveform에서 저항 패턴 감소와 notch가 관찰되었고, 자궁벽·간 탄성도 모두 대조군보다 유의하게 높았으며(p<0.001) ROC 분석에서 높은 민감도·특이도를 보였다. ALT/ALP 비율은 CEH-P로 인한 간손상 검출에 높은 민감도를 보였다. 저자들은 2D-SWE가 CEH-P의 자궁벽 탄성 변화를 평가하는 비침습적 도구이며, 간 강직도 측정을 통해 CEH-P의 전신적(간) 영향을 평가하는 데에도 유용하다고 결론지었다.

## English summary
This prospective study compared 15 healthy diestrus control dogs with 34 dogs affected by cystic endometrial hyperplasia-pyometra (CEH-P) complex using B-mode, Doppler, and two-dimensional shear wave elastography (2D-SWE) ultrasonography. Reduced resistance patterns and notches in uterine artery waveforms were observed in the CEH-P group, and both uterine and liver elasticity values were significantly higher in CEH-P dogs than controls (p<0.001), with high sensitivity and specificity on ROC analysis. The ALT/ALP ratio showed high sensitivity for detecting CEH-P-associated liver injury. The authors conclude 2D-SWE is a non-invasive tool for evaluating uterine wall elasticity changes in CEH-P and for assessing the systemic (hepatic) effects of the disease via liver stiffness measurement.

## 임상 시사점
Pyometra가 의심되는 개에서 기존 B-mode·Doppler 초음파에 2D-SWE를 추가하면 자궁벽 강직도 변화를 정량적으로 파악할 수 있고, 동시에 간 탄성도·ALT/ALP 비율을 확인해 CEH-P로 인한 이차 간손상 여부를 함께 평가하는 데 참고할 수 있다.

## 연구 설계와 한계
단일 기관에서 건강한 diestrus견 15마리와 CEH-P 34마리를 비교한 전향적 대조군 연구다. 대조군·연구군 배정이 임상 검사에 근거한 것으로 무작위 배정은 아니며, 표본 크기(특히 대조군 15마리)가 크지 않아 ROC 분석의 cut-off 값을 임상 전반에 일반화하기 위해서는 추가 검증이 필요하다. 초록에는 구체적 elasticity 수치(kPa 등)나 ROC AUC 값이 제시되지 않았다.

## 원문 초록
This study aims to quantitatively evaluate the elasticity of the uterine wall and liver in dogs diagnosed with cystic endometrial hyperplasia-pyometra (CEH-P) complex and clinically healthy dogs in the luteal stage using two-dimensional shear wave elastography (2D-SWE), and to determine the relationship among the obtained elastographic parameters and clinical findings, hematological-biochemical data, Doppler ultrasonography findings expressing uterine hemodynamics, and possible prognostic indicators. The study included 49 intact female dogs, divided into two groups based on clinical examination: the control group (Group C; n = 15), consisting of healthy diestrus dogs, and the study group (Group P; n = 34), consisting of dogs with CEH-P complex. All bitches underwent abdominal ultrasonography (B-mod ultrasonography, Doppler ultrasonography, 2D-SWE). Reduced resistance pattern and notches in uterine artery waveforms were observed in Group P. Hepatic elasticity was also evaluated to assess the effects of CEH-P on the liver. Both uterine and liver elasticity values were significantly higher in Group P compared to Group C (p < 0.001), with high sensitivity and specificity values in ROC analysis. Hematology and 2D-SWE were evaluated together for accurate diagnosis of liver injury. The alanine aminotransferase/alkaline phosphatase (ALT/ALP) ratio showed high sensitivity in detecting liver injury caused by CEH-P complex in dogs. It was concluded that 2D-SWE is a non-invasive tool to evaluate elasticity changes in the uterine wall in cases of canine CEH-P. It is also useful for determining the systemic effects of CEH-P on the liver by measuring the liver stiffness.
