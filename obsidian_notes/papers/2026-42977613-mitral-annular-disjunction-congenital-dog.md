# Mitral annular disjunction associated with myxomatous mitral valve disease, infundibular pulmonic stenosis and ventricular septal defect in a dog: a case report.

**Journal of Veterinary Cardiology (2026)** · 증례보고, n=1 · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42977613/) · DOI: 10.1016/j.jvc.2026.08.012
관련 주제: [[topics/cardiac-imaging]]

## 국문 요약
빈번한 syncope를 보인 6세 수컷 Shih tzu에서 echocardiography로 mitral annular disjunction(MAD, 수축기에 mitral annulus가 left ventricle와 기능적으로 분리되어 atrium 쪽으로 이동하는 이상)이 MMVD, infundibular pulmonic stenosis, bidirectional shunting을 동반한 perimembranous ventricular septal defect와 함께 진단되었다. 혈역학적으로 Tetralogy of Fallot와 기능적으로 유사하다고 판단했다. 수술 대신 propranolol 보존 치료를 했고 초기 호전 후 점진적으로 악화되어 진단 후 총 생존 기간은 19개월이었다. 저자들은 선천성 기형과 동반된 MAD의 개 첫 보고라고 주장한다.

## English summary
A six-year-old male Shih tzu with frequent syncope was diagnosed by echocardiography with mitral annular disjunction associated with MMVD, infundibular pulmonic stenosis, and a perimembranous VSD with bidirectional shunting, a hemodynamic profile functionally analogous to Tetralogy of Fallot. Conservative therapy with propranolol was chosen over surgery; initial improvement was followed by progressive worsening, with a total survival of 19 months. The authors report it as the first MAD associated with congenital abnormalities in dogs.

## 임상 시사점
Syncope를 보이는 선천성 심기형 또는 MMVD 개에서 echocardiography 시 mitral annulus의 수축기 atrial 방향 이동(MAD)도 확인하는 습관을 들일 가치가 있다. 다만 단일 증례이므로 MAD가 예후나 치료 선택을 바꾼다고 말할 수는 없다.

## 연구 설계와 한계
증례 1예 서술로 MAD의 진단 기준이나 측정값, 치료 효과를 일반화할 수 없다. 초록에는 구체적 echocardiography 수치가 제시되지 않았다. Syncope와 MAD의 인과관계, 그리고 악화가 MAD 때문인지 동반 기형 때문인지는 구분되지 않는다.

## 원문 초록
Mitral annular disjunction is an abnormality of the mitral valve characterized by a displacement of the mitral annulus in which the annulus is functionally decoupled from the left ventricle and moves toward the atrium during systole. In human medicine, it is commonly associated with an increased risk of mitral valve prolapse, cardiac arrhythmias, and sudden cardiac death but rarely described in veterinary medicine. This article describes a case report of a six-year-old male Shih tzu dog with signs of frequent syncope episodes diagnosed by echocardiography with mitral annular disjunction associated with myxomatous mitral valve disease, infundibular pulmonic stenosis, and perimembranous ventricular septal defect with bidirectional shunting. Due to the patient's congenital heart defects, clinical signs, and laboratory results, the hemodynamic profile was determined to be functionally analogous to the Tetralogy of Fallot. Conservative drug therapy with β-blocker propranolol was chosen rather than a surgical approach. Short-term follow-up showed initial improvement of clinical signs, followed by progressive worsening. The total survival time was 19 months from the initial diagnosis and the conservative palliative therapy. To the authors' knowledge, this is the first report of mitral annular disjunction associated with congenital abnormalities in dogs.
