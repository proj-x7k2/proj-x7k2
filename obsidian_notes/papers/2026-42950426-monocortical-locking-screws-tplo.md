# Monocortical locking screws in the tibial diaphysis maintain tibial plateau angle and support osteotomy union after tibial plateau leveling osteotomy in dogs.

**Journal of the American Veterinary Medical Association (2026)** · 후향적 연구, n=117 · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42950426/) · DOI: 10.2460/javma.26.07.0543
관련 주제: [[topics/musculoskeletal-imaging]]

## 국문 요약
Cranial cruciate ligament insufficiency로 TPLO를 받고 distal tibial segment에 monocortical screw를 사용한 개 117마리의 방사선 추적을 후향적으로 검토했다. 술 후와 추적 방사선 사이 tibial plateau angle(TPA) 변화의 중앙값은 2.15°(95% CI 1.70-2.65°)였고 어떤 변수도 TPA 변화와 연관되지 않았다. 평균 43일 추적에서 modified RUST 중앙값은 10(범위 4-12)으로 방사선상 만족스러운 union이었으며, 전체 합병증률은 8.5%(감염 2.6%)였다.

## English summary
In a retrospective review of 117 dogs undergoing TPLO with monocortical distal tibial locking screws (titanium conical locking system), median change in tibial plateau angle during healing was 2.15° (95% CI 1.70-2.65°), with no variables associated with the change. Median mRUST was 10 at a mean follow-up of 43 days, and the overall complication rate was 8.5% (infection 2.6%).

## 임상 시사점
이 titanium conical locking 시스템에서는 monocortical distal screw로도 TPA가 5° 미만으로 유지되고 방사선상 union이 만족스러웠으므로, 술후 방사선에서 TPA 변화 2° 안팎은 예상 범위로 볼 수 있다. 다른 임플란트 시스템에는 적용할 수 없다.

## 연구 설계와 한계
단일 referral hospital의 후향적 연구로 bicortical 대조군이 없어 monocortical 고정의 상대적 우수성은 평가할 수 없다. 추적 기간이 짧고(평균 43일) 추적 방사선이 완비된 개만 포함되어 선택 편향 가능성이 있다.

## 원문 초록
OBJECTIVE: To evaluate the effect of distal monocortical locking screw fixation on construct stability and bone healing following tibial plateau leveling osteotomy (TPLO) in dogs. We hypothesized that monocortical fixation would result in acceptable maintenance of tibial plateau angle (TPA) of < 5° and satisfactory osteotomy union.

METHODS: Medical records from a companion animal referral hospital between December 8, 2021, and February 23, 2024, were retrospectively reviewed. Dogs were included if TPLO was performed for cranial cruciate ligament insufficiency using monocortical screws in the distal tibial segment and complete radiographic follow-up was available. Change in TPA between postoperative and follow-up radiographs were recorded. Bone healing was assessed using the modified Radiographic Union Scale for Tibial Fractures. Associations between outcomes and clinical variables were evaluated with linear regression models.

RESULTS: A total of 117 dogs met the inclusion criteria. Median change in TPA during healing was 2.15° (95% CI, 1.70° to 2.65°), and no evaluated variables were associated with TPA change. Median modified Radiographic Union Scale for Tibial Fractures score was 10 (range, 4 to 12), consistent with satisfactory radiographic union at a mean follow-up time of 43 days. The overall complication rate was 8.5%, including an infection rate of 2.6%.

CONCLUSIONS: Use of monocortical distal tibial screws within a titanium alloy conical locking TPLO system was associated with acceptable maintenance of TPA and satisfactory radiographic union scores.

CLINICAL RELEVANCE: Monocortical distal tibial fixation may provide sufficient stability to support osteotomy healing per manufacturer recommendations. Additional studies are needed to evaluate outcomes for different locking systems, patient size categories, screw combination, and longer-term follow-up periods.
