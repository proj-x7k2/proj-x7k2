# Evaluation of nanofibrillated cellulose as a urethral bulking agent in female dogs with acquired urethral sphincter mechanism incompetence: a prospective clinical study.

**Veterinary journal (London, England : 1997) (2026)** · 전향적 연구, n=8 · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42869270/) · DOI: 10.1016/j.tvjl.2026.106933
관련 주제: [[topics/urogenital-imaging]]

## 국문 요약
반복 시술이 자주 필요한 urethral bulking agent(UBA)의 대안으로 nanofibrillated cellulose(NFC)의 효과와 12개월 경과를 평가한 전향적 중재 임상 연구다. 중성화 암컷 개 8마리를 대상으로 MRI, retrograde vaginourethrography, cystoscopy, urodynamic testing으로 요실금의 다른 원인을 배제한 뒤 NFC를 implant했고, 1·12개월 또는 euthanasia·재발 시점에 MRI, retrograde vaginourethrography, urethroscopy로 추적했다. 1개월째 모든 개가 continent(CS≥8/10)였고 평균 continence score가 유의하게 증가했다(p<0.0001). 12개월째에도 치료 반응은 여전히 유의했으나(p=0.0010) 7마리 중 4마리는 추가 약물치료가 필요했다. 영상·urethroscopy 소견은 implant 부위의 flattening과 mucosal ulceration 동반 여부가 재발과 관련될 수 있음을 시사했다.

## English summary
This prospective interventional clinical study evaluated nanofibrillated cellulose (NFC), a novel biomaterial, as an alternative urethral bulking agent (UBA) given the frequent need for repeated implantations with existing agents. Eight neutered female dogs underwent MRI, retrograde vaginourethrography, cystoscopy, and urodynamic testing to exclude other causes of incontinence before NFC implantation, with follow-up MRI, vaginourethrography, and urethroscopy at 1 and 12 months or at euthanasia/recurrence. All dogs were continent (continence score ≥ 8/10) at 1 month with a significant increase in mean continence score (p < 0.0001). Treatment response remained significant at 12 months (p = 0.0010), though 4 of 7 dogs required adjunctive medical therapy. Imaging and urethroscopy findings suggested that flattening of the implant deposits, with or without mucosal ulceration, may contribute to recurrence.

## 임상 시사점
UBA(NFC) 시술 후 요실금이 재발한 개를 영상으로 추적할 때, implant 부위의 flattening이나 mucosal ulceration 소견은 재발의 구조적 원인을 시사할 수 있으므로 단순 continence score 추적만이 아니라 vaginourethrography·urethroscopy를 통한 implant 형태 평가를 함께 고려할 수 있다.

## 연구 설계와 한계
client-owned 중성화 암컷 개 8마리를 대상으로 한 단일군 전향적 중재 연구로 대조군(기존 UBA 또는 미치료군)이 없어 NFC의 효과를 다른 bulking agent와 직접 비교할 수 없다. 표본이 매우 작고(n=8, 12개월 추적 완료 7마리) 추적 기간도 12개월로 제한되어 장기 durability나 희귀 합병증 발생률은 평가하기 어렵다.

## 원문 초록
Urethral bulking agent (UBA) implantation is a minimally invasive, effective and safe alternative treatment for refractory urethral sphincter mechanism incompetence (USMI). However, repeated implantations are frequently needed to maintain continence. Therefore, a prospective interventional clinical study with eight client-owned neutered female dogs was designed to evaluate the clinical efficacy and 12-month outcome of nanofibrillated cellulose (NFC), a novel biomaterial as a UBA. Concurrent causes of urinary incontinence were excluded with clinical and neurological assessment, blood and urine sampling, magnetic resonance imaging (MRI), retrograde vaginourethrography, cystoscopy, and urodynamic testing. A continence score (CS) was assigned before and after treatment, and monthly thereafter. Furthermore, the dogs were followed with MRI, retrograde vaginouretrography, and urethroscopy at 1 and 12 months or at the time of euthanasia or recurrence. All dogs were continent (CS ≥ 8/10) at 1 month with a significant increase in mean CS (p < 0.0001, difference 5.4, 95% CI 4.1-6.6). Median duration of continence was 8 months (IQR 4 - not estimable). At 12 months, the treatment response was still significant (p = 0.0010) with (n=4/7) or without (n=3/7) medical intervention. The imaging and urethroscopy findings suggest that flattening of the deposits with or without mucosal ulceration may contribute to recurrence. Sporadic bacterial cystitis was diagnosed in two dogs and recurrent cystitis in one. In conclusion, NFC showed promising short-term effect and a low complication rate when used as a UBA. Urinary incontinence resolved after single NFC implantation in all dogs for a median of 8 months without adjunctive treatment.
