# Reference intervals and allometric scaling of echocardiographic measurements in small size growing dogs.

**Veterinary journal (London, England : 1997) (2026)** · 전향적 연구, n=13 · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42735743/) · DOI: 10.1016/j.tvjl.2026.106882
관련 주제: [[topics/cardiac-imaging]]

## 국문 요약
생후 2개월, 체중 1.3~2.55kg의 소형견 순종·잡종 강아지 13마리를 대상으로 생후 첫 1.5년(2개월 간격, 이후 18개월령) 동안 신체검사·체중·심초음파 검사를 반복 시행해 형태·기능 변화를 분석한 전향적 종단 연구다. 체중은 거의 모든 심초음파 형태 변수에 영향을 미쳤으나, 기능 변수 중에는 stroke volume(SV)만 영향을 받았다. 품종 효과는 어떤 변수에서도 나타나지 않았다. 기존에 보고된 대형·중형견 기반 allometric scaling 상수는 이 소형 성장기 강아지 집단의 심초음파 변수를 정확히 예측하지 못했으며, 저자들은 소형 성장기 강아지에 특화된 allometric equation(비례상수 a, scaling exponent b)을 새로 제시했다.

## English summary
This prospective longitudinal study followed 13 client-owned, healthy small-breed and cross-bred puppies (2 months old, 1.3-2.55 kg at enrollment) with physical examination, body weight, and echocardiographic evaluation every two months through the first year of life and again at 18 months. Body weight influenced nearly all echocardiographic morphological variables, while stroke volume (SV) was the only functional variable affected; no breed effect was found. Previously reported allometric scaling constants (derived from larger breeds) did not accurately predict echocardiographic variables in this small-breed growing population, prompting the authors to derive breed-size-specific allometric equations.

## 임상 시사점
소형 성장기 강아지의 심초음파를 판독할 때, 기존 대형·중형견 기반 allometric 참고치를 그대로 적용하면 정상을 이상으로 오판할 수 있으므로, 체중 기반 성장 단계를 고려한 소형견 특화 참고구간 사용이 권장된다.

## 연구 설계와 한계
단일 코호트(소형 순종·잡종 강아지 13마리)를 2개월령부터 18개월령까지 반복 추적한 전향적 종단 연구로, 표본 크기가 작고 단일 기관·소형견 위주 집단이라는 한계가 있다. 초록에는 구체적 allometric 상수(a, b) 값이나 참고구간의 수치가 제시되지 않았다.

## 원문 초록
In growing dogs, echocardiographic studies are scarce and carried out in large and medium breeds. In echocardiography, the most frequently used allometric reference ranges included adult dogs. Therefore, puppy reference values are required to identify normal and abnormal echocardiographic measurements. The aims of this study were 1) to analyse morphological and functional echocardiographic changes in small size dogs during the first 1.5 years of life; 2) to determine the effect of body weight (BW) and breed on these echocardiographic variables; 3) to compare echocardiographic reference intervals (RI) with allometric-scaled RI; 4) to estimate the proportionality constant (a) and the scaling exponent (b) in a specific allometric equation for these pure and cross-bred dogs. Thirteen, 2 months of age, 1.3-2.55 kg, client-owned, healthy puppies were included. The animals were evaluated every two months during the first year of life and then at the age of 18 months. At each time point, a physical examination, BW recording and echocardiographic evaluations were performed. BW had an effect on all echocardiographic morphological variables, while SV was the only functional variable modified. Conversely, no breed effect was shown in any echocardiographic variable. It was demonstrated that the previously reported constants were not accurate to predict echocardiographic variables in these small size growing dogs. Specific allometric equations for small size puppies were provided.
